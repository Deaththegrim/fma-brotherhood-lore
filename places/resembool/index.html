Resembool

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 2-10 · Danger: Low · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A quiet rural town in the south-east of Amestris on the banks of the Rain River, known for its sheep and the wool it sends away for army uniforms. Ishvalan fighters bombed the blocks around its station in 1907; the station was rebuilt, the town stayed small, and every spring it holds a great sheep festival. A stranger is noticed at once.

Areas

  • Resembool Station: The small station with its platform, its waiting bench and the timetable chalked on a board.
  • Sheep Lane: The lane that runs out past the pens to the hills.
  • Village Green: The square at the town's heart, with the well, the cart stand and the wool sheds.
