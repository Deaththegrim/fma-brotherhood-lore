Dune Sea

[Recommended Level: 16-30 · Danger: Severe]

The sea of dunes that fills the middle of the desert, shifting with every wind. Caravans navigate by the stars and by bones left in the sand, and the dunes swallow tracks within a day.

Areas

  • Crest Road: The ridge of the largest dune, where the caravans are most exposed.
  • Hollow Floor: The sheltered hollow between dunes, where the wind is quieter.
