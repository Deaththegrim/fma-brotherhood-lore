Gunja

[Recommended Level: 12-20 · Danger: High]

Gunja climbed a hillside in steps, each house roofed by the courtyard of the one above. The army burned it from the top down. The terraces are still there, and the stone hand mills still sit in the doorways where the women ground meal.

Areas

  • Gunja Mill Doorways: A lane of doorways with millstones left on the sills.
  • Gunja Stepped Courts: Courtyards that are also roofs, falling away down the slope.
