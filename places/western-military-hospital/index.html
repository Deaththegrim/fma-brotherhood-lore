Western Military Hospital

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 14-26 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The military hospital of West City, where the garrison's wounded from the border skirmishes are treated. Its wards fill after every patrol and empty only slowly.

Areas

  • Surgeon's Room: The small room of instruments and the surgeon's desk.
  • Ward B: The ward of iron beds where the patrol wounded are kept.
