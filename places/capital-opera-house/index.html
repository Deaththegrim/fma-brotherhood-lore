Capital Opera House

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 12-20 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

Society goes to the opera to be seen as much as to listen, and the capital's opera house is built for both: four tiers of boxes curving round the stage, so everyone can watch everyone else. The Führer keeps a box by the stage that is seldom used, and the box next to it is seldom empty.

Areas

  • Capital Opera Backstage Wings: The dark wings of ropes, flats and the dressing rooms behind them.
  • Capital Opera Horseshoe Auditorium: The gilded hall of red seats and stacked boxes under the chandelier.

People here

  • Josefa Lindt (Central City citizen)
