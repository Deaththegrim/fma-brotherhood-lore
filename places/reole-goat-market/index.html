Reole Goat Market

[Recommended Level: 10-13 · Danger: Moderate]

On the east edge of town the herders from the dry country bring goats, sheep and the odd camel to sell by the head. The pens are loud and the haggling is louder, and the coffee stall by the weighing post never closes.

Areas

  • Reole Goat Pens: Rows of wattle pens and the animals in them.
  • Reole Weighing Post Stall: The coffee stall beside the post where deals are sealed.

People here

  • Faris Sefir (Ishvalan civilian)
  • Yusuf Daramy (Reole townsperson)
