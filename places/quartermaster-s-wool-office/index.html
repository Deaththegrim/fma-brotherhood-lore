Quartermaster's Wool Office

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 3-7 · Danger: Low · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The army buys the town's wool for its uniforms through this office, where a quartermaster's clerk grades each farm's clip and signs the contracts. The whole town's year turns on what he writes.

Areas

  • Wool Office Contract Desk: A desk with carbon forms, an army stamp and a locked strongbox.
  • Wool Office Grading Table: A long table under a skylight where fleeces are spread and pulled apart.
