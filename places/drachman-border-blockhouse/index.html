Drachman Border Blockhouse

[Recommended Level: 14-20 · Danger: High]

A blockhouse of thick logs on the Drachman side of the border, its loopholes facing the fort. Its garrison of a dozen men changes every fortnight and sometimes waves to the Briggs patrols, and sometimes does not.

Areas

  • Blockhouse Loophole Room: The log-walled room behind the rifle loopholes, with a stove and a rack of rifles.
  • Blockhouse Striped Barrier: A striped pole across the track, with a sentry box beside it.

People here

  • Timofei Barsuk (Drachman soldier)
