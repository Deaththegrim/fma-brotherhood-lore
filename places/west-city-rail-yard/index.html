West City Rail Yard

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 14-20 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

Every soldier bound for the border passes through this yard east of the western headquarters, where troop carriages wait on the sidings beside coal wagons and flatcars of field guns. The western command keeps more rolling stock here than any other area, and it can move a regiment faster than the other commands can pack one.

Areas

  • Troop Sidings: Long lines of carriages where companies sleep the night before they leave.
  • Turntable Pit: The round pit where engines are turned, the turntable groaning under each one.
  • Yard Coaling Stage: The raised stage where engines take on coal, black dust over everything.
