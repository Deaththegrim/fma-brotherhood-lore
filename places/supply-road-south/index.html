Supply Road South

[Recommended Level: 8-18 · Danger: Moderate]

The supply road from South City to the front, a line of trucks and wagons that never stops. Fuel and ammunition go up it, and the wounded come back down it.

Areas

  • Convoy Lane: The lane of trucks and wagons, dusty and loud.
  • Fuel Dump: The dump of drums and sandbags beside the road.
