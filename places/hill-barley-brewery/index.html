Hill Barley Brewery

[Recommended Level: 14-16 · Danger: Moderate]

Barley from the western farms is malted and brewed here into the brown beer the garrison drinks. The brewer's drays roll out at dawn with barrels for every tavern in the city.

Areas

  • Copper Vat House: A hall of copper vats, warm and smelling of wet grain.
  • Dray Yard: The yard of drays and heavy horses.

People here

  • Barnaby Quist (Central City citizen)
