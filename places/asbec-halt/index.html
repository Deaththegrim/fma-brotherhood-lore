Asbec Halt

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 10-14 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

The railway halt at Asbec, a single platform and a waiting room where the stove is lit only when a train is due. The station master knows every face that gets off and remembers the ones that do not belong.

Areas

  • Asbec Halt Platform: A single timber platform beside the track, with a bell on a post and a water tower beyond.
  • Asbec Halt Waiting Room: A small room of benches, a stove and a timetable that is never right.

People here

  • Meinrad Imsel (Central City citizen)
