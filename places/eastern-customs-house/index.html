Eastern Customs House

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 16-22 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

Every load bound east or west is weighed and written down at this whitewashed office. The barrier pole across the road is painted in stripes, and the clerks inside work in shirtsleeves under a slow ceiling fan.

Areas

  • Barrier Pole: The striped pole across the road and the sentry who lifts it.
  • Weighing Room: The office where every load is weighed and entered.

People here

  • Augustin Raddeck (Amestrian sergeant)
  • Wei Shunde (Xingese merchant)
