Sheep Pastures

[Recommended Level: 2-10 · Danger: Low]

The grassy pastures that fill the land around Resembool, dotted with flocks and the shepherds' huts. The wool carries the town's name to the markets of the east.

Areas

  • Lower Meadow: The long meadow by the lane, grazed close and green.
  • North Field: The high field where the flock is kept in the summer, with its shelter and its wall.
