Briggs Gun Gallery

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 14-22 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The long gallery of gun positions on the Drachma face of the wall, its ports looking down a valley that the gunners have ranged to the yard. Nothing crosses that valley in daylight without the gallery knowing.

Areas

  • Gun Gallery Ammunition Hoist: The hoist shaft that brings shells up from the magazines in iron cradles.
  • Gun Gallery Ports: A row of deep ports in the wall, each with its field gun, its shell rack and its range card.

People here

  • Eino Askvik (Amestrian lieutenant)
