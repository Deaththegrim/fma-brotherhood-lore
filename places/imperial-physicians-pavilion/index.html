Imperial Physicians' Pavilion

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 20-26 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The court physicians work beside the Emperor's private rooms, from a pavilion with a herb store, a brazier kept lit day and night, and a guard who empties every basket on a cloth before it goes in. The Emperor is old and his health is said to be failing.

Areas

  • Physicians' Brazier Room: The warm room where decoctions simmer under a physician's eye.
  • Physicians' Herb Store: Shelves of labelled jars behind a lattice door, smelling of root and bark.

People here

  • Yan Shoude (Dragon's Pulse adept)
