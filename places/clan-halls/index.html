Clan Halls

[Recommended Level: 14-26 · Danger: High]

The halls of the great clans, each a walled compound of wooden buildings and painted doors. The clan heads hold court in their own halls, and the lesser families come to them for favours.

Areas

  • Ancestral Hall: The hall of the clan's ancestors, with their tablets and their incense.
  • Clan Courtyard: The pine-shaded courtyard where petitioners wait for the clan head.
