LeCoulte Cliff Road

[Recommended Level: 4-10 · Danger: Low]

The road up from Rush Valley into the mountains, a ledge cut into the rock with a long fall on one side and a plank bridge over a ravine near the top. Customers of the LeCoulte shop climb it with their limbs wrapped in oilcloth.

Areas

  • LeCoulte Cliff Ledge: A shelf of road barely a cart wide, the drop on the left.
  • LeCoulte Plank Bridge: Planks lashed over a ravine, a rope for a rail.

People here
