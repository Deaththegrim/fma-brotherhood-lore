Kauroy Dairy Farm

[Recommended Level: 2-6 · Danger: Low]

A dairy farm at the lake's western edge, where the cows wade into the shallows to cool off in the afternoon heat. Its cheese is sold at Curtis Meats and on the trains north.

Areas

  • Kauroy Cheese Room: A cool stone room of wheels of cheese on wooden shelves.
  • Kauroy Milking Shed: A shed of stalls, stools and pails, warm with cows.

People here

  • Hannes Tisdale (Central City citizen)
