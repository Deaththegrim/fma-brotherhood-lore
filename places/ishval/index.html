Ishval

[Recommended Level: 10-22 · Danger: High]

The south-eastern region of the Ishvalans, who follow Ishvala, a hard country of rock and sand on the edge of the great desert. It was razed in the civil war that began in 1901; its survivors were scattered across Amestris and the army holds the empty land. Amestrian soldiers are not welcome where any Ishvalan still stands.

Areas

  • Holy Ground: The shrine of Ishvala, open to the sky, where the faithful kneel.
  • Ishvalan Bazaar: The old bazaar square of baked-clay stalls, empty since the war; in the rebuilt years it fills again with spice and caravans.
  • Trade Road Gate: The road gate where the army checks everyone who comes in; in the rebuilt years the caravans from Xing pass here.

People here

  • Fessler (Ishval garrison brigadier)
  • Logue Lowe (Ishvalan monk)
