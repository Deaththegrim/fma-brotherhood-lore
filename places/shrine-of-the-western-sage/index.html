Shrine of the Western Sage

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 14-20 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

A modest shrine on a hill road, raised to the Philosopher of the West, the stranger who came out of the desert centuries ago and taught Xing the art that became alkahestry. Pilgrims leave little circles of folded paper on the altar.

Areas

  • Sage's Paper-Circle Altar: The low altar heaped with folded paper circles, some years old.
  • Western Sage Hill Road: The stepped path up to the shrine, lined with stones pilgrims have carved.

People here

  • Bian Shouren (Dragon's Pulse adept)
