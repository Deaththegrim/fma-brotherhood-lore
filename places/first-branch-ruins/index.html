First Branch Ruins

[Recommended Level: 12-22 · Danger: High]

The blackened shell of the National Central Library's First Branch, which went up in flames one night and took every volume inside with it. The library has roped off the gate and posted a notice; the cause of the fire is officially unknown.

Areas

  • First Branch Burnt Hall: The roofless reading hall, ash underfoot and iron shelf frames standing bare.
  • First Branch Cellar Stair: A stair down to the flooded cellar where a few books survived.
