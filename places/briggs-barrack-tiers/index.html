Briggs Barrack Tiers

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 10-16 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

Floor upon floor of soldiers' quarters inside the wall, the bunks four high and the boots drying on the heating pipes. The garrison eats together in a mess hall that smells of cabbage and gun oil.

Areas

  • Barrack Tiers Bunk Rooms: Long rooms of iron bunks four high, each with a footlocker and a hook for a rifle.
  • Barrack Tiers Mess Hall: A hall of long tables and benches, with a serving hatch and a samovar of tea.
