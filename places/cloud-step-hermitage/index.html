Cloud Step Hermitage

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 20-26 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

A stair of stone climbs into the cloud on the way up the high peaks, and at its top an old guard who left the court lives alone. Young warriors climb to ask him to teach them.

Areas

  • Cloud Step Stairway: Thousands of steps cut into the mountainside, slick with mist.
  • Hermit's Cold Room: A bare stone room with one mat and one bowl.

People here

  • Chang Suyin (Xingese martial artist)
  • Yuwen Tie (Xingese martial artist)
