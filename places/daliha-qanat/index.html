Daliha Qanat

[Recommended Level: 14-20 · Danger: High]

From the hills to Daliha, a row of shaft mouths dots the ground like the holes of a flute. Each drops to a tunnel where water from the hills runs downhill in the dark. Ishvalan diggers built it long before Amestris drew its borders, and it still flows.

Areas

  • Daliha Qanat Fallen Shaft: A collapsed shaft mouth choked with earth and stone.
  • Daliha Qanat Outflow Pool: The pool at Daliha's edge where the channel surfaces.
  • Daliha Qanat Tunnel: A low tunnel with a channel of running water down its middle.
