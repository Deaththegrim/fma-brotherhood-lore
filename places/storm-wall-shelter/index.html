Storm Wall Shelter

[Recommended Level: 18-26 · Danger: High]

A curved wall of stone built across the path of the storms. When the sky turns brown, caravans run for it, tie their animals to the iron rings and lie down on the lee side with cloths over their faces.

Areas

  • Lee Side: The sheltered side of the wall, crowded when the storms come.
  • Windward Drift: The heaped sand on the storm side, higher every season.

People here

  • Ulrich Penn (Former soldier drifter)
