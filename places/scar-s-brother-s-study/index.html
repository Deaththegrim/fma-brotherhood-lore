Scar's Brother's Study

[Recommended Level: 14-18 · Danger: Moderate]

A scholar's house in the ruins, where the brother of the man now called Scar studied alchemy and the eastern art of Xing from whatever books he could find. Most of the books burned. The diagrams he chalked on the wall did not.

Areas

  • Scar's Brother's Book Room: Shelves of blackened spines and loose pages.
  • Scar's Brother's Chalk Wall: A whitewashed wall covered with circles and notes in a careful hand.

People here
