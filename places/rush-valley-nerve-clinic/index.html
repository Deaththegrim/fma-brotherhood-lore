Rush Valley Nerve Clinic

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 2-6 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

A small clinic where patients lie up after the nerves are joined to a new port, the stage of an automail fitting that hurts the most and heals the slowest. Its doctor works with half the shops in town and trusts none of their timetables.

Areas

  • Rush Valley Nerve Ward: A ward of iron beds under ceiling fans, screens between them.
  • Rush Valley Port Surgery: A tiled room with a gas lamp over the table and the port tools laid in rows.

People here

  • Leopold Vantreck (Central City citizen)
