Requisition Hall

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 12-16 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

An old assembly hall that the northern command lends to agents sent up from the capital on special business. When it is in use, the hall fills with maps, runners and telephone wire; when it is not, it is cold and locked.

Areas

  • Requisition Map Boards: The long wall of boards where the north's roads, mines and villages are pinned out in pencil.
  • Requisition Runners' Bench: The bench by the door where the messengers wait with their caps in their hands.

People here

  • Irmgard Vinter (Central Command secretary)
