Fort Briggs

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 10-22 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

Amestris' first garrison against Drachma: a single immense wall stretched across the narrow valley where the mountains leave a gap, holding labs, a prison, gun positions, quarters, supply tunnels and one great tunnel down its length. Anyone passing between the two countries must go through it. To the people of Amestris the name means strength, and soldiers shiver at a posting here.

Areas

  • Commander's Keep: The command rooms high in the wall, their windows looking north at Drachma, lit all night.
  • Fort Gate: The great gate on the Amestrian face of the wall, with its sentries and the supply road from North City.
  • Parade Yard: The yard of packed snow inside the wall where the garrison drills before the wind rises.
