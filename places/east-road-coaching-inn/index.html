East Road Coaching Inn

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 3-8 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

Halfway on the coach road from East City, this inn changes the horses and feeds the passengers who could not get a seat on the train. Its stables are bigger than its dining room.

Areas

  • Coaching Inn Dining Room: A long table under the beams where passengers eat in their coats.
  • Coaching Inn Stable Yard: A cobbled yard with a pump, a stable block and a coach waiting on its shafts.

People here

  • Hubert Orme (East City shopkeeper)
