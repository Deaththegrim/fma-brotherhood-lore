Capital Grammar School

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 12-14 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A school of red brick where the children of clerks and soldiers learn their letters, sums and the history of Amestris. The headmistress is strict, the yard is loud, and every child knows the names of the State Alchemists by heart.

Areas

  • Capital Grammar Classroom: A room of wooden desks, inkwells and a large map of Amestris on the wall.
  • Capital Grammar Schoolyard: A walled yard of skipping ropes, marbles and shouting.
