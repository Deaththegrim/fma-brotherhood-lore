South City Cathedral

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 8-12 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The great church of the south, older than the barracks around it and the first thing a visitor is told to see. Its bells ring for weddings, for the hour and, three or four times a week now, for the funerals of men brought home from the line.

Areas

  • Bell Tower Stair: A narrow spiral up to the bells, with a view of the whole city from the top.
  • Cathedral Nave: A long cool hall of pale stone, candles and worn pews.
  • Cathedral Steps: The wide steps where guides wait for visitors and beggars wait for the guides.
