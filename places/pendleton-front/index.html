Pendleton Front

[Recommended Level: 22-26 · Danger: High]

West of Pendleton the hills are cut into trenches that the two armies have traded back and forth for years. Nothing grows there but wire. Patrols go out at dusk in fours and fives, the way the western army has learned to fight, and not all of them return by dawn.

Areas

  • Clay Trench Line: The forward trench, its sides propped with doors from Pendleton's houses.
  • Crater Field: The cratered ground between the lines, where the dead lie until a truce lets them be fetched.
  • Dugout Command: A dugout roofed with rail sleepers where a lieutenant keeps the field telephone.

People here

  • Aurel Lindqvist (Amestrian lieutenant)
