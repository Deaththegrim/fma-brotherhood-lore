Resembool Hill Cemetery

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 2-6 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

The town's burying ground on the slope above the lanes, open to the wind and kept by whoever happens to be passing with a scythe. Trisha Elric lies here under a plain stone, and the brothers come up the path to it whenever they are home, however short the visit.

Areas

  • Cemetery Lychgate: A little roofed gate where the coffins rest on their way in, with a bench under it.
  • Trisha Elric's Gravestone: A modest upright stone near the top of the slope, its lettering kept clean.

People here

  • Bertil Sennholz (Resembool farmer)
