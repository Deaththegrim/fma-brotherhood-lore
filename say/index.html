What to say

The exact lines that set off Amestris' systems: travel, alchemy, factions and atonement.

  • Take a train or coach: At a town's station. Name where you are going; the station clerk takes the fare in the scene. Anyone Barred keeps the whole party off the train.
  • Ask a faction for a job: At the faction's seat, sworn to it, asking an officer.
  • Swear an oath to a faction: In a scene, naming the faction, before someone with the standing to accept it.
  • Atone for killings: In the nave of Resembool Church, carrying infamy: it falls by 10, at most once every 30 turns.
  • Ask a teacher to take you on: With the teacher in the scene; the first lesson is a quest.
  • Train your art: Once you have studied alchemy or the pulse: practice between fights.
  • Use alchemy or alkahestry: An alchemist transmutes what is in the scene; an alkahestrist reads the pulse to heal or strike.
  • Draw a circle or set the kunai: A drawn circle (or a pentagram of kunai for alkahestry) steadies the next working; it fades after.
  • Gather material: Alchemy makes nothing from nothing: take what is really there in the scene.
  • Choose your specialism: Once you have studied alchemy: the field you make your own.
  • Design your own transmutation: Once you have studied alchemy: name it and say what it does; the narrator weighs what it costs.
  • Attempt human transmutation: The taboo. The Gate takes a toll every time: a limb, an eye, an organ, or worse.
