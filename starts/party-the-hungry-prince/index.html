(Party) The Hungry Prince

PARTY · the Promised Day · 2-5 players · Ling Yao, Lan Fan and Fu · guided
📍 Rush Valley, Amestris and its neighbours. The city just north of Dublith that grew fast as the centre of automail making, nicknamed the boomtown of the broken-down.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 2-10)

A young man collapsed from hunger in the middle of Rush Valley's main street, and the party fed him. Now he says he is the twelfth prince of Xing and that he will be travelling with them, and his bodyguards are not asking.
🎭 Brutus and Barker, Male, a pair of Rush Valley hustlers who run an arm-wrestling challenge in the street. (military uniform:-1.2). Two hustlers at an arm-wrestling table in the street, one with giant steel arms.
🎭 Fu, Male, Ling Yao's senior bodyguard and Lan Fan's grandfather. Spiky grey hair, grey shoulder armour. A small old man in black at a gatehouse, kunai already in his hand.
🎭 Lan Fan, Female, a young bodyguard of the Yao clan, Fu's granddaughter and Ling's personal guard. Dark eyes, light grey shoulder armour. A masked girl in black dropping from a roof between her prince and a stranger.
🎭 Ling Yao, Male, the twelfth prince of Xing. Narrow closed smiling eyes, goldenrod shirt with white trim. A smiling prince collapsing in the street from hunger and asking a stranger for lunch.
🎭 Gil Haddon, Male, a bookie who takes wagers on Forge Street in Rush Valley. Slicked-back black hair, a striped waistcoat. A bookie on a bench on Forge Street taking a wager from a mechanic.
🎭 Paninya, Female, the most famous thief in Rush Valley. Blue eyes. A girl on automail legs leaping across a rooftop with a stolen pocket watch.
