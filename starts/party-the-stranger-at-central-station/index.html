(Party) The Stranger at Central Station

PARTY · the Promised Day · 2-5 players · Van Hohenheim · guided
📍 Central City, Amestris and its neighbours. The capital of Amestris and the seat of its government: the legislature, the military's top command, the national libraries and laboratories and the great rail terminal all sit within its walls.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 12-30)

A tall, bearded old man with spectacles has been waiting on a bench at Central Station all morning, watching the trains come and go. He says he is the father of two boys who do not want to see him, and that he needs help with an errand.
🎭 Van Hohenheim, Male, the father of Edward and Alphonse. Golden eyes, white shirt and tie. A tall bearded man in spectacles on a station platform, not turning to look at the boys behind him.
