Ward Three

ERA · the Ishval war · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Eastern Military Hospital, Amestris and its neighbours. The military hospital of East City, where the garrison's wounded and sick are treated.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 8-16)

The trains back from Ishval are full of wounded men, and the eastern military hospital has run out of beds. A State Alchemist doctor from the front has come to help, and he will not say what he did out there.
🎭 Edda Marten, Female, a military nurse at the eastern hospital who runs Ward Three like a drill sergeant. Stern eyes, white apron over a blue military dress. A stern nurse standing between a sick bed and an officer at the ward door.
