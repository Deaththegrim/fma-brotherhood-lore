Ishval Rebuilt

ERA · after the Promised Day · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Ishval, Amestris and its neighbours. The south-eastern region of the Ishvalans, who follow Ishvala, a hard country of rock and sand on the edge of the great desert.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 10-22)

The new government has opened Ishval to its people again, and an officer who fought in the war has asked to be posted there to help rebuild it. The Ishvalans who have come home do not trust him, and they have every reason.
🎭 Roy Mustang, Male, Colonel Roy Mustang. Short messy black hair falling over eyes, blue military uniform. A colonel in white gloves leaning back in his chair, fingers poised to snap.
🎭 Scar, Male, a tall Ishvalan with a great cross-shaped scar across his forehead and face. Red eyes, open yellow jacket. A tall scarred Ishvalan in sunglasses in an alley, one tattooed hand already reaching.
🎭 Riza Hawkeye, Female, Riza Hawkeye. Light brown eyes, blue military uniform over black turtleneck. A blonde lieutenant at a gate, rifle on her shoulder, watching her colonel's back.
🎭 Tim Marcoh, Male, the village doctor of a nameless hamlet on the road between East City and Resembool. Dark tired eyes, white lab coat over collared shirt. An old village doctor in a white coat bent over a child, who stiffens when a soldier walks in.
