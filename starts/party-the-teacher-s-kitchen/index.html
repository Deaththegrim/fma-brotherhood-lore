(Party) The Teacher's Kitchen

PARTY · the Promised Day · 2-5 players · Edward and Alphonse · guided
📍 Curtis House, Amestris and its neighbours. The home of the Sig Curtis household in Dublith, a plain house with a herb garden and a kitchen where the bread is baked each morning.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 2-10)

The Elric brothers have come back to Dublith to face the teacher they disobeyed, confess what they tried, and ask what she knows of the Gate. She has not decided yet whether to hit them or hug them.
🎭 Alphonse Elric, Male, the younger Elric brother, fourteen, who has no body. (giant suit of plate armour:1.3). A giant suit of armour kneeling to offer a stray cat a hand.
🎭 Edward Elric, Male, the Fullmetal Alchemist. Golden eyes, red hooded coat with black Flamel cross on back. A short boy in a red coat clapping his hands together, the street rising into a wall in front of him.
🎭 Izumi Curtis, Female, a housewife of Dublith. Sharp dark eyes, long white sleeveless button coat-dress. A stern woman at a butcher's counter, cleaver in hand, staring down two boys who have come back.
