The Caravan From the East

HOOK · the brothers' journey · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Desert Crossing, Amestris and its neighbours. The last walled stop before the great desert: a caravan yard of mud-brick walls, wells and pens, where traders gather to cross east toward Xing or to come back west.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 16-30)

A caravan has come in across the Eastern Desert with silk, tea and passengers, and one of the passengers is a young man in Xingese clothes who keeps asking about ruins and immortality.
🎭 Mr. Han, Male, a Xingese smuggler with glasses and a goatee who specialises in moving people across. Short black hair, loose Xingese travelling coat. A bespectacled smuggler checking water skins in a caravan yard at dusk.
🎭 Old Ruo, Male, a caravan master of Xing who has crossed the great desert more times than he can count. An old caravan master leaning on a staff in a mud-walled yard full of camels.
