(Party) The Train Into East City

PARTY · the brothers' journey · 2-5 players · Roy Mustang and Riza Hawkeye · guided
📍 Eastern Command Headquarters, Amestris and its neighbours. The headquarters of the eastern military command, a squat block of offices with a guarded gate and a parade ground in front.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 10-18)

Gunmen have taken the night train from the west, and a general and his family are on board. The Flame Alchemist has been told to meet the train at East City and make it go away quietly.
🎭 Roy Mustang, Male, Colonel Roy Mustang. Short messy black hair falling over eyes, blue military uniform. A colonel in white gloves leaning back in his chair, fingers poised to snap.
🎭 Riza Hawkeye, Female, Riza Hawkeye. Light brown eyes, blue military uniform over black turtleneck. A blonde lieutenant at a gate, rifle on her shoulder, watching her colonel's back.
🎭 Halcrow, Male, Major General Halcrow. Blue eyes, blue general's military uniform with medals. A peevish general berating a colonel for taking too long to catch a killer.
🎭 Rebecca Catalina, Female, Second Lieutenant Rebecca Catalina of the eastern command. Black eyes, (military uniform:-1.2). A lieutenant loading a grenade launcher and complaining that there are no decent men in Central.
