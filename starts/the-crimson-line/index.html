The Crimson Line

ERA · the Ishval war · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Ishval War Ruins, Amestris and its neighbours. The ruined towns of the old Ishval, left as the war made them.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 10-22)

The State Alchemists have been given a district each, and the one the party serves under treats the war like a craft. Every house on Blackened Lane is to be emptied by morning.
🎭 Envy, Male, a slim, androgynous young man with long spiky black hair and a black crop top and skort. Violet eyes, (military uniform:-1.3). A grinning youth in black on a rooftop above the avenue, wearing a stranger's face for a moment.
🎭 Roy Mustang, Male, Colonel Roy Mustang. Short messy black hair falling over eyes, blue military uniform. A colonel in white gloves leaning back in his chair, fingers poised to snap.
🎭 Scar, Male, a tall Ishvalan with a great cross-shaped scar across his forehead and face. Red eyes, open yellow jacket. A tall scarred Ishvalan in sunglasses in an alley, one tattooed hand already reaching.
🎭 Solf J. Kimblee, Male, Major Solf J. Narrow golden eyes, white waistcoat. A smiling prisoner with long black hair pressing his tattooed palms together in a cell.
🎭 Alex Louis Armstrong, Male, Major Alex Louis Armstrong. Tight blue military uniform. A giant bald major with a blond moustache, weeping openly and flexing at the same time.
🎭 Giolio Comanche, Male, Major Giolio Comanche. Blue military uniform. A tiny old man on a peg leg grinning at a killer in the dark.
