Papers at the Eastern Gate

HOOK · the brothers' journey · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Eastern Gate Road, Amestris and its neighbours. The road that leaves East City for the east and the north, with a gatehouse where papers are checked and every cart is searched.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 8-18)

East City's gates are checking every traveller for a scarred man, and the queue at the eastern checkpoint stretches back down the road. Everyone in it has a reason to get through today.
