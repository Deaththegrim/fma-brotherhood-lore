(Party) The Road East

PARTY · after the Promised Day · 2-5 players · Alphonse, Jerso and Zampano · guided
📍 Oasis Palms, Amestris and its neighbours. A small oasis of date palms and a green pool, with clay huts for the families who keep the palms and sell the fruit to the caravans.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 16-30)

Alphonse Elric has his body back and has set out east across the desert to learn alkahestry in Xing. Two chimera soldiers who owe him their lives have come along to keep him out of trouble, and they are failing.
🎭 Jerso, Male, a soldier who looks quiet and dangerous on duty and turns out to be friendly. White eyes, (military uniform:-1.3). A stocky soldier with a deadpan face, then a sudden grin at his partner's expense.
🎭 Zampano, Male, a solid man in his late thirties. (military uniform:-1.3). A heavy-set man in an army coat lying smoothly to a sentry.
