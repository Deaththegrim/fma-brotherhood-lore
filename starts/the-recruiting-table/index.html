The Recruiting Table

HOOK · the Ishval war · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Central Command, Amestris and its neighbours. The top of the military government of Amestris: the head of state and his generals keep their offices in a moated compound on a pyramid-like structure at the heart of the capital.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 14-30)

Central City wants more soldiers for the east, and the recruiting table outside Central Command is offering pay, boots and a train ticket. The crowd around it is half volunteers and half people who were told to come.
🎭 Cremin, Male, Brigadier General Cremin of Central Command. Greying hair, blue military general's uniform with gold braid. A cold general ordering artillery onto a residential street.
🎭 Gardner, Male, Lieutenant General Gardner. Swept-back grey hair, narrow eyes, blue military general's uniform with braid. A grey-haired general leading a colleague down into a vault of motionless soldiers.
🎭 Gärtner, Male, Major General Gärtner of Central Command. Short grey hair, blue general's military uniform under black greatcoat with white fur collar. A bespectacled general reading out his orders to a hall of hostile Briggs soldiers.
