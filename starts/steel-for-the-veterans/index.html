Steel for the Veterans

HOOK · after the Promised Day · 1-5 players · guided
📍 LeCoulte Automail, Amestris and its neighbours. The automail shop of Ridel and Dominic LeCoulte, set in the mountains near Rush Valley, where the materials are plentiful and the shop has regular customers from Rush Valley.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 2-10)

The battle in the capital left a great many soldiers without arms or legs, and Rush Valley has more orders than it can fill. The workshops are hiring anyone who can hold a spanner.
🎭 Dominic LeCoulte, Male, a gruff, anti-social old automail engineer who lives in the mountains outside Rush Valley. Grey hair, dark green t-shirt. A scowling old mechanic slamming the door on a girl who wants to be his apprentice.
🎭 Ridel LeCoulte, Male, an automail engineer of Rush Valley. Short black hair, teal work jacket over a black shirt. An automail engineer at a fitting bench, glancing at his heavily pregnant wife.
🎭 Ridel's Son, Male, the infant son of Ridel and Satella LeCoulte. Tiny tuft of light brown hair. A newborn wrapped in a blanket, held up by a tearful grandfather.
🎭 Satella LeCoulte, Female, Ridel's wife. Green eyes, white top under pink apron straps. A heavily pregnant woman letting two wide-eyed boys feel her baby kick.
