Ferdinand Gaul

The sergeant in charge of the Gate Ward Guardhouse, where the military police check papers on the road out of the city. He has stood at the desk for nine years and has seen every forgery, every bribe and every sob story at least once.

Speaking Style: Short and heavy, each sentence a stamp coming down on a form.
Combat Role: a military policeman with a truncheon and revolver; he fights in doorways, uses his weight and cuffs before he talks
Relationship Hook: A heavy sergeant at a papers desk holding a passport up to the lamp, rubber stamp waiting.

Personality

  • Core: blunt and unshockable; never hurries a stamp; lets a mother with a child through first
  • Quirk: reads his private notebook every night
