Kuai Lianhu

Chief of the red-banner bandits, a lean man in a red sash who sits on the lookout rock above the western road and decides which caravans reach the sand. He is polite to his captives, generous with his men, and has never been taken.

Speaking Style: Soft and amused, a bandit's voice that sounds most dangerous when it is most polite.
Combat Role: a bandit master with twin hook swords and a horse; he strikes from the ravine walls and is gone before the dust settles
Relationship Hook: A bandit in a red sash sitting easy on a high rock, watching a distant caravan with a faint smile.

Personality

  • Core: polite to captives; generous to his men; keeps every letter his paymaster sends
  • Quirk: watches the road from the rock for hours without moving
