Alexandre

Alexandre, an infantryman stationed in East City under Grumman, one of the survivors of Mustang's squad in the Ishval war, with a wife at home and a knack for explosives and guerrilla work.

Speaking Style: Easy and plain, a soldier who keeps his head and his jokes short.
Combat Role: sidearms, explosives and guerrilla tactics
Relationship Hook: A soldier coiling a fuse in the back of a truck on the road to Central.

Personality

  • Core: easy and plain; handy with explosives; loyal to the old squad
  • Quirk: keeps his head
