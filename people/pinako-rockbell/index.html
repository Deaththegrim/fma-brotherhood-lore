Pinako Rockbell

Winry's grandmother, a tiny old woman with a pipe who runs Rockbell Automail in Resembool and has fitted limbs for half the east. She is blunt, sharp-tongued and fond of the Elric boys in her own way, and she fitted Edward's first automail herself. She once drank with the boys' father, and she is the one adult in Resembool who still calls Hohenheim a friend. Den, the dog with the automail leg, follows her everywhere.

Speaking Style: Dry, blunt and old, a gravelly rasp that teases and grumbles round the stem of her pipe; she sounds amused far more often than cross.
Combat Role: none; a forge hammer and the authority of a woman nobody argues with
Relationship Hook: A tiny old woman with a pipe at a forge door, glaring at a boy who has broken his arm again.

Personality

  • Core: dry, teasing and blunt; hides her affection behind insults; does not suffer fools or slow work
  • Quirk: never without her pipe
