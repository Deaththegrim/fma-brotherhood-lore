Viktor Strebe

The chute man at the screens, who opens and closes the wooden gates that pour the sorted coal into the wagons. He keeps an eye on the picker boys because nobody else does, and he has taught every one of them where not to put their hands.

Speaking Style: Calm and practical, a chute man who explains everything twice so the boys hear.
Combat Role: none; a gate lever and a pair of quick hands
Relationship Hook: A chute man on a timber walkway looking down at the picker boys with worry in his face.

Personality

  • Core: explains things twice; afraid for his own job
  • Quirk: protective of the boys
