Maike Dunstan

A shepherdess of eighteen at her first lambing on the ridge, who spends her nights in the fold among the ewes with a lantern, a bottle of warm milk for the weak lambs and a song for every one. Shy in the bothy among the men, she is fearless in the fold, and every orphan lamb on the ridge trails after her.

Speaking Style: Soft and unhurried, a young shepherdess who sings to the ewes and talks to people the same way.
Combat Role: none; a lantern swung at a face and a whistle for the dogs
Relationship Hook: A young shepherdess holding a lantern over a straw fold, a lamb's head poking out of her jacket.

Personality

  • Core: shy indoors, bold outdoors; sings to the ewes; hides what she has seen because she half pities it
  • Quirk: carries orphan lambs inside her coat
