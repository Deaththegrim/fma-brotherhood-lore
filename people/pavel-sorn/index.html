Pavel Sorn

The apprentice at Frostworks Automail, fourteen, who runs the cold test shed where limbs are left out overnight to see what freezes. He is clumsy everywhere except at a workbench, where his hands go quiet and certain.

Speaking Style: Breathless and squeaky, an apprentice whose voice has not quite finished breaking.
Combat Role: none; a screwdriver and the fastest legs in the workshop
Relationship Hook: An apprentice in a frosty shed fitting a tiny steel leg to a patient dog.

Personality

  • Core: clumsy and cheerful; goes quiet and certain at a bench; names every limb he works on
  • Quirk: adores his master
