Dov Sable

A pawnbroker on Market Row's Pawn Corner who buys almost anything and sells it back to the same people for more. He is quiet, thin and careful, and he asks no questions of anyone who is in a hurry. He keeps a locked back room full of pieces other people have pawned for their lives.

Speaking Style: Quiet and thin, a pawnbroker who speaks in low offers and never raises his price.
Combat Role: none; a locked back door and a shop knife
Relationship Hook: A pawnbroker behind a counter making a low offer for a dented pocket watch.

Personality

  • Core: quiet, thin and careful; never raises his price; asks no questions in a hurry
  • Quirk: keeps a locked room of other people's pieces
