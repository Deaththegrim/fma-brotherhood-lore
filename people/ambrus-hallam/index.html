Ambrus Hallam

The oldest of the net-menders in the lofts on the west shore, a tiny bald man whose needle never stops and whose memory the town relies on for anything older than the railway. He has mended nets for sixty years and fished for fifty before that, he says, though nobody's arithmetic allows it.

Speaking Style: Thin and twinkling, an old man who tells every story twice and changes the ending the second time.
Combat Role: none; a net thrown over a man's head, which he can still do
Relationship Hook: A tiny old man mending a net with flying fingers, asking a stranger to hold the money for a bet.

Personality

  • Core: never stops mending while he talks; remembers every boat and who drowned in it; bets on the underdog every time
  • Quirk: adds decades to his age
