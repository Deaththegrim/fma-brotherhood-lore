Fritz Amsel

An orphan of eleven with his dead father's forage cap pulled down over his ears, who has decided he is old enough to fight. He drills the younger children in the yard with a stick for a rifle and salutes everybody in uniform.

Speaking Style: High and fierce, a boy who announces everything as if to a parade.
Combat Role: a stick, a slingshot and a boy's fury
Relationship Hook: A skinny boy in an oversized army cap marching across a dirt yard with a stick on his shoulder.

Personality

  • Core: proud and furious; salutes every uniform; bosses the younger children and protects them too
  • Quirk: will not cry where anyone can see
