Stepan Barsuk

A Drachman sergeant who runs a field kitchen in the tent lines, a grey, round man who feeds a company out of one iron pot and keeps its spirits up with soup and gossip. He has been to the border four times in his service and come home every time.

Speaking Style: Hoarse and jovial, a field cook's voice roughened by smoke and gossip.
Combat Role: an old infantryman with a short rifle and a long ladle; he keeps to the back of an attack and comes home
Relationship Hook: A round Drachman sergeant stirring a steaming field pot and telling a joke to a line of cold soldiers.

Personality

  • Core: gossips to keep men cheerful; talks to his soup pot; shares out the last of anything
  • Quirk: never says the word war
