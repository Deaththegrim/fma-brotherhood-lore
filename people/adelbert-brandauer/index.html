Adelbert Brandauer

An army purchasing officer from South City, a tidy, sharp-nosed lieutenant who buys beef, hides and ice for the southern front by the trainload and lodges at the lake hotel on the army's account. He checks every brand against a list, every weight against a scale and every invoice twice, and Dublith dislikes him for all three.

Speaking Style: Crisp and courteous, a lieutenant whose politeness feels like an inspection.
Combat Role: an officer's sabre and service pistol, well drilled and never tested
Relationship Hook: A tidy lieutenant with a notebook standing by a cattle ramp, writing down a brand.

Personality

  • Core: polite in a way that feels like an inspection; eats alone and early; keeps his own copy of every paper
  • Quirk: checks everything twice
