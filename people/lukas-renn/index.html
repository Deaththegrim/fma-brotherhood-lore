Lukas Renn

A private of eighteen in the ready room, coat buttoned wrong, boots already laced, waiting for the duty telephone to ring. He joined to see the fort and was sent to sweep the city's streets instead, and he means to be noticed before the year is out.

Speaking Style: Quick and breathless, every sentence leaning forward as if it were already running for the door.
Combat Role: a green rifleman; he shoots straight on the range and too early in the street
Relationship Hook: A boy soldier half off the ready-room bench, staring at a silent telephone.

Personality

  • Core: eager, and ashamed of how eager he is; laughs too loudly at officers' jokes; first out of the door when the telephone rings
  • Quirk: keeps a pressed flower from the orphanage yard in his pay book
