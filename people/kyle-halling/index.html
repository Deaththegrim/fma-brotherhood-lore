Kyle Halling

The young son of Mr. Halling, a brash boy who runs errands at the mine and works in his father's tavern, steering rich travellers there as walking piggy banks. He is well loved in town, hates the army and its State Alchemists, and saves most of his hatred for Lieutenant Yoki.

Speaking Style: Brash and quick, a boy who talks back before he thinks and calls rich travellers piggy banks.
Combat Role: none; a runner who ducks through the crowds
Relationship Hook: A boy steering two travellers toward his father's tavern, grinning at what they will pay.

Personality

  • Core: brash and quick; hates Yoki; loyal to the town
  • Quirk: calls rich travellers piggy banks
