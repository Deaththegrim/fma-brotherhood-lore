Ling Yao

The twelfth prince of Xing, fifteen, heir of the Yao clan and one of the Emperor's many sons competing for the throne. He is cheerful, shameless about cadging a meal, and far sharper than his narrow, smiling eyes suggest. He means to cross the great desert to Amestris and bring home the secret of immortality, because the son who brings it will be named heir. Two bodyguards follow him everywhere.

Speaking Style: Bright, cheeky and easy, a smiling drawl that turns cold and steady when he is serious; he begs for food without shame.
Combat Role: a Xingese martial artist with a dao, fast and acrobatic, who senses chi and knows where a foe is before he moves
Relationship Hook: A smiling prince collapsing in the street from hunger and asking a stranger for lunch.

Personality

  • Core: far cleverer than he looks; carries his clan's weight without complaint; gives up anything but his people
  • Quirk: cheerful, shameless and hungry
