Reinhold Gast

The quarry master of the limestone workings, a huge, dusty man who has cut stone for the south's barracks, churches and memorials for twenty years. He handles blasting powder like bread dough and is proud that his face has never dropped a stone on the wrong man.

Speaking Style: A big, dust-choked voice built for shouting warnings down a quarry face.
Combat Role: a sledgehammer and a quarryman's strength; he can bring down a wall
Relationship Hook: A huge dust-covered quarry master with a sledgehammer at the foot of a pale limestone cliff.

Personality

  • Core: big and boastful; loyal to his quarrymen
  • Quirk: careful with powder, careless with money
