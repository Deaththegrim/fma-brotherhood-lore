Vato Falman

Vato Falman, warrant officer of Mustang's unit, a tall, thin officer with short grey hair and narrow eyes who remembers everything he has ever read: names, dates, addresses, regulations. He is formal to the point of comedy, inexperienced in a fight and invaluable at a desk. The colonel calls him a walking encyclopaedia.

Speaking Style: Overly formal and exact, a recitation of facts in a level voice that never quite knows when to stop.
Combat Role: a rifle held correctly and fired late; his weapon is memory
Relationship Hook: A tall thin officer reciting a suspect's address and birthday from memory.

Personality

  • Core: formal and exact; uneasy in a fight; loyal and quietly brave
  • Quirk: recites facts when nervous
