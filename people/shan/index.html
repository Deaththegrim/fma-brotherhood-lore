Shan

Elder Shan, an old Ishvalan priestess with an eye patch and a cane, who leads a small group of refugees living in the ruins of Xerxes in the eastern desert. Her people hate Amestrians, and she stops them from harming one. She wants only to live in peace.

Speaking Style: Gentle and dry, an elder who speaks slowly so the young listen.
Combat Role: none; a cane and an elder's word
Relationship Hook: An old woman with an eye patch raising her cane to stop young men from harming a stranger.

Personality

  • Core: patient and wise; forbids her people revenge; remembers the hospital's last night
  • Quirk: grateful to the doctors who saved her
