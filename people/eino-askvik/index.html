Eino Askvik

The lieutenant of the gun gallery, a long-legged, silent officer who walks the valley below the wall alone after every thaw to correct the range cards. The gunners say he can drop a shell on a sledge at three miles, and he has never told them otherwise.

Speaking Style: Very low and spare, speaking in distances and bearings, as if every sentence were a firing order.
Combat Role: an artillery officer and a fine rifle shot; he ranges first, fires once and moves before the reply
Relationship Hook: A tall lieutenant at a gun port with field glasses raised, correcting a range card with a pencil stub.

Personality

  • Core: corrects a range card with a stub of pencil kept behind his ear; walks with a long, soundless stride; speaks in distances; eats with the gunners, not the officers
  • Quirk: touches each gun's breech as he passes
