Josefine Arndt

The bandleader of the little brass band at Lantern Music Hall, who plays the cornet herself and conducts with it between solos. She has been leading the band since her husband's lungs gave out, and she has never missed a night.

Speaking Style: Lilting and warm, a bandleader who half-sings when she talks and counts time with her fingers.
Combat Role: none; a brass cornet swung like a club and a band that will stand up when she does
Relationship Hook: A bandleader with a cornet raised high, the brass band behind her, footlights glowing below.

Personality

  • Core: warm, brisk and musical; counts time in everything she does; plays louder when she is angry
  • Quirk: feeds the band's drummer, who is twelve
