Anders Brecht

The landlord of the Northern Inn in North City, a hearty man who runs the taproom and the rooms upstairs. He hears every rumour in the garrison's town and is a fair host to the soldiers and the travellers alike. He keeps the back room for people who do not want their names written down.

Speaking Style: Loud and hearty, an innkeeper who talks about his beer and his lodgers as if they were family.
Combat Role: none; a bar stool and a loud laugh
Relationship Hook: An innkeeper behind a bar pouring a tankard for a soldier and winking at a traveller.

Personality

  • Core: loud, hearty and fair; talks about beer and lodgers as family; hears every rumour
  • Quirk: keeps a back room for quiet guests
