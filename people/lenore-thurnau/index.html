Lenore Thurnau

A private of nineteen seconded to the colonel's staff as typist, filing clerk and runner, which mostly means she carries papers from Hawkeye's desk to the colonel's and back again. She types faster than anyone in the building and has learned that forms marked urgent can wait until the end of the week. Black Hayate sleeps under her chair.

Speaking Style: Bright and quick, a typist who says 'right' at the start of every sentence and means it as a promise.
Combat Role: none worth naming; basic rifle drill from her training and a typewriter she could probably throw
Relationship Hook: A young typist in uniform rattling at a typewriter while a small black-and-white dog snores at her feet.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful and tireless; thinks Lieutenant Havoc is wonderful and is embarrassed by it; keeps her desk tidier than the colonel's
  • Quirk: notices patterns and cannot leave one alone
