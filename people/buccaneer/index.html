Buccaneer

Captain Buccaneer of Fort Briggs, a bear of a man with a mohawk and a braided ponytail and an automail right arm that he changes for each job: a chainsaw with a crushing jaw or a hand of diamond-tipped claws. He strikes first and asks questions later, distrusts strangers and is loyal to Olivier to the death. He is the fort's loudest automail enthusiast.

Speaking Style: A bear's roar of a voice, rough and loud, that talks about prey and predators and laughs like a landslide.
Combat Role: cold-climate combat automail, a chainsaw arm or a clawed hand, and a bear's strength
Relationship Hook: A huge soldier with a mohawk revving a chainsaw arm in the snow.

Personality

  • Core: abrasive and suspicious of strangers; talks in animal metaphors; loyal to Olivier unto death
  • Quirk: proud of his automail
