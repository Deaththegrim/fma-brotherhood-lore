Gereon Sollmann

One of the two doctors of the Weber Street Free Clinic, a broad, untidy man of forty-five who resigned from the army hospital and took his instruments with him. He treats anyone who joins the queue, smokes on the back step between patients and has no patience for paperwork, officers or pity.

Speaking Style: Deep, dry and unimpressed, a doctor who makes jokes nobody laughs at while he stitches.
Combat Role: none by choice; a big surgeon with a steady hand, a scalpel and an army man's memory of a brawl
Relationship Hook: A heavy-set doctor stitching a man's arm by lamplight, a cigarette unlit between his lips.

Personality

  • Core: blunt, tired and kind underneath; smokes when he should sleep; hates questions about the war
  • Quirk: never refuses a patient
