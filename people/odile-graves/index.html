Odile Graves

A farm girl of nineteen from south of the town who taught herself alchemy from a primer sent by post and wants, more than anything, to sit the State Alchemist examination. She is small, wiry and fearless in water. She talks about the brothers the way other girls talk about actors, and she knows to the month how old Edward Elric was when he passed.

Speaking Style: Bright and rushing, a young alchemist who explains a theory faster than anyone can follow.
Combat Role: none; she would turn the ground under a foe to mud and run
Relationship Hook: A wiry young woman surfacing in a green quarry pool, holding up a lantern in a jar.

Personality

  • Core: lectures people who did not ask; fearless in cold water; idolises the Fullmetal Alchemist
  • Quirk: bright and reckless
