Emeric Rautenberg

The bandmaster of the garrison band, a round, cheerful sergeant with a waxed moustache who conducts the rest-day concerts in the park and drills his musicians as hard as any infantry company. He loves the old eastern marches best of all and plays them with tears in his eyes.

Speaking Style: Hearty and musical, a bandmaster who taps out the beat of whatever he is saying.
Combat Role: none worth the name; a baton and a band who would follow him anywhere
Relationship Hook: A round bandmaster with a waxed moustache raising his baton in a green iron bandstand.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful, emotional and proud; taps the beat with his foot
  • Quirk: cries at marches
