Hubert Waage

The weighman, who sits in the hut by the spur and reads the beam as each coal wagon rolls on the scale. He writes the figure in two books, one bound in red and one in black, and has done so for eleven years without a blot.

Speaking Style: Measured and heavy, a weighman who speaks as if reading figures off the beam.
Combat Role: none; a stout door and a scale beam he can jam
Relationship Hook: A heavy weighman in a hut, one hand on a red book and one on a black, as an inspector knocks.

Personality

  • Core: methodical and silent; has never blotted a page; afraid of losing the hut
  • Quirk: lies only in numbers
