Aziel Rahhad

The eldest man in the slum by the outfall, a former potter from the eastern towns of Ishval who walked to the city with nothing but a clay lamp and the clothes he fled in. He sits under the open shelter in the evenings, settles quarrels over water, wood and children, and keeps the slum's one lamp burning so that nobody ever has to ask where to find him. He prays to Ishvala at dawn without hurry and expects the young ones to do the same.

Speaking Style: Low and unhurried, an elder who lets a silence sit until the other man fills it.
Combat Role: none; an elder's staff and the obedience of a few hundred people who would stand in front of him
Relationship Hook: An old Ishvalan with a white beard sitting under a lamp in a crate-wood shelter, waiting for the next quarrel.

Personality

  • Core: speaks of Ishvala as of an old friend; lets the young talk themselves out before he answers; never says the word Amestrian with a hard edge, though he could
  • Quirk: patient and slow to judge
