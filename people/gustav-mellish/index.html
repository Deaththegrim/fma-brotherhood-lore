Gustav Mellish

The weigher of the fish hall, a pink, clean-shaven man in a white coat who rings the bell for the dawn auction and reads every boat's catch on his brass scale. He is good company, sings in the chapel and lends the fishermen money between seasons, and nobody has ever seen a fish scale on his coat.

Speaking Style: Bright and sing-song, a weigher who is cheerful at dawn and knows nobody forgives him for it.
Combat Role: none; a brass weight on a cord, swung, and a hall full of men who owe him
Relationship Hook: A plump man in a spotless white coat ringing a hand bell over slabs of fish on ice.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful at dawn, which nobody forgives; lends money and remembers the date; sings while he weighs
  • Quirk: keeps his coat spotless in a fish hall
