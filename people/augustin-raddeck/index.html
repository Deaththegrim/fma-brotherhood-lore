Augustin Raddeck

The senior clerk of the customs house, a pale sergeant in shirtsleeves who has sat under the slow ceiling fan for eleven years. He weighs every load and enters it in a neat hand, and he has never once raised his voice.

Speaking Style: Soft and dry, a clerk who sounds bored and misses nothing.
Combat Role: a desk sergeant with a service pistol in the drawer; he talks first and shoots from behind the counter
Relationship Hook: A pale clerk in sleeve garters writing in a book under a slow ceiling fan.

Personality

  • Core: bored-sounding and sharp; never raises his voice; keeps two books
  • Quirk: fond of his ceiling fan
