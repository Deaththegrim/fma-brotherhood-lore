Tarek Osman

An Ishvalan father walking the old exiles' track north to the ruins with his wife and two children, years after the war, having hidden in the southern hills until the hills got too dangerous. He reads the names on the cairns aloud as he passes.

Speaking Style: Gentle and exhausted; a father who keeps his voice low so his children do not hear him fear.
Combat Role: none; a father who stands between his family and anything
Relationship Hook: A weary Ishvalan father with a child on his back resting by a cairn with names scratched into it.

Personality

  • Core: gentle and exhausted; reads every cairn aloud; shields his children
  • Quirk: will not run and leave the weak behind
