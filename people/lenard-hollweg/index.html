Lenard Hollweg

A young corporal discharged from the eastern garrison with his right arm gone below the elbow and the sleeve pinned across his chest. He came off the evening train with a kitbag, his discharge papers and a scrap of paper with one name on it, and he has been asking the taproom whether anybody in this town truly builds limbs. He is polite, careful and very tired.

Speaking Style: Polite and careful, a young ex-soldier who keeps his voice as level as his pinned sleeve.
Combat Role: a soldier's left-handed pistol shooting, slow and steady
Relationship Hook: A young ex-soldier with a pinned sleeve smoothing out a scrap of paper on his knee.

Personality

  • Core: polite to a fault; counts his money every night; hopes too hard and is ashamed of it
  • Quirk: hides the empty sleeve when children stare
