Ansgar Wilk

A road-mender who lives in a stone lean-to above the plank bridge and keeps the cliff road passable: shovelling scree after rain, re-lashing planks, driving new pegs for the rope rail. The Rush Valley shops pay him a little, Dominic LeCoulte pays him a little more, and he has not been down to the town in a year.

Speaking Style: One word where two would do, a mountain man whose silences say the rest.
Combat Role: a mountain man's footing; he fights on the ledge with a long-handled shovel and lets the drop do the rest
Relationship Hook: A bearded road-mender kneeling on a plank bridge over a ravine, lashing a board while the wind pulls at him.

Personality

  • Core: answers in one word; trusts rope more than men; shares his fire with anyone who climbs this far
  • Quirk: tests every plank with his heel
