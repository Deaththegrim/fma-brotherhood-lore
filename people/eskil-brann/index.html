Eskil Brann

The weather observer at the Range Weather Station, a corporal who reads the barometer, the wind and the snow three times a day and wires them to the fort and to headquarters. He lives alone in the hut and likes it.

Speaking Style: Slow and wry, a weatherman who forecasts doom with a little smile.
Combat Role: a mountain scout with a carbine; he knows the ridge and the snow and fights from both
Relationship Hook: A wry observer in a fur hat writing in a notebook beside a white instrument box on a snowy rise.

Personality

  • Core: wry and solitary; trusts the barometer more than officers; talks to the weather
  • Quirk: loyal to the fort
