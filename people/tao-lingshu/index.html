Tao Lingshu

A senior student who runs the infirmary room at the side of the hall, where the poor of the capital come to be treated by students for nothing. He is earnest, thin from skipped meals and better with frightened patients than with examiners.

Speaking Style: Soft and kind, a voice that tells a patient the truth slowly.
Combat Role: a student alkahestrist; a circle drawn fast and a patient's attacker sent stumbling, and then he panics
Relationship Hook: A thin student bending over a cot in a lamplit room, a bandage half wound round a stranger's arm.

Personality

  • Core: earnest to the point of foolishness; forgets to eat when the room is full; talks a frightened patient into stillness
  • Quirk: freezes in front of an examiner
