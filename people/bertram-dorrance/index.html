Bertram Dorrance

The eldest of four brothers who work three adits on the mountainside, a slab-shouldered miner with grey dust ground into every line of his face. The family sells iron and a little nickel to the foundry below, and his sons, nephews and two daughters-in-law all push the carts. He trusts a timber prop more than a contract, and both more than a stranger.

Speaking Style: Gravel and dust, a miner who spits before he answers and means every word he finally says.
Combat Role: a miner's brawl; a short pick held close to the head and the strength of thirty years at the face
Relationship Hook: A dust-grey miner standing at a timbered tunnel mouth, counting his sons as they come out.

Personality

  • Core: spits before he answers a question; will not go into the dark without a candle lit; drives a hard bargain with the foundry and pays his own fairly
  • Quirk: counts his family at every change of shift
