Wiebke Kohler

The matriarch of the charcoal-burning family in the pinewood clearing, black to the elbows, with a pipe in her teeth and a mound that has not gone out in nine years. Her sons cut the wood, her daughters stack it, and she decides everything.

Speaking Style: Rough and sing-song, an old burner who mutters charms over her mound and curses in the same breath.
Combat Role: a charcoal rake and a family that comes running
Relationship Hook: An old charcoal burner with a clay pipe watching a soldier come up the track through the smoke.

Personality

  • Core: shrewd and superstitious; trusts coin more than kin; fierce for her family
  • Quirk: smokes a pipe constantly
