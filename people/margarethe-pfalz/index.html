Margarethe Pfalz

The senior operator of the city telephone exchange, twenty years at the board and the fastest hands in the north. She runs the room of girls with a look and a cough, and every officer at headquarters is a little afraid of her without knowing why.

Speaking Style: Silken and amused, a voice built to say 'connecting you now' and to mean something else by it.
Combat Role: none; a sharp hatpin and the ability to cut a man's line at the worst moment
Relationship Hook: A senior operator at a wall of cords, headset on, one jack held still in mid-air as she listens.

Personality

  • Core: unhurried, amused and very observant; remembers every voice on the board; hands out favours like sweets
  • Quirk: pretends not to understand questions she dislikes
