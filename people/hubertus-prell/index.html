Hubertus Prell

The night sergeant of the stockade, heavy, slow and bored, who walks the corridor of iron doors with a ring of keys and a cup of coffee and opens each eye slot on the hour. He has guarded deserters, drunks and rebels for eleven years and treats them all the same: badly fed, rarely beaten, never spoken to more than needs be.

Speaking Style: A tired rasp, a jailer who sighs before every answer as if the question had cost him something.
Combat Role: a heavy man with a truncheon and a key-ring the size of a fist; he blocks a corridor and waits for help
Relationship Hook: A heavy jailer holding a tin cup in a dim corridor, one hand on an eye slot in an iron door.

Personality

  • Core: slow, tired and hard to surprise; drinks coffee cold and complains about it; keeps his word to prisoners more than to officers
  • Quirk: counts his keys every time he sits down
