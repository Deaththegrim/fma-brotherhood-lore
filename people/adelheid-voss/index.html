Adelheid Voss

The landlady of the Pickaxe Inn, the one house in Youswell with sheets that are white and a bath that fills. She came from East City as a lady's maid, married the inn and buried its owner, and she has kept the place genteel by sheer will. Her parlour clock runs slow so that her guests miss the morning train and stay another night.

Speaking Style: Silk over gravel, a landlady who calls every guest 'dear' and means 'customer'.
Combat Role: none; a hatpin and a bell that brings the porter
Relationship Hook: A landlady in a cameo brooch pouring tea for a guest while reading the label on his trunk.

Personality

  • Core: gracious to the face; notices luggage, boots and hands; plays the lieutenant and his clerk against each other
  • Quirk: keeps the parlour clock slow on purpose
