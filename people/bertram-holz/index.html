Bertram Holz

The sergeant of the guardhouse, a tired soldier near the end of his twenty years who wants to go home to a farm near East City and nothing else. He has kept the peace in Youswell by not looking at things, and he is very good at it.

Speaking Style: Slow and sparing, a sergeant who answers a question with a shrug and a grunt.
Combat Role: a sergeant's bayonet drill, slow but sure
Relationship Hook: A grey sergeant at a guardhouse table, sliding an order under a pile of playing cards.

Personality

  • Core: tired and decent; plays cards badly on purpose; counts the days to his pension
  • Quirk: loses orders he dislikes
