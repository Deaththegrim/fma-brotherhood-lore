Wigand Hoss

The jailer of the stockade, an old sergeant who has kept the keys of the iron corridor longer than most of his prisoners have been alive. He warms his hands at the brazier, reads the same three books in turn and knows each prisoner's breakfast.

Speaking Style: Slow and grandfatherly, the voice of a man who has said goodnight to every kind of prisoner.
Combat Role: an old jailer's economy; a ring of keys swung like a flail, a shoulder into a door, and a truncheon kept for wrists
Relationship Hook: An old jailer warming his hands over a brazier, a ring of keys the size of a dinner plate at his hip.

Personality

  • Core: speaks to prisoners as lodgers rather than criminals; keeps a draughts board on the brazier chair; rereads the same three books in turn
  • Quirk: never hurries a key in a lock
