Wiebke Brahm

A lance corporal who leads the wiring party in the fields before Fotset, a small, hard, silent woman who goes out every night with cutters and coils to mend the belt the shells tore up by day. She has been out more nights than anyone alive on that stretch of line.

Speaking Style: Flat and whispered, the voice of someone who has spent two years talking only in no man's land.
Combat Role: wire cutters, a revolver and a knife; she fights flat in the mud and close
Relationship Hook: A small mud-caked soldier crouching among coils of barbed wire at night with cutters in her hand.

Personality

  • Core: silent and hard; counts nights, not days
  • Quirk: trusts the enemy's wiring party more than her own officers
