Van Hohenheim

The father of Edward and Alphonse, a wandering alchemist who walked out of Resembool when the boys were small and did not come back. He is tall and broad, with a golden ponytail, a beard and spectacles, polite to strangers and hopeless with family. Ed hates him for leaving and for missing their mother's funeral. He travels the country alone, studying the land, and he does not explain what he is looking for.

Speaking Style: Deep, tired and wry, with long pauses and an awkward laugh; he sounds like a man who has heard every argument before and still does not know how to talk to his sons.
Combat Role: alchemy without a circle at a scale few have seen, raised walls and spikes of earth, never in a hurry
Relationship Hook: A tall bearded man in spectacles on a station platform, not turning to look at the boys behind him.

Personality

  • Core: polite, awkward and kind to strangers; laughs at the wrong moments; carries a family portrait he never shows
  • Quirk: cannot find the words with his sons
