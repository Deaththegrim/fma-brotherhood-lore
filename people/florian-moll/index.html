Florian Moll

The caretaker of Stag Ridge Lodge, an old man who lives in a room off the kitchen and keeps the lodge's stag heads dusted for the generals' shooting parties. He opens the lodge with a great deal of fuss whenever someone important comes north.

Speaking Style: Creaking and servile, an old caretaker who agrees with everyone and remembers everything.
Combat Role: none; a hunting lodge full of guns he knows how to clean but not to fire
Relationship Hook: An old caretaker dusting a stag head in a grand trophy hall.

Personality

  • Core: servile and observant; dusts the stag heads daily; afraid of the generals and loyal to none
  • Quirk: remembers every word said at dinner
