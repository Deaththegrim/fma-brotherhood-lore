Rosa Kellner

The head of the oldest vine-growing family on the terraces, a widow of sixty who runs the press house with her three daughters and a nephew too lame for the army. She is broad, red-faced and loud, and she knows the hill paths better than any patrol.

Speaking Style: Rough, warm and teasing, a vine-grower who laughs from the belly and swears at the weather.
Combat Role: a pruning hook and a strong arm; she has seen off a drunk patrol with both
Relationship Hook: A broad old vine-grower in a stained apron laughing between rows of vines on a hillside terrace.

Personality

  • Core: loud and teasing; swears at the weather like a sailor; fiercely protective of her daughters
  • Quirk: thinks borders are for people without mules
