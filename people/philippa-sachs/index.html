Philippa Sachs

The landlady of the officers' billet, a widow of a cavalry captain who has rented her house to the army for twenty years. She meets every new arrival in the letter hall, looks at his luggage, and decides on the spot how long he will stay.

Speaking Style: Crisp and amused, an old landlady who has heard every excuse an officer can make and enjoys the new ones.
Combat Role: none; a heavy brass bell and a voice that can shame a colonel
Relationship Hook: An old landlady in black weighing up a young officer's single battered trunk.

Personality

  • Core: sharp-eyed and entertained by everyone; rings her bell for no reason, to see who comes; judges a man by his luggage
  • Quirk: softens at once for a homesick boy
