Clemens Varga

The head surgeon of the North Area Military Hospital, a major with steady hands who has taken off more frostbitten fingers and toes than any man in Amestris. He is gentle with patients and curt with officers, and he sleeps in his office more nights than not.

Speaking Style: Steady and kind and very tired, a surgeon who explains bad news slowly so you hear it once.
Combat Role: none; a surgeon's steady hands, a scalpel and the authority to throw a colonel off his ward
Relationship Hook: A tired surgeon in spectacles bending over a frostbitten hand on a long ward of iron beds.

Personality

  • Core: gentle with patients, curt with officers; never sits down on a ward; keeps a true record in secret
  • Quirk: drinks his coffee cold because he forgets it
