Aurelius Pfahler

The editor of the city's daily paper, a stooped man with ink in the creases of his hands and a blue pencil always behind his ear. He prints what the garrison permits, writes the city's best obituaries and hides small truths in the advertisements and the weather column for readers clever enough to look. He has been warned twice and has never been closed.

Speaking Style: Measured and ironic, an editor who speaks as though every sentence will be printed and cut.
Combat Role: none; a stick to lean on, a printing press and the threat of a paragraph
Relationship Hook: A stooped editor in half-moon spectacles striking a line from a proof with a blue pencil, the press thundering below.

Personality

  • Core: wry, tired and stubborn; loves the city and distrusts every government it has had; never throws a page away
  • Quirk: edits other people's speech while they are still talking
