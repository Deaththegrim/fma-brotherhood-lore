Eckhart Varnhagen

A wool dealer from East City who comes out on the morning train every market day in a grey coat with a fur collar, carrying a notebook bound in red leather. He is pleasant, well dressed and free with his cigars, and he bids freely too. The farmers like him, because he makes the bidding lively.

Speaking Style: Smooth and unhurried, a city dealer who compliments a fleece while he undercuts it.
Combat Role: none; a sword cane he carries for show and a hired man on the train
Relationship Hook: A sleek city dealer raising one gloved hand at a sheep auction, smiling at nobody.

Personality

  • Core: pleasant and generous; writes in his red notebook mid-conversation; flatters before he bargains
  • Quirk: never takes off his gloves
