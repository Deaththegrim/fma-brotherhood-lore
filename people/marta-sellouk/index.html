Marta Sellouk

The owner of the Sunwheel Inn, a widow in her fifties with flour on her apron and keys on her belt. In the pilgrim years she fed three hundred guests on a feast day; now she feeds soldiers, salesmen and families with no roof, and takes payment from those who have it.

Speaking Style: Brisk, warm and loud enough to carry across a courtyard; she never finishes a sentence without an order in it.
Combat Role: none; a rolling pin and a voice that clears a courtyard
Relationship Hook: A stout innkeeper laying a long table under a fig tree, keys jangling at her hip.

Personality

  • Core: generous and sharp-eyed; feeds anyone who sits at her long table; never says aloud who sleeps upstairs
  • Quirk: keeps every key on her own belt
