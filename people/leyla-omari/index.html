Leyla Omari

The refugee band's midwife and healer, a round-faced woman in her thirties who sits at Shan's right hand under the awning and keeps the camp's few medicines in a leather case. She has delivered every child born in the ruins.

Speaking Style: Warm and brisk, a midwife's voice that soothes and gives orders in the same breath.
Combat Role: none; she stays with the hurt and lets others fight
Relationship Hook: A round-faced Ishvalan woman opening a leather medicine case under a canvas awning.

Personality

  • Core: brisk and kind; loyal to Shan without bowing to her; keeps count of every birth and death
  • Quirk: scolds anyone who wastes water
