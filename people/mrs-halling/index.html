Mrs. Halling

The wife of the town's spokesman and the cook behind the tavern's one good dish, a mutton stew that has kept half of Miners' Row alive through two bad winters. She runs the kitchen, the upstairs rooms and her husband's temper, and she is the reason Kyle Halling washes his hands before he eats.

Speaking Style: Brisk and low, a cook who talks to the stewpot as much as to the customers.
Combat Role: none; a ladle and a kitchen full of heavy pans
Relationship Hook: A cook in a hot back kitchen ladling stew while she listens to the argument in the taproom through the hatch.

Personality

  • Core: calm in a crisis, sharp after it; feeds a stranger before asking his business; scolds Kyle in the same breath she praises him
  • Quirk: keeps her own counsel about money
