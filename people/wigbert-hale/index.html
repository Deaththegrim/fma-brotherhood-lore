Wigbert Hale

An old shepherd who has run a flock on the downs for fifty years and still walks the skyline every day with a crook taller than himself. He knows every dew pond, every hawk's nest and every wind that brings rain, and speaks in weather sayings he mostly contradicts. Half the town's children have been sent up to him for a summer to learn the work.

Speaking Style: Wandering and wry, an old shepherd who talks in sayings and then contradicts them.
Combat Role: an old shepherd's crook and a sling he has used on foxes for fifty years
Relationship Hook: An old shepherd leaning on a tall crook beside a dew pond, squinting at the weather.

Personality

  • Core: wry and wandering; never hurries a flock or a story; teaches any child who climbs the hill
  • Quirk: speaks in weather sayings
