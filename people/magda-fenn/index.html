Magda Fenn

A salt raker with cracked hands and squinting, reddened eyes, who scrapes the brine pans into cones from dawn until the glare is too much. She came out from Youswell with her brothers, and she is the one the rakers listen to.

Speaking Style: Hoarse and fierce; her voice carries across the pans and does not waver.
Combat Role: a raker with a long salt rake and the whole crew at her back
Relationship Hook: A wiry woman in a salt-crusted hat leaning on a long rake among blinding white pans.

Personality

  • Core: fierce and fair; keeps the sums; squints at everything
  • Quirk: fears for her brothers
