Wim Doberstein

A gangly lad of fourteen who runs errands for the alchemists' supply shop and earns extra coin cutting the grass at the Tucker place. He comes twice a week with a pair of shears too big for him, and Nina will not let him start until he has thrown the ball for the dog a dozen times. He thinks Shou Tucker the cleverest man in the east because nobody has ever told him otherwise.

Speaking Style: Eager and breathless, a boy who says 'sir' twice in every sentence and forgets to finish half of them.
Combat Role: none; quick feet and a pair of garden shears he would drop before he swung them
Relationship Hook: A freckled boy with shears too big for him being knocked flat on a lawn by a huge white dog while a little girl laughs.

Personality

  • Core: chatters when nervous and is nervous most of the time; loves the white dog more than his own family's; lies badly and blushes when he does
  • Quirk: will not walk past the side of the house where the cellar grating is
