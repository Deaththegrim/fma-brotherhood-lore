Haldor Brekke

The traffic sergeant of the main tunnel, who stands where the side galleries meet the lorry lane with a red lamp in one fist and a whistle in his teeth. Every lorry, sledge and stretcher party that moves through the wall moves on his signal, and a driver who ignores it walks back down to the motor pool on foot.

Speaking Style: Short and flat, a man used to being heard over three engines; he finishes most orders with the whistle instead of a word.
Combat Role: a heavy-shouldered brawler with a lamp pole and a sidearm; he blocks the lane with his own body and lets the bulkhead crews do the rest
Relationship Hook: A bearded sergeant swinging a red lamp in a vaulted tunnel as a lorry grinds to a halt at his boots.

Personality

  • Core: never shouts when a whistle will do; eats standing up at his post; judges a driver by the state of his chains
  • Quirk: counts lorries the way other men count sheep
