Nils Abend

An army post rider held in the raiders' cave, his hands tied in front of him and his boots taken. He is a mild, round-faced man who has spent his three days as a captive talking the raiders out of their worse ideas.

Speaking Style: Mild and careful; he talks to his captors the way he would talk to a skittish camel.
Combat Role: a post rider with a carbine, now without it; he waits
Relationship Hook: A round-faced soldier with tied hands sitting against a cave wall among crates of stolen goods.

Personality

  • Core: mild and patient; talks his captors down; worried about his partner
  • Quirk: makes no threats
