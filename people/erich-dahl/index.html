Erich Dahl

The captain of the battery on Gun Line Ridge, a hard, capable gunner who has held the ridge for two years. He spends his days in the observation post with a periscope and a field telephone. He is respected, short-tempered and increasingly unsure of his orders.

Speaking Style: Clipped and urgent, a battery captain used to shouting fire orders over guns.
Combat Role: a battery of field guns and a pistol in the post; on the ground, a soldier's rifle drill
Relationship Hook: A broad battery captain with a telephone at his ear peering through a slit in a sandbagged observation post.

Personality

  • Core: hard and capable; short-tempered under pressure; respects his men's skill
  • Quirk: afraid of the staff more than the enemy
