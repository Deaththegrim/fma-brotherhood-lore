Margery Cobb

A wiry old woman in a man's coat who breeds and trains the black-and-white collies that work every flock in the district. Half the dogs on the lane are hers or her dogs' grandchildren, and she calls any of them off a sheep with two notes of a whistle. She has no patience for people and endless patience for a slow pup.

Speaking Style: Brisk and whistling, an old woman who talks to people the way she talks to dogs, short and kind.
Combat Role: none; three collies who come at a whistle and a stout shepherd's crook
Relationship Hook: An old woman whistling two sharp notes while a collie drops flat in the lane.

Personality

  • Core: short with people, gentle with dogs; judges a stranger by how a dog takes to them; never sits down indoors
  • Quirk: whistles instead of calling names
