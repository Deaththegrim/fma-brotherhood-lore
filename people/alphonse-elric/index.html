Alphonse Elric

The younger Elric brother, fourteen, who has no body: he lives as a soul bound to a huge suit of armour. Strangers take him for the Fullmetal Alchemist and his brother for the assistant, which Al finds funny and Ed does not. He is gentle, a sharp alchemist in his own right, fond of stray cats he cannot keep, and the calm one of the two. The armour neither eats, sleeps nor feels, and he does his best not to let anyone see how much that hurts.

Speaking Style: A soft, careful boy's voice ringing out of a hollow metal shell, polite to a fault and quick to apologise; when he is frightened or hurt the armour echoes and his words go thin.
Combat Role: alchemy through the armour, circles drawn quickly in chalk or the dirt, and a martial artist's blocks and throws
Relationship Hook: A giant suit of armour kneeling to offer a stray cat a hand.

Personality

  • Core: gentle and apologetic; says sorry before he has done anything; quiet when afraid, loud only for his brother; will not give up on someone, even one who has lied to him
  • Quirk: cannot walk past a stray cat
