Ferdinand Lader

A coal loader at the depot, a giant young man who tips tubs into wagons all day and arm-wrestles the train crews for beer every evening. He is the town's strongest man and the gentlest drunk.

Speaking Style: Loud and good-humoured, a loader who shouts everything because the depot is always loud.
Combat Role: bare fists and a strength that tips a coal tub alone
Relationship Hook: A giant loader chalking a mark on a coal wagon at night while no one is looking.

Personality

  • Core: loud and gentle; arm-wrestles for beer
  • Quirk: uneasy about easy money
