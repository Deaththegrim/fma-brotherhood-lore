Gerold Gries

The town's smith, a heavy grey man who draws out picks, mends cage chains and fits the pit ponies' shoes. Every miner owes him a favour and he has never called one in. He went to East City as a young man to apprentice to an alchemist and came home without a word about it.

Speaking Style: Deep and unhurried, a smith's voice that drops lower when he talks about the books he burned.
Combat Role: a smith's hammer swung short and heavy, and a chalk circle as a last resort
Relationship Hook: A grey smith shutting his forge door before kneeling to draw a chalk circle round a cracked iron hook.

Personality

  • Core: slow-spoken and generous; never calls in a favour; grieves something he will not name
  • Quirk: shuts the door before he does anything unusual
