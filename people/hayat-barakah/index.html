Hayat Barakah

A young farmer from the refugee camp who climbs the terrace stair before dawn with her uncle, carrying water to one green line of wheat on the topmost shelf, where the garrison's glasses do not reach. She carries water up in a goatskin and knows every step by feel.

Speaking Style: Easy and unhurried, a farmer's daughter who talks to plants and people in the same voice.
Combat Role: none; she knows the terrace walls and drops out of sight behind them
Relationship Hook: A young woman watering a single row of green wheat at dawn on a high stone terrace.

Personality

  • Core: patient and hopeful; climbs the stair in the dark; tells the truth too quickly
  • Quirk: counts the wheat heads every morning
