Marthe Engle

The midwife of the hamlet on the far shore of the lake, a sturdy, unhurried woman who also pulls teeth, sets goats' legs and sews up fishermen, since no doctor lives within an hour's sail. She keeps her bag packed by the door and her boat drawn up in front of her cottage.

Speaking Style: Unhurried and plain, a midwife who says the hard thing first and kindly.
Combat Role: none; steady hands and a heavy medical bag, and a whole hamlet that would row out for her
Relationship Hook: A sturdy midwife wading out to a boat in the dark with her bag held above the water.

Personality

  • Core: unhurried in any emergency; packs her bag before she is asked; speaks to the dying plainly
  • Quirk: will not set foot on a train
