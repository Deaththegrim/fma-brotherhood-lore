Ada Fennimore

The gamekeeper's wife at the fox covert, a strong, plain woman who kept the keeper's cottage, skinned his vermin and hung them on the gibbet rail when he was too tired. She knows the covert's runs nearly as well as he did. Every morning now she walks the edge of the gorse with his gun, because he has not come home.

Speaking Style: Hard and level, a keeper's wife who reports facts and refuses comfort.
Combat Role: her husband's shotgun, held steady and fired without hesitation
Relationship Hook: A hard-faced woman with a shotgun under her arm staring at a dog collar nailed among the pelts.

Personality

  • Core: hard and practical; refuses pity; trusts no one from East City
  • Quirk: walks the same round every dawn
