May Chang

The seventeenth princess of Xing, a small girl of the poor Chang clan with dark braids ending in two buns and a tiny panda, Shao May, on her shoulder. She is a gifted practitioner of alkahestry, the Xingese art that reads the flow of the earth and heals as well as harms, and she throws kunai to draw her circles at a distance. She has an overactive imagination and a romantic picture of the Fullmetal Alchemist she has never met.

Speaking Style: Bright, cheeky and quick, a girl's voice that swings from a squeal over a handsome prince to a sharp, serious lecture on alkahestry.
Combat Role: alkahestry thrown through kunai at a distance, and Xingese martial arts close in
Relationship Hook: A tiny princess with a panda on her shoulder, kunai fanned in one hand, lecturing a man twice her size.

Personality

  • Core: impulsive and quick to like or hate; fierce about her clan; kind to anyone hurt in front of her
  • Quirk: imagines everyone as a prince or a villain
