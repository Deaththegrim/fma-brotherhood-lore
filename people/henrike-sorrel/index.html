Henrike Sorrel

The schoolmistress of Dublith, a tall, untidy woman of thirty with chalk on her sleeves, who teaches forty children their letters and sums in one room. She went to Central City at eighteen to study alchemy, lasted two years and came home without a licence and without saying why.

Speaking Style: Warm and enquiring, a teacher who answers a question with a better one and lowers her voice for a secret.
Combat Role: a half-trained alchemist; a chalk circle drawn fast and a simple transmutation that raises a low wall of earth or knocks a man off his feet
Relationship Hook: A tall schoolmistress kneeling in a dusty yard beside a pupil, studying a circle scratched in the dirt.

Personality

  • Core: answers a question with a better question; defends her pupils to anyone; goes quiet when Central City is mentioned
  • Quirk: untidy with chalk, neat with circles
