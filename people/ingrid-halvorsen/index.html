Ingrid Halvorsen

The engineer who runs Frostworks Automail, the north's own workshop for limbs that do not freeze to the skin. She trained in Rush Valley and came north because the problems were harder, and she has made the lightest cold-proof arm in Amestris.

Speaking Style: Cool and exact, an engineer who describes a stripped joint the way others describe a sunset.
Combat Role: an automail engineer with a heavy spanner and an engineer's knowledge of where any steel limb is weakest
Relationship Hook: An engineer with a spanner bent over a stripped automail arm, frost on the workshop window behind her.

Personality

  • Core: cool and absorbed; talks to a limb as she strips it; never fits a port without explaining every step
  • Quirk: loses track of time completely
