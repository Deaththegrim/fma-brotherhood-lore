Jonas Kirk

A private of the Briggs garrison down from the fort with a new automail arm, learning to grip again on the fitting ward. He lost the arm to frostbite on a patrol he will not describe, and he is desperate to get back up the mountain.

Speaking Style: Rough and cheerful, a mountain soldier who jokes about his arm before you can look at it.
Combat Role: a Briggs rifleman relearning his grip; he fights with his new steel arm as a club when the rifle is too slow
Relationship Hook: A young soldier on a hospital bed flexing a new steel arm and grinning through the pain.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful and stubborn; makes jokes about his own arm; loyal to the fort past sense
  • Quirk: angry when he is alone
