Mu Dalang

A camel driver who sleeps beside his animals in the stables and talks to them more than to people. He has crossed the sand to the old ruins and back so many times that he navigates by the taste of the wind.

Speaking Style: Slow and dusty, a camel driver who says three words where others say thirty.
Combat Role: a driver with a goad and a knife; he keeps a kneeling camel between himself and trouble
Relationship Hook: A camel driver sitting in the straw beside his kneeling beast, rolling a green bead between his fingers.

Personality

  • Core: talks to his camels as to old friends; says little and remembers everything; sleeps in the straw by choice
  • Quirk: superstitious about the ruins of Xerxes and never camps inside them
