Erich Danzer

The billeting sergeant of Pendleton finds beds for every company that rotates through the town and knows every chalked door by its number. He is a born fixer, cheerful and well liked, and he can find tobacco in a town that has none.

Speaking Style: Easy and wry, a billet sergeant who makes a joke of everything and a deal of the rest.
Combat Role: a sergeant's carbine and a knife in the boot; he fights dirty, fast and from the side
Relationship Hook: A wiry grinning sergeant chalking a number on a battered door, a carbine on his back.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful and endlessly resourceful; knows everyone and owes everyone; jokes in the worst moments
  • Quirk: keeps a Cretan cigarette case and never says where it came from
