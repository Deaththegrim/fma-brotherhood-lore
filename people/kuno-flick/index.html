Kuno Flick

A one-time army carter turned smuggler who runs goods out of Youswell through the old drift and past the checkpoint: coal sold off the books, the widows' cheeses, letters the post office would burn. He is likeable, unreliable and always paid in advance.

Speaking Style: Friendly and oily, a smuggler who calls everyone 'friend' a moment before naming his price.
Combat Role: a short cudgel and a carter's dirty tricks, then a fast retreat
Relationship Hook: A grinning smuggler leading a laden mule into a black tunnel mouth by lantern light.

Personality

  • Core: charming and slippery; never carries a weapon openly; loyal only to his mule
  • Quirk: works for both sides
