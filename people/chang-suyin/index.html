Chang Suyin

A young warrior of the poor Chang clan who has sat on the cloud stair for nine days, in mist and rain, waiting for the hermit to teach her. She is thin, wet, stubborn and cheerful about all three.

Speaking Style: Bright and stubborn, cheerful through cold and hunger and a little too loud for the mountain.
Combat Role: a young Chang fighter with a short staff and a few kunai; game, quick, untrained
Relationship Hook: A wet young warrior sitting cross-legged on a misty stone stair, grinning stubbornly up into the cloud.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful in the rain; talks to herself on the stair to keep warm; proud of being a Chang
  • Quirk: refuses every offer of a blanket
