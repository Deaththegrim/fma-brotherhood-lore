Cornelius Roeder

The schoolmaster of the lower town, a mild, shabby man who teaches millers' and tanners' children their letters and sums. He reads them the State Military's notices as lessons in civics, in a voice so flat that the children giggle.

Speaking Style: Mild and kindly, a schoolmaster who always asks a question back instead of giving an answer.
Combat Role: a half-trained alchemist; a chalk circle drawn fast on the floor, a wall of earth or a stone floor buckled under a foe, if he has time
Relationship Hook: A shabby schoolmaster crouched by a child's slate, chalk in hand, staring at a circle he never taught.

Personality

  • Core: mild and patient; answers a question with a question; keeps a box of chalk for any child who asks
  • Quirk: reads army notices in a flat voice
