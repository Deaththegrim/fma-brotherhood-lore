Nils Ober

A recruit of seventeen, three weeks in uniform, who still writes to his mother in a farmhouse north of the city every night. He is all elbows and enthusiasm, polishes his boots twice and believes every poster he has ever seen. The older men like him and keep the worst stories from him.

Speaking Style: Eager and quick, a boy's voice that cracks when he is trying to sound older.
Combat Role: a recruit's rifle, loaded carefully and fired too high
Relationship Hook: A gangly recruit on a bunk writing a letter with his cap pushed back.

Personality

  • Core: eager to please; polishes everything twice; believes the posters
  • Quirk: homesick and hiding it
