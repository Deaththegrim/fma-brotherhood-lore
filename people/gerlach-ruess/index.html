Gerlach Ruess

Weighmaster of the coal wharf for fifteen years, a heavy man gone grey-black with dust who stands at the crane berth with a clipboard and a pencil stub and calls out every barge's tonnage as the grabs swing it ashore. Bargemen curse his scales on the quay and buy him gin in the shelter afterwards, which he accepts as the natural order of things. His daughter keeps his books in a neat hand he cannot match.

Speaking Style: A growl from somewhere under the coal dust; he rounds his words down the way he rounds a load.
Combat Role: a heavy, slow man with an iron counterweight on a short chain, swung once and hard
Relationship Hook: A coal-blackened weighmaster with a clipboard squinting up at a crane grab swinging over a barge.

Personality

  • Core: greedy in a small, steady, unambitious way; cannot bear to be hurried at the scales; frightened of the mine's owners and of nobody else
  • Quirk: treats every bargeman as an old friend and every miner as a stranger
