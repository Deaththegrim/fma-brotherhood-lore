Brother Tobin

The sexton of the Church of Leto in Reole, who keeps the nave swept, the candles lit and the crypt locked. He knows the church better than Cornello does and has heard more confessions than the preacher has. He is not a believer and never says so. He keeps the keys and asks nothing.

Speaking Style: Dry and grumbling, a sexton who speaks with the bored patience of a man who has cleaned the same floor for twenty years.
Combat Role: none; he has a lamp, a broom and a bunch of keys
Relationship Hook: A sexton with a ring of keys, sweeping the nave and watching who comes in.

Personality

  • Core: dry, tired and loyal to his keys; asks no questions and remembers every answer; complains about the stairs and the damp
  • Quirk: never says what he believes
