Hong Shuisheng

Master of the Yellow Silt Ferry, from the family that has worked the crossing for six generations. He knows every face that uses his ferry and the business of most of them, and he splices the great rope himself.

Speaking Style: Deep and slow, the voice of a man hauling a rope across a river every day of his life.
Combat Role: a ferryman with a boat pole and arms like rope; he fights on a deck that only he can stand on
Relationship Hook: A broad ferry master running a cut rope through his scarred hands in a shed full of coils.

Personality

  • Core: splices the rope himself and trusts no one with it; knows every regular by face and business; loyal to the Yao clan like a debt
  • Quirk: reads the river's colour every morning
