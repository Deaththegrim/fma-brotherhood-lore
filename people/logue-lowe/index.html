Logue Lowe

The Grand Cleric of Ishval, spiritual leader of the faith of Ishvala and a man his people hold to be the god's prophet. When the war began he went into hiding in the wilderness to keep the faith's structure alive. He is wise, grave and grieving, and he has watched the army take his people's towns one by one.

Speaking Style: Deep, grave and patient, a holy man who speaks in measured lines and prophesies without anger.
Combat Role: none; a prophet's word and his people's faith
Relationship Hook: An old cleric in white walking out of the desert toward the army's lines, hands empty.

Personality

  • Core: grave and patient; prays before every decision; prophesies without hatred
  • Quirk: will trade his own life for his people's
