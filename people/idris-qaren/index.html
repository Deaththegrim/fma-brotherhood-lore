Idris Qaren

A tinsmith of the row, a lean, scarred Ishvalan who learned metalwork in the bazaars of Ishval before the war and now makes stovepipes and buckets for a town that pays and does not thank. He works fast, talks little and sings at the anvil.

Speaking Style: Low and even, a man who sings at his anvil and says little else.
Combat Role: a smith who fights with tin shears and a hammer; quick, close, and aimed at hands
Relationship Hook: A lean Ishvalan smith hammering a tin sheet on an anvil, singing under the noise.

Personality

  • Core: sings at the anvil; never haggles with another Ishvalan; keeps the scar on his arm covered
  • Quirk: argues with the elders and loses gracefully
