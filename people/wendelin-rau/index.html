Wendelin Rau

The hermit of the tarn is a gaunt, silent fisherman who has lived in a turf hut on the black shore for years. He nets trout in the snowmelt, trades fish with the goatherds and turns away anyone who asks him where he came from.

Speaking Style: Slow and hoarse from disuse, a hermit who speaks as if remembering how.
Combat Role: an old infantry soldier; he still handles a rifle like a drill sergeant and still hates to
Relationship Hook: A gaunt bearded hermit in a faded army greatcoat standing by a black mountain lake, nets over his shoulder.

Personality

  • Core: silent unless pressed; never lights a fire by day; trades fish and never takes coin
  • Quirk: wakes shouting and goes back to sleep
