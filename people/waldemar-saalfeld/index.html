Waldemar Saalfeld

The captain who heads the fort's research and development shop, a precise grey man with a slide rule in his breast pocket and drawings rolled under each arm. Six winters have gone into turning steel plate, a field gun and a tractor engine into the machine on the assembly floor, and he talks about it the way other men talk about their children.

Speaking Style: Measured and exact, every number given to the decimal; he warms only when the talk turns to steel.
Combat Role: an engineer officer who fights with what the shop holds; a pistol, a pry bar, and a hoist chain dropped at the right moment
Relationship Hook: A grey captain in spectacles bent over a drawing table, slide rule in hand, while a turret hangs from a hoist beyond the glass.

Personality

  • Core: measures twice and argues once; trusts figures before rank; turns almost gentle when a welder asks a good question; never lets a visitor past the middle of the hall
  • Quirk: corrects a drawing while talking about something else
