Reinald Steen

Master of the stone quarry, a short, broad man deafened in one ear by black powder, who cuts a few blocks a month for walls, gateposts and the odd headstone blank for Bertil Sennholz. He works the face with three men and a timber crane and shouts everything. The town's old houses came out of his hill, and he likes to point at them.

Speaking Style: Hoarse and blunt, a quarryman who shouts by habit because the drills taught him to.
Combat Role: a quarryman's pick, and a stick of black powder he would only ever use on stone
Relationship Hook: A short, broad quarryman bellowing up at a crane, pick on his shoulder.

Personality

  • Core: proud of every old wall in town; fond of powder and careful with it; superstitious about the drowned workings
  • Quirk: shouts everything
