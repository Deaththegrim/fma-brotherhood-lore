Gu Fenglin

Keeper of the Sand Rim Caravanserai, a big, genial man who meets every caravan at the gate with water, dates and a question about the crossing. He has kept the last walled stop on the western road for twenty years, as his father did before him, and he holds the only key to the storeroom behind his quarters.

Speaking Style: Warm as an uncle and twice as interested in what is in your bags.
Combat Role: a heavy man with a heavy cudgel and twenty drivers who owe him favours; he shouts first and swings second
Relationship Hook: A big keeper in a sand-coloured robe holding out a water dipper to a dust-caked driver at the gate.

Personality

  • Core: greets every driver by name and every camel by its temper; laughs from the belly at his own jokes; never asks what is inside anything
  • Quirk: keeps the storeroom key on a cord round his neck, even in the bath
