Leonhard Esch

A wine merchant who rents the old weighing hall as his warehouse, the third of his family to deal in southern wines. He is courteous, generous and well liked, and he never says a hard word about Aerugo, which some of his neighbours hold against him.

Speaking Style: Warm and persuasive, a merchant whose voice makes a bad deal sound like a fair one.
Combat Role: none; a merchant with a cart and a good story
Relationship Hook: A well-dressed merchant rolling a wine barrel across a stone weighing hall.

Personality

  • Core: courteous and generous; never says a hard word about Aerugo
  • Quirk: brave in a quiet, foolish way
