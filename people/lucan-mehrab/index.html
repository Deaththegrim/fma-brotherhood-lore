Lucan Mehrab

A former deacon of the church who leads a handful of the prophet's last loyalists in a cellar under a burned house. They light candles under a painted sun and talk about the day it will rise again over Reole.

Speaking Style: Soft, fervent and coaxing; he speaks as if to one person even in a room full.
Combat Role: none of his own; he keeps two big believers between himself and any trouble
Relationship Hook: A thin preacher in a worn yellow robe lifting a candle toward a sun painted on a cellar wall.

Personality

  • Core: fervent and ambitious; remembers every face that comes down the cellar stair; quotes the prophet word for word
  • Quirk: keeps the anonymous letters in his robe
