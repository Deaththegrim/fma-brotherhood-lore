Dolcetto

A short, stocky swordsman in a white keikogi who keeps the door at the Devil's Nest and draws a katana faster than most men draw a pistol. He has a dog's nose and, he says, a dog's loyalty. He is touchy about his strength after losing a few fights in a row, and he knows when not to kill.

Speaking Style: Short-tempered and blunt, a swordsman's clipped bark that sulks when he is teased about losing.
Combat Role: a fast katana and a dog's senses, cuts aimed to disarm
Relationship Hook: A short swordsman in white at a bar door, sniffing the air with his hand on his katana.

Personality

  • Core: blunt and touchy about his strength; loyal to Greed like a dog, he says; grateful to be alive at all
  • Quirk: smells trouble before he sees it
