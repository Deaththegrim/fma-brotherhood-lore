Felix Bramm

The telegraph operator at the end of the line, a young corporal who taps out convoy reports and weather and reads cheap adventure novels between messages. He is lonely and glad of anyone who will talk about the novels.

Speaking Style: Chatty and quick; he talks the way he taps, in bursts, and laughs at the end of each one.
Combat Role: none worth naming; a signaller with a pistol he has never fired
Relationship Hook: A skinny freckled soldier at a telegraph key with a cheap novel open beside him.

Personality

  • Core: lonely and chatty; reads cheap novels; copies every message
  • Quirk: frightened of the dark beyond the hut
