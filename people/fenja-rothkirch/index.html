Fenja Rothkirch

The night supervisor of the switchboard girls, a sharp-eyed young woman who walks behind the row of stools with a pencil and catches a crossed line before the caller has noticed. She has worked the exchange since it opened and can find any number in the city from memory. The soldier standing behind the army's two boards brings her coffee and is trying, so far without success, to make her laugh.

Speaking Style: Clear and practised, an operator's voice that says 'number please' even when she is only asking the time.
Combat Role: none; a sharp tongue, a long pencil and the ability to cut off any call in the city
Relationship Hook: A young operator in a headset walking behind a row of switchboard girls, pencil raised to point at a lamp.

Personality

  • Core: quick, crisp and hard to fluster; keeps a list of her girls' debts
  • Quirk: hears everything and repeats nothing
