Ma Deshi

A lean young Xingese who arrived at the envoys' camp alone and has been rebuilding its cairn stone by stone. His grandfather's grandfather guarded an envoy who died on the way west, and the family has always known where the guard was buried.

Speaking Style: Quiet and deliberate, a fighter who speaks in short level sentences and means each one.
Combat Role: a Xingese martial artist; open hand, low kicks and a short staff, fast and quiet
Relationship Hook: A lean young man in black kneeling to set a stone back on a pulled-apart cairn.

Personality

  • Core: calm and relentless; keeps a vow he made at the grave; polite to everyone
  • Quirk: practises forms at dawn
