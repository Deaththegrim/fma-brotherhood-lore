Emmerich Pottle

Proprietor of the dry goods store, a mild, bald man in sleeve garters who sells flour, nails, lamp oil and sweets and will send to East City for anything else. He gives credit easily and remembers it precisely. Children like him because the sweet jar sits low on the counter; their parents like him less by the end of each winter.

Speaking Style: Mild and obliging, a shopkeeper whose kindness always arrives with a book.
Combat Role: none; a shotgun under the counter he has never fired
Relationship Hook: A mild bald shopkeeper writing in a book while a child reaches into the sweet jar.

Personality

  • Core: mild and obliging; writes everything down; gives sweets to children whose parents owe him
  • Quirk: never raises his voice or forgives a debt for nothing
