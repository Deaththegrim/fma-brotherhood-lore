Philippa Marsh

The landlady of the coaching inn on the north road keeps the best cider in the West and the warmest fire on the road. She is a handsome widow of fifty who runs the inn alone and treats every traveller as a guest she has been expecting.

Speaking Style: Bright and warm, a landlady's welcome with a faint foreign lilt she has never quite lost.
Combat Role: none; a cellar door bolted from inside, and a cider cask rolled down the stairs if it came to that
Relationship Hook: A handsome auburn-haired landlady pouring cider in a cool cellar of casks.

Personality

  • Core: welcoming to everyone; keeps the best cider on the road; a faint foreign lilt she cannot lose
  • Quirk: nervous of soldiers and hides it well
