Barnaby Quell

The keeper of the toll house at the foot of the climb to Rush Valley, a lean man with a drooping moustache and a book for anything with wheels. He lifts the striped pole for carts that pay, waves walkers round it, and knows every carter on the road by name, load and temper. His shotgun hangs on two nails above the stove and has not come down in years.

Speaking Style: Dry and drawling, a toll keeper who answers slowly and writes while he talks.
Combat Role: an old shotgun he has not fired in years and the barrier pole dropped across a cart's path
Relationship Hook: A toll keeper raising a striped pole for a cart, writing its name down before it has passed.

Personality

  • Core: writes before he speaks; loyal to carters, careful with soldiers; keeps the stove lit in every weather
  • Quirk: pulls at his moustache when lying
