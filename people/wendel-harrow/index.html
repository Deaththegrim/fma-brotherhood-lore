Wendel Harrow

The deputy of the shift at Mine Number Eight, the man who goes down first with the safety lamp and comes up last with the tally. He is broad, bald under his cap and fast with his fists, and the young men on his crew would follow him into a fire.

Speaking Style: Short words, loud in the dark, a deputy who gives orders as if each one costs him.
Combat Role: a pick handle and a brawler's head-down rush, learned in pit-yard fights
Relationship Hook: A shift deputy holding up a brass lamp in a low gallery while the timbers creak overhead.

Personality

  • Core: blunt and fast to decide; counts his men under his breath; trusts timber more than paper; loses his temper and apologises after
  • Quirk: will not leave a man below
