Tarvo Rinne

The head groom of the remount stables, a slow, soft-voiced man who talks to horses in a hill dialect and to officers as little as he can. He can tell which animal will face a blizzard by watching it eat.

Speaking Style: Slow and soft in a hill accent, the voice he uses on frightened horses and rarely on men.
Combat Role: a groom's defence; a pitchfork, a stable door slammed, and a horse taught to rear at a whistle
Relationship Hook: A soft-spoken groom leading a shaggy, blanketed horse down a row of stalls, murmuring to it.

Personality

  • Core: talks to horses as people and to people as weather; grooms Smith's horse before his own supper; reads with a finger under each word
  • Quirk: goes out into any storm after a loose horse
