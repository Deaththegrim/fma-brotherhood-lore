Lambrecht Tilney

Resembool's stationmaster, a neat grey man in a railway cap who has met every train into the town for twenty-two years and still checks his watch against the engine's whistle. He was on the platform the morning of the bombing in 1907 and came away with a ringing in one ear that has never stopped. He keeps the waiting room swept, the timetable brass polished and a bucket of sand by every door for reasons he will not discuss.

Speaking Style: Precise and a little pompous, a stationmaster who announces the time as if it were news.
Combat Role: none; a heavy brass signal lamp and the authority of the platform whistle
Relationship Hook: A stationmaster holding his watch up to an arriving engine, one hand cupped behind his ear.

Personality

  • Core: fussy about minutes, forgiving about everything else; proud of the station, prouder of its clock; jumps at a slammed door and pretends he did not
  • Quirk: taps his deaf ear when he does not want to hear a question
