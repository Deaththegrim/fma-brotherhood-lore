Dankwart Riepe

Quartermaster-sergeant and the army's beef buyer at the yards, a plump man in a bowler hat worn over regulations who walks the holding pens at dawn with a stick and a fat purse. Drovers from the eastern farms like dealing with him: he pays on the nail, haggles cheerfully and never makes them wait for a clerk.

Speaking Style: Easy and oily, a buyer who calls every drover 'friend' and means it about as far as the purse goes.
Combat Role: none; a walking stick, a purse and a gift for being elsewhere when the shouting starts
Relationship Hook: A plump quartermaster in a bowler hat leaning on a cattle pen and counting notes into a drover's palm.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful, plausible and greedy; never looks a beast in the eye; panics quietly and goes on smiling
  • Quirk: tells the same three jokes to every drover
