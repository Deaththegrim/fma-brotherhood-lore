Gesche Wust

The loading foreman on the depot siding, a broad woman who runs a crew of loaders and gets a freight train empty faster than anyone in the north. Her crew is mostly widows and boys, and she will hear no word against any of them.

Speaking Style: Gravelly and short, a loader foreman who talks in shouts even when she whispers.
Combat Role: none; a cargo hook and a crew of loaders who would swing a crate for her
Relationship Hook: A broad foreman with a cargo hook shouting a crew of loaders across a snowy siding.

Personality

  • Core: loud and fair; protects her crew; works harder than any of them
  • Quirk: keeps notes on what she should not
