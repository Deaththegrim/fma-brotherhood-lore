Lutz Ammer

A pit boy of ten who carries his father's and uncles' dinner pails along the approach road at noon, because the road behind the mine is shorter than the lanes. He is quiet, stubborn and very good at noticing things lying in the sand.

Speaking Style: A slow, serious boy who answers questions with exactly as many words as they need.
Combat Role: none; he runs, and he knows every gap in the mine fence
Relationship Hook: A small boy kneeling on a sandy roadside, holding a tin pail out to a figure lying face down.

Personality

  • Core: solemn for his age; shares food before he is asked; afraid of soldiers
  • Quirk: keeps secrets very well
