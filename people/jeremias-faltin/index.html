Jeremias Faltin

An eel-catcher and wildfowler who has lived in a hide on stilts in the reed marsh below the city for twelve years, since he left the army's scouts with a fowling gun and no wish to see a road again. He sells eels and ducks at the tannery gate and the brewery yard, says as little as he can and can cross the whole marsh by night in a punt without touching a light.

Speaking Style: A low rasp, never above a murmur, as if the reeds might be listening.
Combat Role: an old army scout with a long fowling gun and a gutting knife; he fights from the reeds, shoots once, moves, and is never where the reply lands
Relationship Hook: A lean bearded man in an oilskin coat poling a flat punt through tall reeds at dusk, a long gun across his knees.

Personality

  • Core: silent, watchful and patient; trusts the marsh more than any man; eats what he catches and nothing else
  • Quirk: keeps every promise and makes very few
