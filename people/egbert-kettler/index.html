Egbert Kettler

The manager of the Westbank Gasworks, a stout man in a frock coat with soot on the cuffs he pretends not to see. He reads the gasholders the way a captain reads the sky and can say to the hour when the city's kitchens light their stoves. He is proud of the works, contemptuous of the council and cold to the stokers, whom he counts as tonnage.

Speaking Style: Measured and flat, a manager who quotes figures instead of answering questions.
Combat Role: none; a manager who calls the yard's watchmen and stands behind the gate
Relationship Hook: A stout manager in a frock coat staring up at a rising gasholder, a clipboard pressed to his chest.

Personality

  • Core: proud, exact and cold; talks in cubic feet; afraid of inspectors and of officers alike
  • Quirk: rewards any man who keeps his mouth shut
