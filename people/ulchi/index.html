Ulchi

A tall crocodile chimera who keeps the ground floor of the Devil's Nest, throws drunks out and makes passes at any woman who comes in. He has scaled skin and a jaw that clamps. He is all swagger and appetite, and he once made the mistake of trying it on with a butcher's wife.

Speaking Style: Loud, leering and quick, with a snickering laugh and a line for every woman who walks in.
Combat Role: a crocodile's bite and bulk, a bouncer's shove
Relationship Hook: A tall scaly bouncer leaning over a woman at the bar, not noticing her husband behind him.

Personality

  • Core: picks on anyone who looks weak; swaggers until someone bigger arrives; treats Greed as a legend
  • Quirk: loud, leering and pushy
