Leopold Vantreck

The doctor of the Rush Valley Nerve Clinic, a tall, stooped man from Central City who came south to study automail ports for a year and has stayed nine. He joins nerve to metal with a patience the engineers lack, sits with patients through the worst nights of a fitting and keeps a chart of every port he has set, with its date and its maker.

Speaking Style: Slow and level, a doctor who gets quieter the angrier he is and never once promises a date.
Combat Role: none; a surgeon's steady hands and a bottle of ether he will use on anyone who threatens his ward
Relationship Hook: A stooped doctor sitting on the edge of an iron bed, holding a crying boy's steel ankle still.

Personality

  • Core: speaks more slowly the angrier he gets; keeps a chart of everything; never promises a patient a date
  • Quirk: sits up with the frightened ones himself
