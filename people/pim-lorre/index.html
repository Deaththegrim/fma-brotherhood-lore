Pim Lorre

A private of nineteen from a farm in the east, posted to Youswell because nobody else wanted the post. He guards the lockup, sleeps through most of it and has made more friends in town than the rest of the garrison together.

Speaking Style: Cheeky and quick, a young private who cannot keep a grin off his face for long.
Combat Role: a rifle he fires badly and a pair of quick legs
Relationship Hook: A freckled young private grinning through the bars of a cell he is supposed to be guarding.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful and careless; a terrible liar
  • Quirk: sides with the miners without thinking about it
