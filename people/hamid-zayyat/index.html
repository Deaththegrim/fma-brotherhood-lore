Hamid Zayyat

Reole's gravedigger, a thin old man with a spade worn to a crescent and a register of every burial since his grandfather's time. He dug the graves of both sides of the town's quarrel and wrote them in the same book in the same hand.

Speaking Style: Mild and slow, a gravedigger who speaks of the dead as tenants and of the living as visitors.
Combat Role: none; he leans on his spade until trouble leaves
Relationship Hook: An old gravedigger leaning on a spade in a shed doorway, watching a figure among the stones.

Personality

  • Core: gentle and unbothered; keeps the register in perfect order; talks to the graves as he passes
  • Quirk: lets the scraper work and says nothing
