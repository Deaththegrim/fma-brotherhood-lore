Engelbert Stoss

Foreman of the retort house, short, broad and missing most of both eyebrows, who has fed coal to the furnaces that light East City for twenty-five years. His stokers call him the old man to his face and worse behind it, and every one of them would walk into a burning bench after him. He tells the time by the gasholders like the children do.

Speaking Style: A furnace-hall bark, loud from habit; he has to remember to lower it in a parlour.
Combat Role: a short broad man with a stoker's shovel he swings like an axe, and twenty men at his back
Relationship Hook: A short broad foreman with a pipe clenched in his teeth glaring down a hall of glowing furnaces.

Personality

  • Core: loud, loyal and suspicious; knows the birthday of every stoker's child; trusts no hand who has worked less than five years
  • Quirk: chews his pipe stem when he thinks
