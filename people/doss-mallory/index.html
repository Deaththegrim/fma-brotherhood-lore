Doss Mallory

A scrapper of Baschool who strips the dead rail line for iron and sells it to the town's smiths. He is rough, hoarse and practical, and he knows every rusted bolt on the line. He has seen the soldiers come and go and keeps his distance from both sides.

Speaking Style: Rough and hoarse, a scrapper who speaks about metal as if it were alive.
Combat Role: none; a crowbar and a cart
Relationship Hook: A scrapper pulling rusted iron from a dead rail line beside a cart.
