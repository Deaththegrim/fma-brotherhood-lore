Gerolf Krall

The lieutenant in charge of the railhead, who meets every train from North City with a clipboard, a flask of coffee and a temper. He has to turn a freight car into lorry loads before the pass closes for the night, and he has done it every day for two winters.

Speaking Style: Clipped and impatient, a man who speaks in loads, minutes and last light.
Combat Role: a junior officer with a revolver and a loading crew who follow him; he fights from behind the stacked sacks
Relationship Hook: A lieutenant with a clipboard glaring at a sealed freight car, his breath steaming.

Personality

  • Core: checks his watch every few minutes; swears at the crane and apologises to its driver; drinks his coffee boiling
  • Quirk: keeps two sets of figures
