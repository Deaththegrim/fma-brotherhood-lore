King Bradley

King Bradley, the Führer of Amestris, fifty-nine, who has ruled for twenty years and sent the army into one war after another. He is an older man with an extraordinary physique, slicked black hair, a black moustache and an eyepatch over his left eye, and he is the finest swordsman in the country. In public he is affable, informal with soldiers and fond of his wife and son; his enemies tend to die.

Speaking Style: A deep, courteous voice that is often warm and even playful with subordinates; when it goes quiet, someone dies.
Combat Role: five sabres and an eye that sees every move, a swordsman fast enough to cut bullets
Relationship Hook: An eyepatched Führer stepping off a train with a sabre at his side, smiling at a frightened lieutenant.

Personality

  • Core: courteous and informal with soldiers; fond of his wife; speaks softly when most dangerous
  • Quirk: enjoys a worthy opponent
