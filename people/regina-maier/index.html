Regina Maier

The senior operator at the South City Telephone Exchange, who runs the board that carries the calls to the front. She is twenty-eight, sharp as a pin and never seems to listen, which is why the generals talk so freely on her lines. She has worked the board since before the war and knows every officer by the first word he speaks.

Speaking Style: Crisp and level, an operator's voice that says 'connecting you' and means nothing else by it.
Combat Role: none; a headset cord and a quick hand on the alarm bell
Relationship Hook: A poised operator plugging a cord into a tall switchboard, eyes on nothing.

Personality

  • Core: never seems to be listening; recognises any voice from one word; sharp-tongued off the board
  • Quirk: keeps a list of oddities in shorthand
