Imke Dannhauer

A seamstress in her forties who keeps the geraniums in the stone trough beneath the bronze plate by the station, and has done so every week since her husband's name went up on it. She still wears mourning seven years on, faded now to grey. The town thinks her devoted. She is mostly angry.

Speaking Style: Flat and tired, a widow who has said the same thing to every official and expects no answer.
Combat Role: none; dressmaker's shears and a hatpin
Relationship Hook: A woman in faded mourning watering geraniums beneath a bronze list of the dead, glaring at one name.

Personality

  • Core: tends the flowers with her jaw set; answers kindness with suspicion; sews faster when she is angry
  • Quirk: never says her husband's name aloud
