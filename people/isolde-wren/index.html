Isolde Wren

A State Alchemist posted to the southern front, a major in her thirties whose speciality is the transmutation of metals: she bends rails, seals trench walls and makes bridges out of wreckage. She is cool, exacting and very private. Her silver watch is always in her hand.

Speaking Style: Low and level, an alchemist who weighs every word as she would a reagent.
Combat Role: metal transmutation through a circle tattooed inside her wrist; she turns wire, rails and helmets into walls and spikes
Relationship Hook: A tall alchemist in a field coat kneeling by a stretcher, a glow of alchemy under her gloved hands.

Personality

  • Core: cool and exacting; kind only in the dark; breaks rules with great care
  • Quirk: turns her silver watch over and over
