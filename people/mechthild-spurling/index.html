Mechthild Spurling

A spare, sharp-eyed widow who sells steel cable by the yard in the covered market, cutting it with shears as long as her forearm. She came to Rush Valley with one drum of wire and now has six. She will not haggle, will not gossip and will not serve a man who has been drinking, and the trade respects all three.

Speaking Style: Flat and final, a widow who states the length and the price and does not say either twice.
Combat Role: none; long cable shears held point out, and a reach that keeps a man at arm's length
Relationship Hook: A widow in black cutting steel cable with long shears, refusing to name a lower figure.

Personality

  • Core: refuses drunks on principle; keeps her debts in her head like a book; softens only for apprentices with burnt hands
  • Quirk: measures twice, cuts once and talks never
