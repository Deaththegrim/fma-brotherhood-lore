Liesl Dorn

The owner of the Kettle and Stove Café, a round, cheerful woman who lets clerks and junior officers sit all afternoon over one coffee because a full room keeps the heat in. She knows how everyone takes their cup and who they came in with.

Speaking Style: Cosy and teasing, a café keeper who calls everyone 'dear' and means it about half the time.
Combat Role: none; a coffee pot of boiling water and a broom she swings like a halberd
Relationship Hook: A round café keeper pouring coffee for a clerk while watching two officers by the window.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful, teasing, shrewd; remembers every customer's cup; keeps the stove roaring whatever coal costs
  • Quirk: has a soft heart for underfed clerks
