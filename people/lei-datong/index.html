Lei Datong

Master smith of the lane, a barrel-chested man who balances each finished knife on one finger before he lets it go. His mark at the base of a blade is a small lightning stroke, and alkahestrists of a dozen clans buy their circle knives from nobody else.

Speaking Style: A hammer-bench growl, loud from years of shouting over anvils.
Combat Role: a smith with a hammer and a rack of his own knives; strong arms, a straight throw and no grace
Relationship Hook: A barrel-chested smith balancing a finished throwing knife on one fingertip in the steam of a quench trough.

Personality

  • Core: balances every knife on one finger; shouts over an anvil even where there is none; proud of his mark above all else
  • Quirk: slow to anger and slower to forgive
