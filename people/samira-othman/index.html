Samira Othman

A young widow who keeps the temple's ablution court, carrying water from the qanat outflow at dawn to fill the stone basin for whoever comes to wash before prayer. Most days nobody comes but the priests; she fills it anyway.

Speaking Style: Soft and patient; she speaks to strangers in the gentle tone people use with frightened animals.
Combat Role: none; she stays calm and talks the frightened down
Relationship Hook: A young widow pouring water from a clay jar into a stone basin in an empty court.

Personality

  • Core: gentle and persistent; fills the basin whether anyone comes or not; asks every soldier about her husband
  • Quirk: hides how far she now walks for water
