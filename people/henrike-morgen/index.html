Henrike Morgen

A schoolmaster's widow in good black who comes to the new rows of the war cemetery every morning with fresh flowers. Her husband was a reserve lieutenant, called up at forty and killed in his second week at Fotset. She is composed, educated and very stubborn.

Speaking Style: Steady and low, a widow who has done her crying and now wants answers.
Combat Role: none; a lawyer's letter and a straight back
Relationship Hook: A widow in black standing before a fresh wooden grave marker with flowers in one hand and a spade in the other.

Personality

  • Core: composed and immovable; polite in a way that frightens clerks
  • Quirk: brings flowers to the unvisited graves too
