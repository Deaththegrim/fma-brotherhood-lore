Yakov Belling

The trader at the pelt post, a dark-eyed man of mixed Amestrian and Drachman blood who grades each pelt with his thumb and a look, and whose grades are final. He sells traps, powder, coffee and advice, and only the advice comes cheap.

Speaking Style: Smooth and dark, with a faint Drachman lilt he lets grow stronger when he is bargaining.
Combat Role: a trader with a shotgun under the counter and a skinning knife in his boot
Relationship Hook: A dark-eyed trader running his thumb through a pelt on the counter, already naming its grade.

Personality

  • Core: grades a pelt with his thumb before his eyes; talks to his coffee pot in Drachman; never haggles twice
  • Quirk: likes a good liar better than a bad honest man
