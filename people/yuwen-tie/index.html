Yuwen Tie

The hermit at the top of the cloud stair, an old warrior who served in the Emperor's own guard and left in disgrace. He lives in a bare stone room with one mat and one bowl, and young warriors climb thousands of steps to sit on his stair and ask him to teach them.

Speaking Style: Slow, deep and rare, a voice that speaks once in a day and means it.
Combat Role: a royal guard master; unarmed, he moves on a narrow stair like water, and anyone who comes up it comes down faster
Relationship Hook: An old hermit standing barefoot in a bare stone room, looking down into the mist at a figure on the stair.

Personality

  • Core: speaks once a day and means it; eats from one bowl and sleeps on one mat; tests students by ignoring them
  • Quirk: has not forgotten one face from the palace
