Shao May

May Chang's pet panda, a tiny black-and-white panda no bigger than a cat, who rides on her mistress's shoulder and copies her every pose. She was abandoned by her own kind for being small, and May raised her like a sister. She is sweet to May and vicious to anyone else who gets too close.

Speaking Style: Squeaks, hisses and pleased chirps; she copies her mistress's sounds a moment late.
Combat Role: a biter; she leaps for a hand and clings until the foe gives up
Relationship Hook: A tiny panda on a girl's shoulder copying her pose and baring its teeth.

Personality

  • Core: fierce to strangers, gentle to May; copies her mistress's moves; hates being called a cat
  • Quirk: afraid of very large armour
