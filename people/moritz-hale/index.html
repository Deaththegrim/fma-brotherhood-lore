Moritz Hale

The old shepherd's grandson, twelve, who spends every hour he can at the hill oak, swinging on the frayed rope or lying along the low bough reading anything with print on it. He is thoughtful, solemn and a little lonely. He can name every set of initials in the trunk and most of the hands that cut them.

Speaking Style: Quiet and careful, a boy who weighs his words as if someone were checking them.
Combat Role: none; up the tree and out of reach in three seconds
Relationship Hook: A solemn boy lying along an oak bough with a book, a frayed rope swing turning below him.

Personality

  • Core: solemn and bookish; proud and lonely; knows every name in the bark
  • Quirk: keeps watch from the bough
