Amalie Steinmar

The registrar of deaths at the town hall, a spare, grey-eyed woman of fifty in steel spectacles who writes every certificate in the city's best copperplate. She has kept the book of the dead for twenty years and can tell from the weight of a page which winter it was written in. She is courteous to every uniform and does exactly what the law says, which some of them find harder to bear than rudeness.

Speaking Style: Precise and quiet, a registrar who reads a question back to you before she answers it.
Combat Role: none; a book, the law and a clear voice in a public counter-room
Relationship Hook: A thin registrar in spectacles looking up from a vast book over a counter, pen held still.

Personality

  • Core: exact, patient and unbending; reads every form twice, then once more aloud; will not sign what she has not seen
  • Quirk: treats a widow's question as more important than a major's
