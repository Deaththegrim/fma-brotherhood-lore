Drachman Commander

The commander of the Drachman army on the Briggs front, whose name nobody in Amestris knows. He trusts a great train of artillery to break the northern wall and expects the snow to open the pass for his men. He is cold, certain and dismissive of the soldiers who die for him.

Speaking Style: Cold and contemptuous, a commander who announces his attack like a verdict and berates the survivors when it fails.
Combat Role: artillery command from behind the line
Relationship Hook: A Drachman commander on a snowy ridge above a line of artillery, looking towards the fort.

Personality

  • Core: cold, certain and dismissive; gives orders through aides; never admits a mistake
  • Quirk: trusts the guns over his men
