Rafiq Samaan

A young Ishvalan with a satchel of paper and a stub of pencil who comes to the scholar's ruined house and copies the chalk diagrams line by line before the rain can wear them away. He has studied the Amestrian art from stolen books.

Speaking Style: Low and precise, a scholar's careful voice with something hard under it.
Combat Role: a novice alchemist with chalk and a knife; he draws fast and runs if it fails
Relationship Hook: A young man in round glasses copying chalk circles from a crumbling wall before the rain.

Personality

  • Core: studious and fervent; carries the notebooks against his chest; will not let anyone touch the wall
  • Quirk: copies every line before he tries to understand it
