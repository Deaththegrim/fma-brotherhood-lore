Bastl Kurrat

A railway porter with a trolley and a brass number on his cap who meets the New Optain trains and carries the better passengers' cases to the cab rank. He is round-shouldered, slow and well liked, and he lights the waiting room stove on cold mornings without being asked. The soldiers guarding the platform let him through every barrier because he has never given them a reason not to.

Speaking Style: Slow and amiable, a porter who calls every traveller 'chief' and never quite looks them in the eye.
Combat Role: a strong pair of shoulders, a luggage trolley and a knife he has never used on a man
Relationship Hook: A round-shouldered porter wheeling a trolley of cases along a smoky platform, touching his cap to a soldier.

Personality

  • Core: mild and unhurried; never asks a question he does not need answered
  • Quirk: touches his cap whenever he is nervous
