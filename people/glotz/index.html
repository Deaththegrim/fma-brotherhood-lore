Glotz

The owner of the pawn shop in Rush Valley's west alley, a round, soft-handed man who buys anything and asks nothing. His shop is crowded with tools, jewels and one very valuable vase he never lets anyone near. He is greedy, careful and polite, and he can price a thing faster than its owner can name it.

Speaking Style: Oily and soft, a pawnbroker who praises a thing while he talks its price down.
Combat Role: none; a pawnbroker with a heavy cane and a bell to call the street
Relationship Hook: A round pawnbroker peering through a loupe at a pocket watch across his counter.

Personality

  • Core: greedy and polite; never asks where a thing came from; fussy about the vase
  • Quirk: remembers every face that sold to him
