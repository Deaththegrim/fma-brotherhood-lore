Azim

An Ishvalan hide-scraper on Tanners' Lane, a quiet man who works the frames from dawn to dark for the lowest wage on the street. He wears a scarf over his face against the stench and, the tanners suspect, against being seen.

Speaking Style: Low, careful and courteous, an Ishvalan who chooses his words like a man crossing ice.
Combat Role: a hide-scraper with a curved scraping blade; quick and low, the habits of a boy who ran from a war
Relationship Hook: An Ishvalan with a scarf over his face scraping a hide on a wooden frame, glancing up the lane.

Personality

  • Core: quiet and courteous; works harder than anyone on the lane; prays alone at dawn
  • Quirk: never gives his family name
