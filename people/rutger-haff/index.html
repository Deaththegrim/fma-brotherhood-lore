Rutger Haff

The shed foreman of the junction, a railway sergeant who has kept the south's engines running since before the army took over the line. He is short, square and black with coal to the eyebrows. He can name an engine by its whistle and a fault by its knock, and he treats his locomotives better than his men, who do not mind.

Speaking Style: Loud over machinery and never quite quiet after, a railwayman who shouts out of habit.
Combat Role: a coal shovel and a short temper; he fights like a man used to heavy iron
Relationship Hook: A soot-blackened shed foreman with a shovel in front of a steaming locomotive in a roundhouse.

Personality

  • Core: shouts out of habit; loves the engines like horses; covers for his fitters
  • Quirk: logs everything except what he is told not to
