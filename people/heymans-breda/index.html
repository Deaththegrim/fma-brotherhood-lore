Heymans Breda

Second Lieutenant Heymans Breda of Mustang's unit, a stocky, red-haired officer with a shaggy crew cut and the sharpest strategic mind in the room. He knows the foreign powers' politics, plays chess like a general and gathers information the way other men gather debts. He is terrified of dogs, which the unit's own dog finds very interesting.

Speaking Style: Plain, quick and clever, the voice of a man who has already worked out the answer and is waiting for you; it climbs an octave when a dog appears.
Combat Role: a pistol and a plan; he wins the fight before it starts
Relationship Hook: A stocky red-haired lieutenant moving a chess piece and then leaping onto a chair to escape a puppy.

Personality

  • Core: clever and fast with a plan; plays chess to win; eats like two men
  • Quirk: terrified of dogs, even small ones
