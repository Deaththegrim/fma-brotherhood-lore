Coralie Ashdown

A singer at the music hall, dark-haired and thirty, who has sung comic songs and sentimental ballads on the music hall stage for ten years. The gallery throws flowers when she sings and the boxes pretend not to listen. Lately she has started singing a song of her own about the desert war, and veterans walk out halfway through with their faces turned away.

Speaking Style: Low and smoky, a singer's speaking voice that sounds like the last verse of a sad song.
Combat Role: none; a hatpin and a voice that can silence a gallery
Relationship Hook: A dark-haired singer in velvet standing in the footlights, one gloved hand raised, mid-song.

Personality

  • Core: warm, tired and stubborn; keeps her husband's letters in her dressing room; laughs too loudly at the comedian's jokes
  • Quirk: sings when she is angry
