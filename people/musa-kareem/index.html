Musa Kareem

A qanat digger, the last of his trade in Ishval, who goes down the shafts with a lamp, a short spade and a rope held by a priest at the top. He knows the tunnel from the hills to Daliha as other men know their own street.

Speaking Style: Earthy and good-humoured, a digger who talks with his hands and laughs into the dark.
Combat Role: a digger with a short spade in a tunnel where nobody else can swing
Relationship Hook: A powerful digger climbing out of a collapsed shaft, lamp in his teeth.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful and fearless underground; trusts the man on his rope with his life; will not leave the work for long
  • Quirk: sings to judge the tunnel's echo
