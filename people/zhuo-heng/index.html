Zhuo Heng

Foreman of the flashpowder works outside the wall, a careful man missing two fingers of his left hand and very proud of the eight that remain. He mixes the Yao clan's smoke, flash and pepper charges himself and lets nobody else touch the strongest batches.

Speaking Style: Low and even, never above a murmur in the sheds, where a raised voice is a careless voice.
Combat Role: a powder man; a pouch of pepper charges and the nerve to throw them close, then a long knife for whoever is still standing
Relationship Hook: A foreman with two fingers missing stirring a basin of powder with a wooden spoon, his mask pulled down to speak.

Personality

  • Core: counts his fingers when he is nervous; speaks in the sheds as if in a temple; trusts powder more than people
  • Quirk: loyal to the Yao clan without loving it
