Veit Lutt

A clay digger for the brickworks, a cheerful young father with clay to his knees all summer and mud to his waist all winter. He sings filthy songs while he digs and stops whenever a woman walks past.

Speaking Style: Easy and chatty, a clay digger who can talk the whole length of a barrow run.
Combat Role: a clay spade and a strong back
Relationship Hook: A clay-splattered digger leaning on his spade, glancing nervously at a sacking bundle under a plank ramp.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful and loose-tongued; keeps one secret badly
  • Quirk: sings while digging
