Hobb Carraway

The town's sewerman, a small, stooped man in waders who has cleared the old brick drains under the bad side for twenty years and knows each culvert by its echo. Respectable people cross the street to avoid his smell; the men from the bar on the corner buy him drinks, and he does not ask himself why.

Speaking Style: Mumbling and grateful, a sewerman who apologises for his smell before anyone mentions it.
Combat Role: none; he knows the drains, so he runs, and anyone who follows him into the dark is lost
Relationship Hook: A stooped sewerman in waders holding up a lantern where four drains meet, humming to himself.

Personality

  • Core: finds his way by echo with his eyes shut; hums in the tunnels to keep the rats moving; never looks up at a grating
  • Quirk: grateful for any kindness, loyal past sense
