Hilde Norrel

A furrier in North City's Fur Row who sells pelts and coats to the soldiers and the townsfolk. She is brisk, kind and direct, and she has made coats for half the garrison. She is always in a hurry, and she always has time for a child who needs a scarf.

Speaking Style: Brisk and kind, a furrier who speaks to her customers as if they were late for a train.
Combat Role: none; a pair of shears and a quick hand
Relationship Hook: A furrier at a counter fitting a fur coat on a soldier, a child waiting with a scarf.

Personality

  • Core: brisk, kind and direct; always in a hurry; makes time for children
  • Quirk: made coats for half the garrison
