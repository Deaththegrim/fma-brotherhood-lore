Trudi Marrow

The senior of Garfiel's three apprentices, seventeen and certain she will have her own sign over a door within five years. She came down from a farm near South Hood with a bag of her father's broken clocks and talked her way onto a bench by mending one in the window while Garfiel watched. She files finger joints all day, eats lunch standing and keeps a pencil behind each ear in case one snaps.

Speaking Style: Quick and chattering, an apprentice who names each tool as she reaches for it and laughs at her own burns.
Combat Role: none worth the name; a bench file swung like a club and a loud shout for the master
Relationship Hook: An apprentice in an oily apron filing a steel knuckle and chattering to it under her breath.

Personality

  • Core: jealous of talent and too honest to hide it; eats the sweets meant for the tea corner when nobody looks; offers to fix things that are not broken
  • Quirk: talks to the joint she is filing, never to the customer
