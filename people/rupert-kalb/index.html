Rupert Kalb

The editor of the paper printed at the Southern Gazette Office, a rumpled man with ink on his cuffs who has run the paper through two wars and four censors. He prints what he is allowed and keeps the rest. His reporters think he has gone soft; his rivals think he is waiting.

Speaking Style: Gravelly and tired, a newspaperman's voice worn down by cigarettes and arguments with censors.
Combat Role: none; a heavy paperweight and the threat of a headline
Relationship Hook: A rumpled editor at a desk of proofs, reading a soldier's notes through cigarette smoke.

Personality

  • Core: rumpled and sharp; smokes when he should be sleeping; loyal to his reporters
  • Quirk: keeps what he cannot print
