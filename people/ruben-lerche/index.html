Ruben Lerche

The foreman of the timber camp is a huge, cheerful axeman who runs his crews on hard work, good food and no questions. He sells larch to the army for trench props, sleepers and coffins, and he jokes that the coffins pay best.

Speaking Style: Hearty and loud, a timber boss whose laugh carries down the whole chute road.
Combat Role: a felling axe swung with a woodsman's ease; he could split a man as easily as a log and would rather not
Relationship Hook: A giant bearded woodsman with an axe on his shoulder laughing beside a sawpit full of sawdust.

Personality

  • Core: loud, cheerful and generous; never asks a man where he came from; jokes about death to keep it away
  • Quirk: protects his crews fiercely
