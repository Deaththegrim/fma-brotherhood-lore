Leopold Harn

A retired army surgeon who runs the dispensary with the red cross on its door: bones, boils, burns and lungs. He served in field hospitals in the west and in the east, and he chose Youswell because he wanted somewhere no one would salute him. Miners trust him because he never lies about a cough.

Speaking Style: Dry and weary, an old surgeon who makes jokes at the worst moments, gently.
Combat Role: a field surgeon's steadiness and a scalpel he would rather not use
Relationship Hook: An old surgeon listening to a miner's chest with a stethoscope and saying nothing for a long time.

Personality

  • Core: never lies about a diagnosis; refuses to be saluted; drinks one glass a night, exactly
  • Quirk: dry humour at bad moments
