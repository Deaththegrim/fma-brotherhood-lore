Barnabas Hufner

The ostler of the pit ponies, a small bow-legged man who knows each pony's name, temper and favourite apple. He sleeps in the loft when one is ill and has cried over more ponies than men.

Speaking Style: Soft and low, an ostler who speaks to people the way he speaks to frightened ponies.
Combat Role: none; a pitchfork he would never use on a man
Relationship Hook: A small ostler standing between a grey pony and a man with a pistol.

Personality

  • Core: gentle and stubborn; trusts animals over men
  • Quirk: hides what he loves
