Lambert Grell

The sexton of South City Cathedral, a bent man of sixty who has rung, swept and locked the great church for thirty years. He lights the candles before dawn, keeps the burial register in a fair round hand and knows which pew every old family sits in. Visitors take him for a sleepy caretaker. Soldiers' widows know better: he is the one who tells them when the next coffins are due in from the station.

Speaking Style: Low and unhurried, a churchman's murmur that drops further whenever a coffin is in the nave.
Combat Role: none; a heavy ring of church keys and the dark of a building he knows blind
Relationship Hook: An old sexton lighting candles beside a row of trestles in a dim stone nave.

Personality

  • Core: unhurried in everything, even grief; kinder to the mourners than to the clergy; distrusts anyone in a clean uniform
  • Quirk: counts under his breath while he works
