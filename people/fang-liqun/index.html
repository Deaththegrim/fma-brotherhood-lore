Fang Liqun

A Xingese caravan mistress who brings tea and medicines west and has camped her string in the red canyon for the shade, against the advice of her guide. She is generous, quick and sure of her own luck.

Speaking Style: Cheerful and loud, a caravan mistress who laughs at danger until it arrives.
Combat Role: a caravan mistress with a whip and a knife who keeps her camels between herself and trouble
Relationship Hook: A laughing caravan mistress in a bright blue jacket among camels in the floor of a red canyon.

Personality

  • Core: bold and cheerful; trusts her luck; generous with tea
  • Quirk: dismisses local warnings
