Silas Brack

The head porter of the Railway Hotel West has stood in its lobby for twenty years and forgotten nothing he has seen there. Staff officers tip him to remember their preferences; their wives tip him more to forget their visitors.

Speaking Style: Smooth and low, a head porter's courtesy so perfect it sounds like a private joke.
Combat Role: none in the open; a sap in his sleeve and a quiet word with the hall boys if a guest grows rough
Relationship Hook: A tall silver-haired porter in maroon livery standing among potted palms, watching the door.

Personality

  • Core: impeccably polite to the point of menace; remembers every face and most secrets; never takes a side, only a tip
  • Quirk: fond of the hotel cat and no one else
