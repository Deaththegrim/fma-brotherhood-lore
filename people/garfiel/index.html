Garfiel

The owner of Atelier Garfiel in Rush Valley, a big, muscular automail engineer with effeminate, effusive manners and real skill. He takes on clients' service calls across the valley and is delighted by talent wherever he finds it.

Speaking Style: Effusive, sing-song and fussy, a mechanic who coos over a pretty limb and a pretty apprentice alike.
Combat Role: none; a mechanic's strength
Relationship Hook: A muscular mechanic clasping his hands in delight over a beautifully made automail arm.

Personality

  • Core: effusive and fussy; generous to an apprentice; serious about his craft
  • Quirk: delighted by talent
