Gunnar Hesse

The landlord of the Bear's Head Tavern, a huge bearded man who served fifteen years at Fort Briggs and lost two fingers to frostbite and none of his loyalty. His tavern is where the fort's men drink when they come down; city troops who wander in are served, slowly.

Speaking Style: A rumble from somewhere under the beard, unhurried and final, like a door closing in a snowstorm.
Combat Role: a frontier brawler with a cudgel under the bar; he hits once, very hard, and the fight is over
Relationship Hook: A huge bearded landlord with a missing finger leaning on the bar, watching a city corporal hold his beer too carefully.

Personality

  • Core: huge, slow and courteous; serves city troops last and fort men first; keeps a fort mug on the shelf for every friend who died
  • Quirk: breaks up fights by lifting both men off the floor
