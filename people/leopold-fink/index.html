Leopold Fink

The keeper of East City's clock tower, an old clockmaker who winds the great clock every morning and oils its gears every week. From the top of his tower he can see the whole city and the road east, and he notices what changes. He is precise, private and very proud of the clock.

Speaking Style: Precise and dry, a clockmaker who measures his words like seconds.
Combat Role: none; an old man with a heavy winding key
Relationship Hook: An old clockmaker winding a great clock high above a city square.

Personality

  • Core: precise and private; proud of the clock; watches the city from above
  • Quirk: keeps a log of everything
