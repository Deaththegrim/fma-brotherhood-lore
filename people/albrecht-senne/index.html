Albrecht Senne

An old physician retired out of East City to a cottage on the coach road, who holds surgery here three mornings a week, driving in by pony trap with a bag of instruments gone dull. He is gentle, shrewd and by noon usually a little drunk. He sends what he cannot manage up to Pinako Rockbell, who sends what she cannot manage on to the city, and the town has learned to live with it.

Speaking Style: Cultured and slurred at the edges, a city doctor whose sentences trail off where the drink starts.
Combat Role: none; a scalpel he would never raise and a pony trap to flee in
Relationship Hook: An old doctor in a rumpled frock coat pouring a dose into a medicine glass, then drinking it himself.

Personality

  • Core: gentle and shrewd; tells long stories that never reach the war; treats children for nothing and adults grudgingly
  • Quirk: drinks to steady his hands and they shake the worse for it
