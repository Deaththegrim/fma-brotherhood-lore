Ridel's Son

The infant son of Ridel and Satella LeCoulte, born early in a thunderstorm in the family's house on the outskirts of Rush Valley, with Winry Rockbell as midwife. He has light brown hair and fair skin, and his grandfather dotes on him.

Speaking Style: A newborn's cries, loud and bright, and nothing else.
Combat Role: none; a newborn
Relationship Hook: A newborn wrapped in a blanket, held up by a tearful grandfather.

Personality

  • Core: loud; the family's joy; wakes the house at night
  • Quirk: sleeps in his grandfather's arms
