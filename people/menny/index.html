Menny

A little girl of Dublith whose cat Chiko has just died giving birth, and who believes Izumi Curtis can fix anything. She is earnest and polite, and she carries her grief in both hands. When she is told that a life cannot be mended like a broken pot, she understands it better than many grown alchemists.

Speaking Style: Small and tearful, a girl who sniffs between words and tries very hard to be brave.
Combat Role: none; a small child
Relationship Hook: A little girl in the market holding a wrapped bundle and asking where the alchemist lives.

Personality

  • Core: earnest and polite; believes the grown-ups can fix things; tries not to cry in public
  • Quirk: loves animals
