Gaspard Lunn

A wiry old poacher from the hamlet on the far shore who rows across to Yock Island at dusk to work the rabbit runs by the toad pool, with snares of plaited grass so fine a keeper would never spot them. He sells the rabbits in the Dublith market as tame stock from his own hutches, and the market pretends to believe him.

Speaking Style: Cackling and sly, an old poacher who laughs at his own lies before anyone can catch them.
Combat Role: a poacher's ambush; a weighted cosh from the long grass, and gone before his man gets up
Relationship Hook: A gap-toothed poacher crouched by a rabbit run at dusk, plaiting grass into a snare.

Personality

  • Core: never sets a snare the same way twice; generous with rabbits, mean with secrets; keeps well clear of the butcher's wife
  • Quirk: cackles at his own lies
