Ge Lianshou

One of the keepers of the emperors' tombs, a gaunt man who lives in the lodge at the end of the spirit road and sets out the offerings every new moon. He has a dry way of telling visitors exactly how the three emperors died, and he enjoys their faces.

Speaking Style: Low and deadpan, a keeper of the dead who finds the living faintly ridiculous.
Combat Role: a keeper's guard with a long-handled axe; he fights on the spirit road among the stone beasts, using them as cover
Relationship Hook: A gaunt keeper at a low lodge door, holding a cup in two shaking hands and smiling at the grass mounds.

Personality

  • Core: tells the emperors' deaths to every visitor with relish; holds his cup in both hands; fond of the stone beasts on the road and names them
  • Quirk: distrusts anyone who asks about elixirs
