Anselm Duvall

The priest of the brick chapel above the workshops, a lean, sunburnt man of forty-five who once got a whole street of smiths to stop hammering on rest days simply by asking, which the town still talks about. He keeps the pews, the bell and the young graveyard himself, and in the afternoons he walks the workshops blessing new limbs for any owner who wants it.

Speaking Style: Mild and unhurried, a priest who asks instead of telling and waits for the answer.
Combat Role: a medic's composure; he never strikes first, and disarms a man with a wrist lock learned from orderlies
Relationship Hook: A sunburnt priest in rolled sleeves splinting a smith's broken finger on the chapel step.

Personality

  • Core: asks rather than tells, and is usually obeyed; blesses machines as gravely as people; never speaks of the east
  • Quirk: flinches at the smell of burning
