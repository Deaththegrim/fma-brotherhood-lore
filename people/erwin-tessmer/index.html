Erwin Tessmer

The lieutenant's under-manager at the pits, a young engineer in a clean collar with a degree he mentions often. He drew the plans for the new galleries at Mine Number Eight and the budget for its timber, and he is proud of both.

Speaking Style: Smooth and quick, an under-manager who answers safety with savings.
Combat Role: none; a revolver in his coat he has never drawn
Relationship Hook: A young engineer with rolled plans telling a crowd of miners that the creaking is only settling.

Personality

  • Core: confident and glib; quotes his training; polite to the miners and contemptuous of them
  • Quirk: cannot admit an error
