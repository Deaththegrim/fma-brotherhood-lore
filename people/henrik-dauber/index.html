Henrik Dauber

The wheelwright's elder son, twenty-two, who works the tyring platform by day and by night locks himself in the back shed with a half-built motor carriage, a pile of catalogues and parts sent by post. He is clever, impatient and black with grease to the elbow, and he tells the tavern Resembool will have motor roads inside ten years.

Speaking Style: Fast and excited, a young mechanic who talks in horsepower and apologises for it.
Combat Role: a heavy wrench and a young man's reach; scrappy and fast
Relationship Hook: A grease-streaked young man pulling the dust sheet off a half-built motor carriage.

Personality

  • Core: draws engines on napkins; embarrassed by his own ambition; too proud to ask his father anything
  • Quirk: excited and impatient
