Black Hayate

A black and white shiba puppy who lives with Lieutenant Hawkeye and goes everywhere the unit goes. Fuery found him in the rain and nobody else would take him, so Hawkeye did, named him and housebroke him with warning shots. He is now the most obedient dog in the army and the unit's mascot.

Speaking Style: Animal noises only, never a word: a happy yap, a low growl at strangers, a whine when Riza leaves.
Combat Role: a small dog's bite and rush, aimed where an enemy cannot reach
Relationship Hook: A black and white puppy sitting perfectly straight beside a lieutenant's boots.

Personality

  • Core: obedient to Riza above all; growls at strangers; jumps on people he is told not to
  • Quirk: loves the whole unit except the man who fears him
