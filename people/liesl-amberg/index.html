Liesl Amberg

The senior operator who supervises the switchboard floor, a precise woman of forty with perfect posture and a headset she rarely takes off. She trained half the girls on the floor and can tell which of them is on which line by the sound of the plug going in.

Speaking Style: Clear and even, the trained voice of a switchboard supervisor; she says 'connecting you' as if it were a kindness.
Combat Role: none; she would sooner pull a plug on a line than lift a hand, and a hatpin is her only weapon
Relationship Hook: A composed woman in a headset sitting very straight before a switchboard of plugs and cords.

Personality

  • Core: composed in every crisis; corrects grammar without thinking; notices everything and remarks on nothing
  • Quirk: keeps her shorthand notebook in her stocking
