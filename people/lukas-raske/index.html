Lukas Raske

A gaunt, sunburnt man who lives somewhere in the reed marsh at the west end of the lake, on eels, stolen bread and whatever the herons leave, and is never seen unless he wants to be. The eel-catchers call him the reed man and leave a loaf at the marsh's edge in hard weather. He moves through the channels without a sound.

Speaking Style: Rusty and clipped, a man who has not spoken in months and falls into drill commands when frightened.
Combat Role: a trained infantryman gone feral; a bayonet lashed to a reed-cutter's pole, ambush from the water and no noise
Relationship Hook: A gaunt man rising out of black water between the reeds, a bayonet on a pole held level.

Personality

  • Core: flinches at thunder; repays every kindness in eels; would kill to stay free and hates that he would
  • Quirk: silent for days, then talks to himself in drill commands
