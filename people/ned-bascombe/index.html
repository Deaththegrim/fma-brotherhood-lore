Ned Bascombe

An orderly of the field hospital, a bricklayer's son with big hands and a gentle manner who has carried more men off the field than anyone in his company. He sorts the wounded in the triage tent and sleeps standing up.

Speaking Style: Steady and kind, an orderly who talks to the wounded in a low, unbroken stream.
Combat Role: none by choice; he shields the wounded with his body and drags them out of the line of fire
Relationship Hook: A big young orderly with a white armband kneeling beside a stretcher in a lamplit tent.

Personality

  • Core: talks to every wounded man as if they were friends; never runs, always walks quickly; cannot shoot a man and has made peace with it
  • Quirk: sleeps anywhere, any time
