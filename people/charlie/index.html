Charlie

Sergeant Charlie, a veteran of the Ishval war stationed in East City under Grumman, with lines shaved into his hair and streaks like lightning bolts. He served in Mustang's squad and still calls the column of fire that went before them a flame of trust. He is a family man and an expert with explosives.

Speaking Style: A sergeant's bark, rough and cheerful, that threatens a cornered enemy and comforts him in the same breath.
Combat Role: sidearms, explosives and guerrilla tactics
Relationship Hook: A sergeant with lightning-bolt streaks in his hair lighting a fuse under a bridge.

Personality

  • Core: rough and cheerful; loyal to Mustang; a family man
  • Quirk: an expert with explosives
