Hennig Stroh

A barley farmer of the western plain with blue shutters on his house, bread in the oven and a long list of grievances against the army, which buys his barley, took his horses and wants his son next. He is generous to strangers and impossible to bully.

Speaking Style: Dry and stubborn, a farmer's slow drawl that turns every complaint into a joke at the army's expense.
Combat Role: a farmer with a shotgun and a pitchfork; he fights from his own doorway and will not give a step
Relationship Hook: A weathered bearded farmer with a shotgun standing in the doorway of a blue-shuttered farmhouse.

Personality

  • Core: generous with bread and stingy with respect; complains about the army in every sentence; will not be bullied by anyone in a uniform
  • Quirk: softer about his daughter than he lets on
