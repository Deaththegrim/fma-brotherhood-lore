Rosalie Hamburgang

The only daughter of the wealthy Hamburgang family, doted on by her parents, the servants and the household's own alchemist, Jude. She is curious, restless and bored with good manners, and she leads any visitor she likes straight to the corners of the house she was told to keep them out of.

Speaking Style: Bright and nosy, a girl who whispers secrets loudly and giggles at her own daring.
Combat Role: none; a child who knows every hiding place in a big house
Relationship Hook: A girl in a frilled dress leading a visitor by the hand toward a locked wing.

Personality

  • Core: curious and bold; bored by lessons and manners; keeps the house's secret and enjoys it
  • Quirk: loyal to her new family
