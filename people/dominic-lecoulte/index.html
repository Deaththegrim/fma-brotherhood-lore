Dominic LeCoulte

A gruff, anti-social old automail engineer who lives in the mountains outside Rush Valley with his son Ridel and daughter-in-law Satella. He is little known and wants it that way, refuses every would-be apprentice, and builds limbs of a quality nobody in the valley can match. He dreams of building weapons into automail without changing a limb's shape or weight.

Speaking Style: Gruff and anti-social, a grumbling old craftsman who barks at visitors and melts at his grandson.
Combat Role: none; a master's tools
Relationship Hook: A scowling old mechanic slamming the door on a girl who wants to be his apprentice.

Personality

  • Core: gruff and anti-social; refuses apprentices; kind under the scowl
  • Quirk: terrified of Pinako Rockbell
