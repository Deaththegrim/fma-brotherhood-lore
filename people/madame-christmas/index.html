Madame Christmas

Chris Mustang, known as Madame Christmas, owner of a hostess bar in Central where Colonel Roy Mustang is a regular. She is a gruff, heavy-set woman with a mole and a cigarette always to hand, and she is the colonel's aunt: she took him in as a small boy when his parents died and raised him, she says, to be a good man.

Speaking Style: Gruff, low and smoky, sharp with everyone and sharpest with her nephew, whom she calls by his first name in front of anyone.
Combat Role: none in the open; her network and a cool head
Relationship Hook: A heavy-set madame behind a bar counter, smoke curling from her cigarette as two officers talk.

Personality

  • Core: gruff and blunt; misses nothing said at her counter; keeps her girls safe
  • Quirk: sharp with Roy and proud of him
