Ewald Weissbeck

An Amestrian in a wooden cage in the magistrate's gaol, the foreign smuggler the whole court is arguing over. He is sunburnt, unshaven and cheerful in the way of a man who has nothing left to lose, and he has taught the gaoler to play cards.

Speaking Style: Broad and rough, Amestrian vowels mangling the few Xingese words he has, then a laugh at himself.
Combat Role: an old infantryman; fists, a stool leg and every dirty trick of the trenches
Relationship Hook: A sunburnt Amestrian grinning through the bars of a wooden cage, a hand of cards fanned in his fingers.

Personality

  • Core: learns the Xingese for every card game; never names his buyer; homesick for bread and pretends otherwise
  • Quirk: cheerful in a cage
