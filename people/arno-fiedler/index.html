Arno Fiedler

An army engineer lieutenant who walks the survey line in the cool hours behind a theodolite, setting a white stake every hundred paces toward the eastern haze. He is sunburnt, cheerful and obsessed with the flattest ground.

Speaking Style: Precise and dry, a surveyor who speaks in distances and bearings and enjoys it.
Combat Role: an engineer with a service revolver and a surveyor's rod; he would rather find cover than fight
Relationship Hook: A sunburnt lieutenant peering through a theodolite down a line of white stakes into the haze.

Personality

  • Core: enthusiastic and exacting; measures everything, including his tea; sleeps under the cart at noon
  • Quirk: trusts his guide more than his orders
