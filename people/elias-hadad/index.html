Elias Hadad

The eldest novice left at the Seminary of the Sun, a tall young man of nineteen in a yellow robe that has been let out at the hems twice. He rises before dawn to lead the greeting of the sun for the six boys who stayed, and in the afternoons he sits alone in the library.

Speaking Style: Soft, earnest and well trained; a young preacher's voice that falters when he means what he says.
Combat Role: none; a novice who stands between the younger boys and whatever comes
Relationship Hook: A tall novice at an east-facing lectern, reading a sermon's margins with a stricken face.

Personality

  • Core: earnest and torn; leads the dawn greeting though he no longer believes it; protects the younger novices
  • Quirk: reads the margins again every night
