Wen Qiaoyun

A healer of the warm-spring valley, who lays the sick on mats by the pools and works the earth's flow through them with her hands in the water. She came to the valley to train for a season and has stayed nine years.

Speaking Style: Soothing and unhurried, a voice made for patients lying in warm water.
Combat Role: a healer; she fights only to reach a patient, with a staff and a calm that unsettles attackers
Relationship Hook: A healer kneeling at a steaming pool with her hands in the water and a patient lying on a mat beside her.

Personality

  • Core: keeps her hands in water when she talks; stays calm in every crisis but money; argues with the farmers politely and loses
  • Quirk: names each pool as if it were a patient
