Winry Rockbell

An automail engineer of fifteen who learned her craft in her grandmother's workshop in Resembool, and whose best work walks around on a childhood friend's arm and leg. She is bossy, warm and practical, tells the brothers off for every broken joint, and falls in love with any well-made machine she sees. She grew up with Ed and Al as family and waits for them to come home. A wrench is never far from her hand, and she will throw it at Ed's head.

Speaking Style: Bright, bossy and warm; she scolds the brothers like a sister, laughs at her own temper and goes breathless with delight over a new machine.
Combat Role: none worth the name; a thrown wrench and a mechanic's toolkit
Relationship Hook: A girl with a wrench at a workshop door, arms crossed, waiting for a boy who broke his arm again.

Personality

  • Core: bossy, fierce and practical; she lectures before she hugs; cries in private, and for other people; goes starry-eyed over good machinery
  • Quirk: carries a wrench and throws it
