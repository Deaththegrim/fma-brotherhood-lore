Ning Cuifen

One of the two maids of the Chang consort's wing, a farm girl of the clan who has polished the same floors every day for four years. The wing has so few callers now that she can tell from the feet in the colonnade whether someone is coming to her door or walking past it.

Speaking Style: Small and careful, a maid's murmur that stops dead at a footstep outside.
Combat Role: none; she knows the wing's second door and every cupboard a girl can hide in
Relationship Hook: A young maid kneeling on a polished floor with her cloth gone still, listening to footsteps in the colonnade.

Personality

  • Core: listens to footsteps the way others listen to music; devoted to the consort and too shy to show it; counts the day's callers on her fingers each evening, and lately needs none
  • Quirk: polishes when she is frightened
