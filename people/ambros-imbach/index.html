Ambros Imbach

A grey-bearded alchemist from Central who has slept in the scholars' hall for a month, copying the carvings into books by lamplight. He sat the state examination thirty years ago, failed it, and has been proving the examiners wrong ever since.

Speaking Style: Dry and courtly, an old academic who quotes himself and enjoys it.
Combat Role: an old alchemist who transmutes the floor under a man's feet and then apologises
Relationship Hook: An old scholar copying carvings into a book by lamplight in front of empty stone shelves.

Personality

  • Core: pompous and kind; quotes his own papers; lends his lamp to anyone who asks
  • Quirk: secretive about the back of the hall
