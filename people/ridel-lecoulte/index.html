Ridel LeCoulte

An automail engineer of Rush Valley, Dominic's son, who lives and works with his father in the hills on the outskirts of town. He is calm and friendly where his father is gruff, and his wife Satella is about to have their first child.

Speaking Style: Calm and warm, a craftsman who explains every bolt and panics only when his wife is in labour.
Combat Role: none; a fitting bench and a steady hand
Relationship Hook: An automail engineer at a fitting bench, glancing at his heavily pregnant wife.

Personality

  • Core: calm and friendly; explains every bolt; keeps the peace with his father
  • Quirk: devoted to his wife
