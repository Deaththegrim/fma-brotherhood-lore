Baldur Haskett

The farrier and smith, a huge soot-black man who shoes the carthorses, mends the ploughs and, when Rockbell Automail runs short of a fitting, hammers that out too, complaining all the while that it is clockmaker's work. He can hold a carthorse's hind hoof on his knee for an hour. Under the grumbling he admires Winry's work above anything made in the town.

Speaking Style: Rough and grumbling, a farrier who talks kindly to horses and to people as if they were horses.
Combat Role: a smith's heavy hammer and arms that can lift a cart's tail; slow, crushing blows
Relationship Hook: A huge soot-black smith grumbling as he hammers a small steel fitting on the anvil.

Personality

  • Core: gentle with horses; too proud to ask for help; works his anger out on the anvil
  • Quirk: grumbles at everything and does it anyway
