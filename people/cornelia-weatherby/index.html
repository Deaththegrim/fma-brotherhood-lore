Cornelia Weatherby

The secretary of the engineers' hall, a precise woman of forty who builds delicate hands for pianists and clerks and has kept the hall's minutes for twelve years in a shorthand only she can read. She runs the contest rules like a railway timetable and is the one person the loudest masters stop talking for.

Speaking Style: Clipped and exact, a secretary who reads out a rule as if it had always been true and dares anyone to argue.
Combat Role: none; the gavel, the rules and a room full of men who owe her a favour
Relationship Hook: A precise woman rapping a gavel once, and a room of shouting engineers falling silent.

Personality

  • Core: writes everything down and says little of it; prefers fine work to strong work; dislikes contests and runs them perfectly
  • Quirk: raps the table once, never twice
