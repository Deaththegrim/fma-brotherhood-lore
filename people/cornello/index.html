Cornello

Father Cornello, founder of the Church of Leto in the desert town of Reole, who calls himself the messenger of the sun god Leto and works miracles in the square: flowers from nothing, statues from stone, a promise to raise the dead. The town believes him and gives him its money, its faith and its young men. He is kindly in his sermons and wears a red stone set in a ring.

Speaking Style: Warm, paternal and certain, a preacher's voice full of promises that turns to a coward's shriek when the miracle fails.
Combat Role: alchemy through a counterfeit stone, and a crowd of believers between him and any enemy
Relationship Hook: A priest in white robes on a church balcony, arms spread, a red ring glinting as a statue rises from the stones.

Personality

  • Core: paternal and kindly in the pulpit; answers doubt with a staged miracle; a coward without his stone
  • Quirk: contemptuous of his flock in private
