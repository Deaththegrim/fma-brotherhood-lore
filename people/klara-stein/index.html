Klara Stein

A lieutenant of engineers sent out with a survey party to see whether the Fotset rail bridge can be rebuilt. She is twenty-five, brilliant, sunburnt and cheerful, with a notebook full of loads and stresses. She has never seen a battle and is too busy measuring to worry.

Speaking Style: Bright and quick, a young engineer's voice that turns everything into a problem to be solved.
Combat Role: a pistol she has fired only on the range; she relies on her escort
Relationship Hook: A young engineer leaning out over the twisted end of a broken rail bridge, measuring tape in hand.

Personality

  • Core: turns everything into a problem; fearless out of inexperience
  • Quirk: brilliant and cheerful
