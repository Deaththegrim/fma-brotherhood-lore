Reinmar Gaskell

Captain of railway transport for the eastern command, a hard, sunburnt officer who can put a full battalion and its horses on the rails before the men have finished complaining. He lives in the yard office more than in his quarters, with three telegraph keys, a stove and the loading tables of every regiment in the east chalked on the walls. He was a lieutenant on the trains to Ishval and has never talked about what he loaded on the trains coming back.

Speaking Style: Clipped, flat and exact, a transport officer who talks in times, numbers and wagon counts.
Combat Role: a field officer with a carbine and a sabre he knows how to use, who fights from behind a wagon wheel and makes every man around him fight the same way
Relationship Hook: A hard-faced captain in his shirtsleeves chalking wagon counts on a board while three telegraph keys chatter at once.

Personality

  • Core: exact to the minute and impatient with anyone who is not; respects engine drivers more than majors; smokes only when a train is late; dislikes the capital's officers on principle
  • Quirk: sleeps four hours and calls it plenty
