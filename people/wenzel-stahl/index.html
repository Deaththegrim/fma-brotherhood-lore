Wenzel Stahl

The night foreman at the tram depot, a long-boned man in a peaked cap who signs the cars in when the last ones come home and signs them out again before dawn. He has worked the trams since they ran behind horses, and he can name every car in the shed by the squeal of its brakes. Drivers say he is fair, slow to anger and impossible to hurry.

Speaking Style: Unhurried and low, a night man's voice that never rises above the hum of the shed.
Combat Role: none worth the name; a big quiet man with a switch iron and the dark of the shed on his side
Relationship Hook: A tall old foreman in a peaked cap, rubbing out a line in his night book as a dark tramcar rolls past the window.

Personality

  • Core: patient and dry; knows every car by its sound; keeps his book in pencil so he can rub it out
  • Quirk: feels responsible for anyone in his shed
