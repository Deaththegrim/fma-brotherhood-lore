Hal Wendt

The miller of the Rain River, a widower who grinds the district's wheat and knows every cart on the road and who drives it. He is broad, floury and in no hurry, and he will talk the afternoon away with anyone who leans on his door. Nothing moves along the river without him seeing it.

Speaking Style: Slow and friendly, a miller's voice dusted with flour; he likes a long chat at the door.
Combat Role: none much; a strong old man with a flour sack and a shovel
Relationship Hook: A floury miller leaning on his door and watching a cart cross the river.

Personality

  • Core: friendly and slow-spoken; watches the road from his door; works through the night
  • Quirk: gives flour on credit to widows
