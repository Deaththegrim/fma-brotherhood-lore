Wendel Fosse

The railhead clerk, a stooped old private past his service years who was kept on because nobody else could read his filing. He checks the weekly supply train off the ramp with a pencil behind his ear and a box of forms on a stool.

Speaking Style: Fussy and mild, an old clerk's precise little voice that apologises for the facts.
Combat Role: none; an old clerk who drops flat and waits
Relationship Hook: An old clerk on a loading ramp, a clipboard in his hand and a battered box at his feet.

Personality

  • Core: fussy and decent; files everything twice; talks to the empty ramp between trains
  • Quirk: keeps the second box under his own stool
