Joakim Lindahl

The private posted at the deepest valve of the boiler deck with a stool, a lamp, a doctor's stethoscope and an order to listen. He was picked because he was the quietest man in his platoon, and he has grown quieter since.

Speaking Style: Barely above a whisper, with long pauses, as if he were still listening for something under the words.
Combat Role: a poor shot and a stubborn one; he stays at his post with a carbine across his knees and fires at whatever comes up the pipe gallery
Relationship Hook: A thin soldier on a stool with a stethoscope pressed to a cold valve, eyes shut, listening.

Personality

  • Core: speaks only when the furnaces are banked; presses an ear to cold iron without thinking; keeps tally marks on scraps of lagging
  • Quirk: does not laugh at the stokers' ghost stories
