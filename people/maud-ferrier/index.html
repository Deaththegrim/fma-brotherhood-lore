Maud Ferrier

The goods clerk at Halden Junction is twenty, quick with a weighing beam and quicker with gossip, and she runs the goods shed while the porters smoke. She has a sweetheart at the border and writes to him every night on the backs of waybills.

Speaking Style: Chirpy and quick, a goods clerk who calls everyone 'love' and never stops counting.
Combat Role: none; a weighing beam's iron weight in a pinch
Relationship Hook: A curly-haired young clerk in a straw boater writing on a waybill beside a weighing beam.

Personality

  • Core: chatty, cheerful and sharp; counts sacks without looking; writes letters on anything with a clean side
  • Quirk: hides worry behind good humour
