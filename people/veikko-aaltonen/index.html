Veikko Aaltonen

The sergeant of the ski patrol, a lean, silent man who can cross the range faster than a horse and teaches his patrol to do the same. The map of routes on the hut wall is the best on the mountain, and nobody else has seen half of it.

Speaking Style: Quiet and spare; he never says a thing twice and seldom says it once.
Combat Role: a ski trooper; a rifle on his back, a knife at his belt, and a downhill run no pursuer can follow
Relationship Hook: A lean sergeant in a white smock waxing a ski by the stove, his eyes on the empty slot in the rack.

Personality

  • Core: waxes his skis while he talks; never says anything twice; goes out alone before he sends anyone else
  • Quirk: keeps his white smock on even indoors
