Kasimir Eichel

Dublith's doctor, a white-haired, quick-moving man who has stitched the town's hooked hands, butchers' slips and summer fevers for thirty years from three rooms behind a brass plate near the market. He charges the bad side double and the fishermen half and sees no need to explain either.

Speaking Style: Quick and precise, an old doctor who hums while he works and stops humming when it matters.
Combat Role: none; a scalpel and a doctor's calm, and half the town would come if he shouted
Relationship Hook: A brisk white-haired doctor threading a needle over a wounded man who will not say who he is.

Personality

  • Core: moves fast and talks faster; keeps a confidence like a locked box; charges by who you are
  • Quirk: hums opera while stitching
