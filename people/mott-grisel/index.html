Mott Grisel

A former private of Eastern Command who left the army after Ishval and now scrapes bat dung out of the back rooms of the lanes to sell at the crossing as fertiliser and powder stock. He smells of it and does not care. He calls the lanes his estate.

Speaking Style: Gravel and smoke; he swears softly and keeps his jokes short.
Combat Role: a drifter with a rifle butt and a short spade, who fights dirty and runs
Relationship Hook: A gaunt man with a cloth over his face filling a sack by lantern light in a dark back room.

Personality

  • Core: sour and harmless; works at night; talks to the bats
  • Quirk: will not speak about the war
