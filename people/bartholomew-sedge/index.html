Bartholomew Sedge

A retired railway clerk from South City who rows out to Yock Island every week in fine weather to sketch the birds of the crags, and has filled eleven notebooks with gulls, cormorants and one owl he is very proud of. He brings a field glass, a folding stool and enough sandwiches for a regiment, and the foxes know it.

Speaking Style: Fussy and delighted, a retired clerk who describes birds as if reading out a timetable.
Combat Role: none; a folding stool, a field glass and a quick retreat down the ridge path
Relationship Hook: A plump old man on a folding stool on a windy crag, sketching a cormorant and shouting at a fox.

Personality

  • Core: names every bird by its proper name and every person by their hat; shares food with anyone and anything; precise about dates
  • Quirk: too polite to interfere and ashamed of it
