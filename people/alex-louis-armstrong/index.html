Alex Louis Armstrong

Major Alex Louis Armstrong, the Strong-Arm Alchemist, more than two metres of muscle with a bald head, a single blond curl and a blond moustache. He tears his shirt off when moved, which is often, sparkles faintly, and tells anyone who will listen about the techniques passed down the Armstrong line for generations. He is a gentle, sentimental man and a devastating fighter, who punches transmutation through steel gauntlets.

Speaking Style: A huge, booming voice full of flourish and feeling; he bursts into tears and into family history with equal ease.
Combat Role: Armstrong family martial arts and alchemy through spiked steel gauntlets, punching stone into a storm of fists
Relationship Hook: A giant bald major with a blond moustache, weeping openly and flexing at the same time.

Personality

  • Core: loud, sentimental and generous; weeps openly at a sad story; never abandons a subordinate
  • Quirk: tears off his shirt when moved
