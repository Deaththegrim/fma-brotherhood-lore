Satella LeCoulte

Ridel's wife, a kind, loving woman who lives in the mountains outside Rush Valley with her husband and his father. When the journey opens she is nine months pregnant with their first child.

Speaking Style: Kind and calm, a woman who speaks softly and laughs at her father-in-law's grumbling.
Combat Role: none
Relationship Hook: A heavily pregnant woman letting two wide-eyed boys feel her baby kick.

Personality

  • Core: kind and calm; laughs at her father-in-law's grumbling; brave when it counts
  • Quirk: lets visitors feel the baby kick
