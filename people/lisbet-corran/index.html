Lisbet Corran

The postmistress's niece, seventeen, who minds the telephone nook and the switchboard while her aunt is at the counter. She means to be an operator on the exchange in East City, where girls sit in rows with headsets and a hundred lines, and she practises her telephone voice on the farmers who come in to ring the wool buyers.

Speaking Style: Quick and bright, a girl on the switchboard who says number please even off duty.
Combat Role: none; she would pull the plug on the line and run
Relationship Hook: A freckled girl with a telephone earpiece to her ear, frowning at what she has just heard.

Personality

  • Core: bright and eager; loyal to her aunt and growing suspicious of her; taps out the time on the switchboard
  • Quirk: practises a city accent under her breath
