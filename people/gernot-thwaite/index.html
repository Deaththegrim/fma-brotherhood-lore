Gernot Thwaite

Foreman of the goods yard behind the passenger platform, a broad, slow man in a railway waistcoat who loads the wool wagons, unloads the farm machinery and knows the weight of everything that passes his gate. He has worked the yard since before the bombing and helped dig out the platform after it.

Speaking Style: Steady and confiding, a railway foreman who talks low because the sentry is always in earshot.
Combat Role: a loader's iron bar and a strong back; he would rather swing a crate than a punch
Relationship Hook: A broad railway foreman tapping a crate stencilled plough parts, frowning.

Personality

  • Core: slow and steady; weighs everything with his eyes; loyal to his loaders before the railway
  • Quirk: keeps his voice low around uniforms
