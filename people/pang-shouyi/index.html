Pang Shouyi

The counter-man of the dried-goods house is a pleasant fellow with an abacus and a habit of telling customers that the dates are out of season. Pang Shouyi decides who reaches the back room, and most people never learn there is one.

Speaking Style: Pleasant and apologetic, the shop voice of a man sorry the dates are not better.
Combat Role: none; a heavy abacus and the bolt on the back-room door
Relationship Hook: A counter-man sliding beads on an abacus and apologising that the dates are out of season.

Personality

  • Core: apologises for the thin stock with real feeling; never says the master's name aloud; feeds the guides as if they were his sons
  • Quirk: taps the abacus when he lies
