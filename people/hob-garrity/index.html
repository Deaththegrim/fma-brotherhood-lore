Hob Garrity

The owner of the soldiers' tavern lost his left arm at the border wall in the war's first winter and opened the bar with his discharge money. His automail hand opens a bottle by the neck, ends a fight by the collar, and taps the bar in time when the old songs start.

Speaking Style: Rough and slow, a barman's growl that softens only for soldiers just back from the line.
Combat Role: a bar brawler with an automail fist; he takes the first punch, catches the second and ends it with the steel hand
Relationship Hook: A burly ginger-bearded barman opening a bottle with a steel automail hand, sandbags for seats behind him.

Personality

  • Core: slow to anger and fast to settle it; never asks a soldier what he did out there; keeps a shelf of bottles for men who did not come back
  • Quirk: taps the bar with his steel fingers when he is thinking
