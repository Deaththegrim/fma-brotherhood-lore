Ba Tuoshan

Master of the first relay on the western road, a bow-legged old courier who has ridden every stage to the desert edge and now saddles fresh horses for younger riders. The dispatch bell rings, and he has the next horse at the gate before the rider is in sight.

Speaking Style: A courier master's bark, short and hoarse from shouting riders out of the gate.
Combat Role: an old courier with a horseman's bow and a sabre; he shoots from the saddle and rides through
Relationship Hook: An old courier master turning a sealed dispatch bag over in his hands, a riderless horse steaming at the door.

Personality

  • Core: has a fresh horse ready before the bell stops; bow-legged and proud of it; takes his cut and does not think about it
  • Quirk: grieves for a lost rider like a father
