Leberecht Dunkel

The gatekeeper of the military cemetery, a one-legged veteran on a wooden peg who lives in the lodge by the gate, keeps the burial book on its lectern and rakes the gravel walk every morning. He lost his leg on the eastern front and was given the lodge instead of a pension. He talks to the dead more easily than to the living.

Speaking Style: Short and slow, an old gatekeeper who answers in as few words as the question allows.
Combat Role: none; a crutch, a peg leg and a lodge door that bolts
Relationship Hook: A one-legged old gatekeeper leaning on a rake at the end of a long row of white headstones.

Personality

  • Core: silent and patient; keeps the gravel perfect
  • Quirk: salutes the Ishval rows every dawn
