Qiao Dingxiang

A shipping agent under one of the awnings on the row, a neat woman in her thirties who keeps her passage book in a locked box and her opinions to herself. Her junks carry tea and silk down the coast, and now and then a family.

Speaking Style: Crisp and courteous, the cool voice of someone selling something that does not officially exist.
Combat Role: none; a sampan at the foot of the steps and the tide tables in her head
Relationship Hook: A shipping agent under a canvas awning, a locked book under her arm and her eyes on the harbour tower.

Personality

  • Core: polite to everyone and familiar with nobody; keeps the passage book locked even when she is writing in it; reads the letter from the far shore when business is slow
  • Quirk: never takes a booking from a family with small children, and has broken that rule twice
