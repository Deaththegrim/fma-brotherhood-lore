Hartwig Lomond

Owner of the carding house on the leat, a lean, dry man who runs three secondhand carding engines off one waterwheel with four hands to help. He cards the coarse fleeces the army will not take into yarn for the town's knitters and makes a thin, honest living from it. He oils the engines himself and knows each one by its noise.

Speaking Style: Dry and level, a mill owner who answers every worry with arithmetic.
Combat Role: none; a long oil can and a spanner the length of his forearm
Relationship Hook: A lean mill owner with his ear pressed to a shaking carding engine, an oil can in his hand.

Personality

  • Core: dry and careful; pays on time to the hour; cannot throw anything away
  • Quirk: listens to machines more than to people
