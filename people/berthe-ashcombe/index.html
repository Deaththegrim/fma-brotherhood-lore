Berthe Ashcombe

Landlady of the town's one inn, a broad, handsome woman of fifty who serves the shearers in season, the drovers out of it and anyone off the evening train who looks able to pay. She keeps a clean house and an honest measure, and looks hard at every stranger's footwear. Good boots get the front room. Poor boots pay first.

Speaking Style: Brisk and appraising, a landlady who prices a man by his boots before he opens his mouth.
Combat Role: a short oak club from behind the bar and a landlady's elbows
Relationship Hook: A broad landlady leaning over the bar to look at a stranger's boots.

Personality

  • Core: fond of rough men and short with smooth ones; keeps a stout club behind the bar and has used it; softer than she looks with the wounded
  • Quirk: judges by boots
