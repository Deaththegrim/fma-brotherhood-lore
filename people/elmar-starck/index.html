Elmar Starck

The patient in the end bed of the fort's ward, a wiry man with a new automail left leg and a cheerful way of not answering questions. The orderlies call him the lodger, because nobody can remember him being admitted.

Speaking Style: Easy and joking, always one quip ahead of the question he does not want to answer.
Combat Role: an old Briggs rifleman on a new leg; a crutch swung low and a kick from a steel foot nobody expects
Relationship Hook: A grinning man on the edge of an iron bed, flexing a steel knee and pretending not to watch the door.

Personality

  • Core: walks the ward at night to test the new knee; tucks in the other patients' blankets; goes silent when an officer from the south comes in
  • Quirk: jokes before anyone can ask him something real
