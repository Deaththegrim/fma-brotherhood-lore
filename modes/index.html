Game modes

How a story in Amestris leans.

Alchemist of the People

POWER · Easy
The party are prodigies of their arts, strong enough to change a town's fate, with the state taking notice.
Expect · big transmutations, quick growth, heroic wins
Lighter on · slow training

Apple Pie in Resembool

GENTLE · Very easy
A slower, kinder road: green hills, workshops, good food and small troubles, with the war far away.
Expect · warm scenes, kind neighbours, small stakes
Lighter on · battles and loss

Briggs Garrison

ROLE · Hard
The party hold the northern wall at Fort Briggs, where the strong survive and the weak are buried in the snow.
Expect · cold, discipline, a border war
Lighter on · comfort

Broken Gate

RUN · Very hard
A human transmutation failed in this world before the start, and someone else paid the Gate's toll.
Expect · the Gate's echo, a stranger's sin, forbidden knowledge
Lighter on · safety

Brothers Apart

RUN · Hard
The Elric brothers part at Reole and take separate roads, and the party travel with one of them.
Expect · split stories, a brother missing, a reunion that may not come
Lighter on · the canon's order

Chaos

CHAOS · Very hard
One failed transmutation tears the Gate open, and every player character is pulled through at once and comes back changed.
Expect · the Gate for everyone, a frightened country, a hunt for all of you
Lighter on · the canon's order

Chimera Outlaws

ROLE · Hard
The party are chimeras and their friends, made by the state's experiments and living outside its law.
Expect · outlaw life, loyalty, a hunted body
Lighter on · respectability

Dog of the Military

ROLE · Hard
The party serve the state as its alchemists and soldiers, sent wherever Central Command points.
Expect · orders, duty, moral pressure
Lighter on · freedom

Envoys from Xing

ROLE · Medium
The party are envoys of a Xingese clan, sent across the desert to find the secret of immortality for their emperor.
Expect · a foreign land, clan rivalry, a deadline
Lighter on · settling down

Multiplayer

TABLE · Medium
A party of alchemists, soldiers and travellers from different corners of the country, on one road.
Expect · shared scenes, a spotlight for everyone, mixed callings
Lighter on · solo arcs

Rush Valley Workshop

ROLE · Medium
The party run or work in an automail workshop in the automail country, where every customer has a story.
Expect · craft, customers, trade rivalries
Lighter on · wars

Scar's Reckoning

RUN · Hard
The scarred man's hunt for State Alchemists begins at the end of the war, years early, and the bounties begin with it.
Expect · a hunter in the dark, fear in the state, the war's debt
Lighter on · a peaceful middle

Steel and Nerve

SPECIALIST · Medium
An automail engineer or a fighter with steel limbs, in a country where the best weapon is the one you wear.
Expect · craft, combat automail, the cost of steel
Lighter on · magic

The Canon Road

RUN · Medium
The brothers' story as the series tells it, with the party living alongside it.
Expect · the canon's great events, the cast at their canon points
Lighter on · what-ifs

The Colonel's Officers

ROLE · Medium
The party serve under an ambitious officer who means to reach the top and change the country from there.
Expect · loyalty, intrigue, ambition
Lighter on · open war

The Dragon's Pulse

SPECIALIST · Medium
An alkahestrist from Xing, reading the life-flow of the land in a country that does not know it exists.
Expect · healing, sensing, a foreign art
Lighter on · brute force

The Ishvalan Road

ROLE · Hard
The party are Ishvalans and their friends, scattered by the war and hunted under the state's order.
Expect · survival, faith, hidden kindness
Lighter on · safety

The Other Elric

ELRIC RUN · Hard
The players are two siblings from Resembool in the Elric brothers' place, with the brothers' sin, their bodies and their road.
Expect · the Gate's toll, the Philosopher's Stone, the state's work
Lighter on · a quiet life

The State Exam

SPECIALIST · Hard
Alchemists climbing toward the state's certificate, through the written test, the evaluation and the practical, and what comes after.
Expect · study, rivalry, the price of the silver watch
Lighter on · freedom
