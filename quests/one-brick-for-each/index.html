One Brick for Each

The track ends in the stepped courts of Gunja, at a low wall beside a row of beans. An old woman sits by it with her lips moving. She says each brick is set for a name, and that she cannot keep the names any longer without something to set them in.

Goal: Learn who brings the bricks
