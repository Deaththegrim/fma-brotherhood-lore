Ishval and Reole

Ishval and Reole is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. The region of the Ishvalans, who follow Ishvala: razed in the civil war that began in 1901 and left empty under army guard, its survivors scattered across the country and its ruins still standing. Reole lies to the north-west, the town of the sun god Leto that the prophet Cornello ruled. Its people carry the war with them, and the soldiers who guard the road are not always welcome. Its hub is Ishval; its other places: Ishval War Ruins, Ishvalan Refugee Camp, Priests' Shrine, Reole, Reole Church, Cornello's Manor, Reole Market Hall, Ishval Border Gate, Salt Flats, Ishvalan Shrine Hill, Mountain Hideout, Ishvalan Graves, Ruined Cistern, Statue of Leto, Reole Station, Sunwheel Inn, Reole Hillside Cemetery, Reole Brick Yard, Reole Army Post, Seminary of the Sun, Reole Schoolhouse, Reole Pump House, Reole Date Groves, Reole Dispensary, Sun Street Forge, Reole Dye Vats, Reole Limestone Pits, Reole Council House, Reole Goat Market, Reole Burned Quarter, Kanda, Gunja, Daliha, Daliha Temple, Kanda Field Command, Ishval Army Encampment, Rockbell Field Clinic, Scar's Brother's Study, Cleric's Hermitage Caves, Wandering Priest's Camp, Alchemists' Railhead, Kanda Stockade, Ishval Wheat Terraces, Ishval Rail Survey Line, Ishval Military Cemetery, Ishval Occupation Barracks, Red Mesa Signal Post, Ishval Scrub Pastures, Ishvalan Red Canyon, Kanda Sniper Ridge, Ishval Quartermaster Depot, Ishval Dry Wash, Gate Road Trading Post, Refugee Weaving Sheds, Daliha Qanat. By land it joins East City, Eastern Desert.
