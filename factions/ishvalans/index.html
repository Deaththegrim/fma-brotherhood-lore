Ishvalans

The ethno-religious people of Ishval, the region in the south-east of Amestris razed in the civil war that began in 1901; most of the survivors live scattered in slums and camps across the country. Nearly all have brown skin, white hair and red eyes. They follow Ishvala, the god of creation, whose teaching forbids alchemy, since to remake what the god made is to defy him. A stranger meets them as a neighbour on a market road or a survivor at a grave; a State Military patrol meets them as a suspect. One who joins them learns the faith's words and rises by keeping its rites in a land that has not forgotten the war.
