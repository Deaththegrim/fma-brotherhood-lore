Xing imperial house

The ruling house of Xing, a monarchy across the Eastern Desert from Amestris. The Emperor rules fifty hereditary clans, and the clans compete for the throne through heirs and envoys. The house sends its princes and envoys abroad to find the means to succeed. A stranger meets the court as an envoy or a suppliant; a clan rival meets it as a blade in the dark. One who serves the house rises by serving a clan well.
