Rush Valley automail trade

The automail engineers of Rush Valley, the boomtown of the broken-down, who hold contests in the street and push their wares on anyone with a steel limb. They are rivals in every shop and allies against every outsider. A visitor meets them as hands grabbing at a sleeve to see the arm beneath; a customer meets them as the best limbs in the country. One who joins rises by making a limb the street talks about.
