State Military

The armed forces of Amestris and, in all but name, its government: the Führer is its Generalissimo, it rules from Central City and keeps a command headquarters in each of the four outer areas. Its soldiers wear blue, salute every superior on entry and keep to a strict protocol, and those who break it face detention or court-martial. The army holds the long border wars with Aerugo in the south and Creta in the west, guards the north against Drachma, and keeps order in the cities. A stranger meets it as a checkpoint, a recruiting poster or a patrol; a foreigner meets it as a prisoner. One who joins trains at the military academy, starts as a private and climbs by orders obeyed and wars won, and the highest ranks sit close to the Führer.
