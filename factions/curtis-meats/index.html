Curtis Meats

A meat shop in Dublith, kept by Izumi and Sig Curtis with one assistant, Mason. The shop is narrow and goes back further than it looks. It sells meat and ties to the neighbourhood, and its back room is where the Curtises keep their own business. A stranger meets the shop as a counter and a cleaver; a friend meets it as a warm welcome. One who works here learns the trade and rises by keeping the Curtises' trust.
