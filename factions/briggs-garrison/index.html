Briggs garrison

The soldiers who hold Fort Briggs on the border with Drachma, in the snows of the Briggs range, under Major General Olivier Mira Armstrong. It is the army's northern wall, a garrison with its own tanks and its own rules. Winters are long and fierce. A stranger meets the garrison as a guard who will not let them pass; a friend meets it as a hard but honest comrade. One who serves here learns to hold a position in bad weather and rises by never giving the snow a gap.
