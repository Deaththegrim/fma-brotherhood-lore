Eastern Command

The State Military's command for the east, seated in East City and counted the strongest offensive force in the nation. Its men bore most of the Ishval war and came home veterans, and they think of the east as theirs. Lieutenant General Grumman commands it, and the men hold him in high regard. A traveller meets the command as a checkpoint and a long wait; a recruit meets it as hard drill and good officers. One who serves rises by steady work.
