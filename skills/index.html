Skills

The skills of Full Metal Alchemist Brotherhood: what each covers and how hard things are.

agility

Running, climbing, leaping, rolling and keeping balance on a moving train. A basic success gets there; a great success gets there first, or where nobody thought a person could.

Covers: running, climbing, leaping, rolling and balance on a moving train or a roof. Not: enduring, which is stamina, and strength, which is might. Easy: climb a village wall. Medium: leap roof to roof in a poor district. Hard: swim a moat under fire. Very hard: climb a fortress's outer wall by hand. Extremely hard: cross a moving train's roof in a gale.

Primary Attribute: Body

alchemy

The art of changing matter by exchange: a material understood, broken down and reformed, its cost paid in matter of the same kind. A basic success changes what you have studied; a great success reshapes it whole, with no rebound.

Covers: comprehension, deconstruction and reconstruction of a material; the transmutation circle as the focus; Equivalent Exchange and the Rebound when an exchange is overreached; the art's rungs open the full reshaping and the rest. Not: a circle drawn in chalk, which is circle-craft, and a bullet's making, which is firearms. Easy: turn a loose iron filing into a bolt the size of a thumb. Medium: mend a cracked table leg from its own wood. Hard: reshape a broken chair into a stool with a clean seat. Very hard: turn a wall of a shop into a door for a fleeing witness. Extremely hard: rebuild a bridge rail that a Rebound has torn in half.

Primary Attribute: Craft

alkahestry

The Xingese art of the Dragon's Pulse: healing and purification drawn from the life that runs through the land, activated by kunai set at the points of a pentagram. A basic success heals or purifies; a great success opens a larger array than any Amestrian circle could hold.

Covers: the Dragon's Pulse drawn from the land: medical transmutation, purification, the pentagram in the open and the array that uses several points. Not: a plain circle, which is circle-craft, and the sense of a living thing, which is chi-sense. Easy: heal a grazed hand with kunai set at the pentagram. Medium: clean a poisoned well with a purification circle. Hard: stop a fever with a five-point array. Very hard: open a pentagram in the open air on a hillside. Extremely hard: draw a multi-point array across a village square.

Primary Attribute: Wit

automail-engineering

Building, fitting and tuning automail: the steel and carbon frames, the nerve joints, the calibration that makes a limb answer its wearer. A basic success fits a limb that works; a great success builds one a soldier would trust in a fight.

Covers: building, fitting and tuning automail: steel and carbon frames, nerve joints, the calibration to the wearer's body. Not: a machine's repair, which is engineering, and a forge's work on blades, which is smithing. Easy: adjust a loose elbow joint. Medium: fit a new leg to a child who grew. Hard: tune a limb so a hand closes right. Very hard: build a combat arm with a hidden blade. Extremely hard: rebuild a lost arm from a soldier's wrecked joint in one night.

Primary Attribute: Craft

awareness

Noticing: the sniper on the roof, the tail in the crowd, the loose stone, the thing out of place. A basic success spots what is obvious; a great success spots what was hidden on purpose.

Covers: noticing: the ambush in a pass, the tail in a crowd, the loose stone, the thing out of place. Not: a trail on the ground, which is tracking, and a clue in a corpse, which is forensics. Easy: spot a pickpocket in a market. Medium: see the ambush before the rocks fall. Hard: notice a man following you through three streets. Very hard: spot the sniper's glint on a rooftop. Extremely hard: see the feint before the blow.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

chi-sense

Feeling the Dragon's Pulse: the life in people and animals, the flow of the land, a Stone's presence nearby. A basic success senses living things close by; a great success reads what they intend.

Covers: feeling the Dragon's Pulse: living things in a place, the flow of the land, a Stone's presence nearby. Not: crossing into a trance, which is meditation, and knowing a story, which is lore. Easy: feel that a forest has people in it. Medium: know the flow of a river's life at night. Hard: feel a living animal behind a wall. Very hard: sense a Stone's presence in a room. Extremely hard: read the intent of an armed man before he moves.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

circle-craft

Drawing transmutation circles and arrays: the rune, the rim, the scale of the work. A basic success draws a circle that holds; a great success draws one that takes a whole room and keeps its shape under load.

Covers: drawing circles and arrays: the rune, the rim, the scale of the work, the circle that holds under load. Not: the chemistry of a material, which is alchemy, and an alchemical tattoo worn on the skin, which is a trait. Easy: draw a circle with chalk on a stone floor. Medium: draw a rim that holds a heavy cart. Hard: etch a circle into a steel plate. Very hard: draw an array across a barrack's yard. Extremely hard: draw a circle in dirt with a stick and keep its runes straight in the rain.

Primary Attribute: Wit

command

Covers: leading people in the open: soldiers, a crew, a militia, a band of workers. Not: courtly manoeuvring, which is intrigue, and a lie, which is deception. Easy: get a ferry crew rowing together. Medium: rally a village against raiders. Hard: hold a militia line through a charge. Very hard: lead a work gang through a collapse. Extremely hard: hold a line of men under a barrage.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

Easy: lie to an innkeeper about your name. Medium: pass as a refugee at a border ferry. Hard: hold a forged pass's story for a week. Very hard: fool a military inspector face to face. Extremely hard: talk your way past a guard in a uniform that is not yours.

Primary Attribute: Wit

Covers: explosives, fuses, grenades and the charge set where a wall will fall; the trench-clearing of the front. Not: a gun, which is firearms, and the drill of a siege engine, which is engineering. Easy: set a fuse and walk away. Medium: bring down a shed without hurting the house beside it. Hard: throw a grenade into a trench. Very hard: clear a trench line with three charges. Extremely hard: bring down a fortress wall at the right moment.

Primary Attribute: Craft

engineering

Covers: machines that change a war: trains, tanks, drills, the siege engines of the front; the rails and the fittings. Not: a small lock, which is tinkering, and automail's fitting, which is automail-engineering. Easy: keep a train's brakes working. Medium: repair a broken axle on a supply wagon. Hard: stop a tank with what is in a village. Very hard: bring a stalled supply train over a snow pass. Extremely hard: find the one weakness of a siege drill.

Primary Attribute: Wit

firearms

Covers: handguns, rifles and the field gun: loading, aim, the steady breath, the clean recoil; the Amestrian army's standard weapons. Not: a machine gun on its tripod, which is heavy-weapons, and a thrown knife, which is thrown weapons. Easy: hit a bottle at twenty paces. Medium: hit a man running across a yard. Hard: keep a rifle steady on a windy ridge. Very hard: hit a sniper's window from a moving train. Extremely hard: shoot the lock off a Briggs armoury door from the far side of the yard.

Primary Attribute: Craft

flame-alchemy

The combat specialism of the State Alchemists' Flame: air made dense around a target until it burns, water split into fuel and oxidiser through gloves. A basic success sets a target alight; a great success burns a street and keeps the fire where it was aimed.

Covers: the combat specialism of Flame: oxygen made dense around a target until it burns, water split into fuel and oxidiser through gloves. Not: a torch or a match, which anyone can light, and alkahestry's healing, which is alkahestry. Easy: set a straw dummy alight with a snap of gloves. Medium: burn a wooden door from its hinges. Hard: split a pail of water into fuel and oxidiser. Very hard: hold a ring of fire against three soldiers. Extremely hard: burn a barracks' gate and keep the fire from the stable next door.

Primary Attribute: Craft

Covers: reading a scene: wounds, burns, blood pattern, shell casings, and the thing someone left behind. Not: a trail on the ground, which is tracking, and a lie told in a room, which is deception. Extremely hard: tell that a scene was staged by someone who knew the trade.

Primary Attribute: Wit

Covers: machine guns, the crew-served gun, the saw-armed automail arm, the grenade launcher; a position held under fire. Not: a single rifle, which is firearms, and a charge laid in the ground, which is demolition. Easy: set up a machine gun in a trench. Medium: keep a gun firing with a jammed belt. Primary Attribute: Body
