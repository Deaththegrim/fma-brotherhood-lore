District: Xing

Xing is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. The Emperor's country beyond the desert: a capital of tiered roofs and red gates, fifty clans with their own halls, a river port that carries the tea and iron of the mountains, and an archive that keeps the empire's histories. Its court is careful about who is let in. Its hub is Xing Capital; its other places: Imperial Palace, Clan Halls, Yao Clan Estate, Xing Market, Eastern Harbour Road, River Port, Western Mountain Pass, Jade Temple, Imperial Archive, Mountain Shrine, Xing Barracks, Eastern Border Post, Tea Terraces, Chang Clan Compound, Court of Fifty Concubines, Heirs' Pavilions, Imperial Physicians' Pavilion, Silent Lantern House, Yao Retainers' Hall, Han Smugglers' House, Sand Rim Caravanserai, Clan Flashpowder Works, Sea Road Harbour, Yao Clan Rice Lands, Hall of Purification Arts, Shrine of the Western Sage, Tombs of the Mercury Emperors, Dragon's Pulse Peaks, Pulse Spring Valley, Kunai Forge Lane, Elixir Apothecaries' Street, Little Panda Bamboo Grove, Hall of Nine Cups Teahouse, Lantern Opera House, Inkstone Lane, Magistrate's Court of the Capital, House of Foreign Guests, Bridge of Clan Truces, Ever-Full Granaries, Imperial Horse Relay, Grain Canal Locks, Yellow Silt Ferry, Cormorant Fishers' Village, Willow Lake Pavilions, Mulberry Silk Sheds, Celadon Kiln Hill, Bamboo-Pipe Brine Wells, Upriver Iron Foundry, Northern Jade Diggings, Pine Smoke Ridge, Ginseng Slopes, Imperial Hunting Park, Cloud Step Hermitage, Red Banner Ravines, Northern Steppe Horse Runs. By land it joins Eastern Desert.
