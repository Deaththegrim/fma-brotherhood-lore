Rockbell Automail

An automail workshop in the Rockbell house at Resembool, run by Pinako Rockbell with her granddaughter Winry Rockbell. The house is also home to the Elric brothers while they are in town. The workshop builds and fits automail limbs and keeps them working. A stranger meets the house as a plain country home with a workshop out the back; a customer meets it as the one place that can fix a metal arm. One who works here learns the craft and rises by fitting limbs that hold.
