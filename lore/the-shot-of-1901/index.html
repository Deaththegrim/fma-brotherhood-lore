The shot of 1901

In 1901, in a tense and occupied Ishval, an Amestrian soldier shot and killed an Ishvalan child. The army called it an accident. The region did not believe it. Riots and protests followed within days, then attacks on garrisons, then open war. Every Ishvalan family can tell you where it was when the news came, and every veteran of the eastern army knows the date the fighting began. Who the soldier was, and what became of him, nobody in the street can say; the records released to the public say only that he was a member of the State Military.
