The Ishval war as the army teaches it

At the military academy, the Ishval war is taught as a lesson in pacification: a long insurgency, foreign arms, a rebel population that would not negotiate, and a decisive commitment of new force that ended it in a year. Cadets learn the name of the order and not much about what followed. The veterans who teach the course often teach it flatly, eyes on the page. Ask one about it after hours and he will either change the subject or tell you more than the syllabus ever would.
