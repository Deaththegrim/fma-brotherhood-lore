The Gold Steiner story

Mountaineers in the north tell a tale about a young woman who came seeking an apprenticeship from the famous alchemist Silver Steiner and found only his elder brother, Gold Steiner, an eighty-eight-year-old martial artist who was hard of hearing. Gold misunderstood her and handed her a machete with orders to live alone in the Briggs mountains for a month. She came back carrying a dead bear. Silver Steiner had already died; Gold Steiner took her on himself, and she beat him senseless in her first sparring bout. The northerners insist every word is true, and alchemists in the south say they know who she grew up to be.
