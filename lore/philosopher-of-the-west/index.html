Philosopher of the West

Xingese records say that four hundred years ago the art they call alkahestry did not exist. Their healers swallowed minerals and potions they believed would lengthen life, and three generations of the imperial family died of mercury taken as medicine. Then a stranger came across the desert from the west, from Xerxes they say, and taught real transmutation. Xingese physicians wove his teaching into their older healing arts, and alkahestry was born. Xing honours him as the Philosopher of the West and knows nothing more of him; whether he and Amestris's own wise stranger were one man is a favourite argument wherever alchemists of both countries meet.
