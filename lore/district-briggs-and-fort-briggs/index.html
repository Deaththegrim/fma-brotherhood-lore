District: Briggs and Fort Briggs

Briggs and Fort Briggs is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. The snow range on the Drachma border and the fort that holds it: Fort Briggs and its garrison, the refugee town of Asbec, the abandoned mine of Baschool, and the lands to the north where Drachma's army waits behind its own border. Winter here is measured in lives. Its hub is Fort Briggs; its other places: Briggs, Briggs Pass, Frostline Outpost, Asbec, Baschool, Drachma, Wind Gap Camp, Snow Field Inn, Ice Road Crossing, Briggs Lookout Hut, Old Lumber Camp, Briggs Main Tunnel, Briggs Boiler Deck, Briggs Engineering Bay, Briggs Wall Infirmary, Briggs Stockade, Briggs Gun Gallery, Briggs Stores Tunnels, Briggs North Rampart, Briggs Tank Proving Ground, Briggs Barrack Tiers, Briggs Customs Hall, Briggs Telegraph Room, Briggs Remount Stables, Briggs Wallfoot Snowfield, Drachman Siege Camp, Drachman Border Blockhouse, Grizzly Gully, Hermit's Bivouac, Whiteout Cirque, Eaglehead Crag, Wolfpack Hollow, Briggs Pinewood, Briggs Supply Railhead, Briggs Rail Snowshed, Briggs Ski Patrol Hut, Briggs Garrison Graveyard, Briggs Pass Telegraph Relay, Glasswater Tarn, Briggs Sled Kennels, Range Pelt Post, Briggs Charcoal Clearing, Snowmelt Sulphur Baths, Highcrag Goat Steading, Greywall Quarry, Asbec Halt, Asbec Soup Kitchen, Asbec Tinsmiths' Row, Asbec Back-Street Hideout, Baschool Mine Galleries, Baschool Company Store, Baschool Pithead. By land it joins North City.
