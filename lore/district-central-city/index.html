District: Central City

Central City is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. The capital of Amestris and the seat of its government: the legislature, the military's top command, the national library and laboratories, and the great rail terminal. Soldiers in state blue walk the avenues and every stranger's papers are read twice. The roads are the station's platforms and the plaza avenues, and the trains run out in every direction. Its hub is Central City; its other places: Central Command, Central Prison, Laboratory 5, National Central Library, Radio Capital, Central Hospital, Market Row, Parliament House, Eastern Tenements, Central Rail Yard, Central Gardens, Old Arsenal, Madame Christmas's Bar, Hughes Family Home, Central Military Cemetery, Military Investigations Office, Führer's Residence, Armstrong Mansion, Sheska's Book-Buried Flat, Linden Court Hotel, Barry's Old Butcher Shop, Cinder Alley, Dr. Knox's House, First Branch Ruins, State Certification Hall, Kiosk Corner, Vestry Bridge, Capital Tramway Depot, Westbank Gasworks, Kellerhof Brewery, Capital Gazette Press, Central Telephone Exchange, Valdemar Automail Clinic, Hartwig's Alchemical Supply, Brunner's Military Tailors, Wallenstein Officers' Club, Booksellers' Arcade, Linden Street Church, Capital Grammar School, Weber Street Free Clinic, Capital Military Police Precinct, Halden Coal Wharf, Grauwasser Reservoir, Capital Stockyards, Central Locomotive Works, Ziegler Brick Pits, Southfield Market Gardens, Haldorf Dairy, Grauwald, Army Rifle Range, Waggoner's Rest Inn, Grand Meridian Hotel, Capital Opera House, Brenner's Department Store. By land it joins Dublith and Yock Island, East City, North City, West City and the Creta border.
