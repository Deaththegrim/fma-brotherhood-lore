The expansion wars

Amestris grew by war. Since its founding the state has pushed against every border, annexing towns, then provinces, then whole peoples, and folding their land into its own areas. The Ishvalans of the east and the border towns taken from Creta in the west all came under Amestrian rule this way, and the war with Aerugo has run since 1835. The habit shaped the country: a stable economy and a strong government came to depend on the next campaign, and the army rose to the top of every ladder of prestige. Old soldiers say Amestris has never known a decade without a front somewhere. Young recruits are told the wars keep the nation safe; their grandmothers, in the annexed towns, remember it differently.
