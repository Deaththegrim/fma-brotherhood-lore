District: South City and the Aerugo border

South City and the Aerugo border is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. The southern military hub at South City, the supply road to the front, the ruined town of Fotset and the long line that faces Aerugo across the hills. The war in the south has run for years and most of it is fought in ruins. Its hub is South City; its other places: South Command Headquarters, South City Market, South Hospital, Fotset, Aerugo Front Line, Aerugo, Border Watchtower, Southern Road Inn, Dry Riverbed, Supply Road South, South Watch Post, Old Frontier Mill, Hill Shepherd Camp, South City Cathedral, Fountain Promenade, South City Barracks, Station Hotel, Limb Works of the South, South City Telephone Exchange, Southern Gazette Office, Southern Recruiting Hall, Southern Officers' Club, Southern War Cemetery, Old Border War Memorial, South Junction Engine Sheds, Hillside Convalescent Home, Aerugonian Prisoner Compound, Southern Ordnance Works, Cavalry Remount Depot, War Orphans' Home, Old Customs House, Pawnbroker of Front Street, Terrace Vineyards, Old Olive Grove, Limestone Quarry, Southern Copper Workings, Maren Hamlet, Holm Oak Wood, Windpump Station, Red Gullies, Railhead Camp, Forward Dressing Station, Gun Line Ridge, Reserve Trenches, Wire Fields, Fotset Chapel Ruin, Fotset Rail Bridge, Fotset Refugee Tents, Southern Pass Fort, The Ceded Strip, Sanvento, Aerugonian Farmlands, Red Coat Encampment, Aerugonian Customs Post, Smugglers' Saddle. By land it joins Dublith and Yock Island.
