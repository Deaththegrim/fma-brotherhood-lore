District: West City and the Creta border

West City and the Creta border is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. The western command at West City and the border with Creta: river port and hospital, the wall through the hills, the mountain passes and the small patrols that fight the border war. Little of the west is known in the capital, and the soldiers who serve there rarely come home to talk of it. Its hub is West City; its other places: West Command Headquarters, West City Market, Western Military Hospital, Western River Port, Creta, Creta Border Post, Border Wall, Western Hills, Mountain Pass West, Abandoned Mine West, Western Garrison Camp, Border Road Inn, Western Cemetery, Western Signal Station, Pendleton, Pendleton Front, Cretan Tribal Line, West City Rail Yard, Western Barracks, West City Telephone Exchange, Western Limb Works, Western Alchemy Registry, West City Garrison Chapel, Western Officer Cadet School, West City Courier Office, Railway Hotel West, The Sandbag Tavern, Western Quartermaster Depot, West City Gasworks, West Quay Tannery, Dockside Free Clinic, Hill Barley Brewery, West City Soldiers' Orphanage, Western Military Stockade, Iron Bridge West, Western Plain Farms, Western Home for Disabled Soldiers, Halden Junction, Larch Hollow, Greyface Quarry, Ridge Sheep Station, Larch Ridge Halt, Border Refugee Camp, Pendleton War Cemetery, Pendleton Field Hospital, Cold Seam Colliery, Hill Powder Magazine, Western Timber Camp, Eagle Crag Lookout, Thornback Gully, No Man's Strip, Cretan Hill Fort, Frontier Customs House, Northroad Coaching Inn, Snowmelt Tarn, Southwest Ranges.
