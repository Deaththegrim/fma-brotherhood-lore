District: North City

North City is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. The State Military's northern command, a city south of the Briggs range with its headquarters, its market and the road up to the fort. Its people are hardy and its officers are careful, and the rail line ties it to the capital. Its hub is North City; its other places: North Command Headquarters, North City Market, Briggs Road, Northern Rail Halt, Northern Inn, North Watchtower, Cemetery of the North, Northern Farms, Frozen Lake, Grain Mill Quarter, Central Road Crossing, Ice House, Back Lane Clinic, North Area Barracks, North City Officers' Billet, Requisition Hall, Fort Escort Yard, North City Telephone Exchange, North City Hall, North Herald Press, Northern Savings Bank, Kettle and Stove Café, Bear's Head Tavern, Lantern Music Hall, Oven Street, North City Bathhouse, Gate Ward Guardhouse, North City Stockade, North Area Military Hospital, Pinewood Orphanage, Lower Town School, Snowbell Chapel, Frostworks Automail, Lindqvist Alchemy Supply, Northern Quartermaster's Depot, Snowplough Sheds, North City Coal Yard, North City Gas Works, Northern Foundry, Tanners' Lane, Northbank Sawmill, Sledwright's Yard, North City Horse Fair, Fir Hill Brewery, North Line Trestle, Range Weather Station, Northern Drill Field, Stag Ridge Lodge, Kessel Ford, Blackpine Wood, Grey Wolf Hills, Slate Hill Quarry, Charburners' Clearing, Hollin Peat Moor. By land it joins Briggs and Fort Briggs, Central City.
