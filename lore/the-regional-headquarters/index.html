The regional headquarters

Outside the capital, government means the army. Each of the four outer areas has a military headquarters in its main city, East City, North City, South City and West City, and the commanding general there is governor in all but name, answerable only to Central. The headquarters compete fiercely: for budget, for promotions, for the Führer's attention. Officers posted to one carry its loyalties for life, and a transfer between them can feel like moving to a different army. Regional commanders have little real freedom from Central, and a great deal of politics among themselves.
