District: Resembool and the Rain River

Resembool and the Rain River is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. The sheep country of the south-east, a quiet farming town on the Rain River and the village between it and East City. Its wool goes east by train and its families know one another's business. The station was struck in 1907 and rebuilt, and the town keeps its own counsel about what it remembers.
