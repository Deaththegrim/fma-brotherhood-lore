District: Ishval and Reole

Ishval and Reole is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. The region of the Ishvalans, who follow Ishvala: razed in the civil war that began in 1901 and left empty under army guard, its survivors scattered across the country and its ruins still standing. Reole lies to the north-west, the town of the sun god Leto that the prophet Cornello ruled. Its people carry the war with them, and the soldiers who guard the road are not always welcome. By land it joins East City, Eastern Desert.
