Alkahestry

Alkahestry is Xing's own form of alchemy. It does much the same work, but its purpose is different: where Amestrian alchemy grew toward engineering and war, alkahestry grew out of medicine and the search for the elixir of life. Its practitioners heal better than any Amestrian, mending wounds and easing sickness by guiding the body's own energy. They can also do what Amestrian alchemists cannot, sending a transmutation through the ground to act far away. It is almost never seen outside Xing; an alkahestrist in Amestris is a curiosity, and a much-sought one.
