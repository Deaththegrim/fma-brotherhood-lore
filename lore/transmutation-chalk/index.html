Transmutation chalk

Every working alchemist carries chalk. A circle can be drawn in pencil, ink, paint or blood, scratched in dirt or sewn into cloth, but chalk is cheap, quick and shows up on almost anything, so it is what apprentices learn with and masters still keep in a coat pocket. Alchemists who fight carry their circles ready-made instead, etched on gauntlets, sewn into gloves or tattooed into skin, because nobody waits for you to finish drawing.
