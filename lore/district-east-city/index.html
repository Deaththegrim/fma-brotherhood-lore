District: East City

East City is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. The strongest offensive force in Amestris: a garrison of veterans from the Ishval years, barracks, trams and canals. Many call the city a backwater and its officers do not argue. The roads lead north to the Ishval country, south to Youswell and back west to the capital. Its hub is East City; its other places: Eastern Command Headquarters, Eastern Barracks, Canal Ward, Eastern Market, Clock Tower, Veterans' Hall, Eastern Military Hospital, Eastern Gate Road, Eastern Tram Depot, Old Garrison Yard, Old Quarry Road, Tucker House, Sunken Lane Crossing, East City Ishvalan Slum, Grumman's Office, Mustang Unit Office, New Optain Line Platform, Eastern Command Troop Sidings, Eastern Command Rifle Range, Eastern Command Stockade, Eastern Command Motor Pool, Eastern Remount Depot, East City Town Hall, East City Telephone Exchange, Eastern Courier Press, Marl Bank School, Eastern War Orphans' Home, Eastern Military Cemetery, Haldane Automail Fitters, Ostermann Uniform Outfitters, Voss Licensed Gunsmith, Brecht Pawnbroker, Wexler Alchemical Supply, Garrison Steam Baths, Grand Marl Hotel, Lindenhof Coffee House, Pavilion Music Hall, Garrison Park, Marl River Coal Wharf, Marl Iron Bridge, Marl Weir Pump House, Lower Marl Tannery, East City Gasworks, Ostfeld Brickworks, East City Worsted Mill, East City Stockyards, Eastgate Brewery, East City Engine Roundhouse, Marl Junction Signal Box, Marl Flats Market Gardens, Hallerhof Dairy, Lower Marl Reed Marsh, Ashgrove Wood. By land it joins Central City, Ishval and Reole, Resembool and the Rain River, Youswell and the coal frontier.
