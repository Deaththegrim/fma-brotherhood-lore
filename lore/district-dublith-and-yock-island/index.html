District: Dublith and Yock Island

Dublith and Yock Island is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. The southern lake town of Dublith on Kauroy Lake, its island of the survival test, and the automail boomtown of Rush Valley north of it. Fishermen, butchers and automail smiths live side by side, and the bars close early. Its hub is Dublith; its other places: Devil's Nest, Curtis Meats, Curtis House, Yock Island, Kauroy Lake, Rush Valley, LeCoulte Automail, Glotz Pawn Shop, Dublith Chapel, Lakeside Halt, Dublith Rail Yard, Kauroy Ferry Pier, Old Lake Fort, Island Cove, Atelier Garfiel, Rush Valley West Alley, Rush Valley Station, Rush Valley Contest Square, Rush Valley Parts Market, Rush Valley Tin Roofs, Rush Valley Nerve Clinic, Rush Valley Patients' Lodging, Rush Valley Foundry, Rush Valley Scrap Yard, Rush Valley Engineers' Hall, Valley Road Toll House, LeCoulte Cliff Road, Rush Valley Iron Diggings, South Hood, Rush Valley Chapel, Dublith Sewers, Kauroy Fish Hall, Dublith Abattoir, Dublith Tannery, Kauroy Lake Hotel, Dublith Schoolhouse, Dublith Town Clinic, Dublith Military Police Post, Dublith Low Wharf, Dublith Post and Telegraph Office, Kauroy Boatyard, Kauroy Ice Works, Kauroy Net Lofts, Dublith Cattle Pens, Kauroy Beacon, Heron Islet, Kauroy Reed Marsh, Kauroy South Shore Hamlet, Yock North Shore, Yock Island Ridge, Yock Toad Pool, Dublith Fig Orchards, Kauroy Dairy Farm, Dublith Brick Kilns, Kauroy Outflow Mill, Kauroy Bathing Beach. By land it joins Central City, South City and the Aerugo border.
