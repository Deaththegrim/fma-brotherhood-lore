District: Eastern Desert

Eastern Desert is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. The desert between Amestris and Xing: caravan yards, dune seas, glass flats, the ruins of a country older than any kingdom in the books of Amestris, and the wells that decide who crosses. Few go into it without a guide and fewer come back without one. Its hub is Desert Crossing; its other places: Xerxes, Dune Sea, Dry Well Waystation, Glass Flats, Salt Ridge, Desert Road Fort, Old Caravan Track, Sandstone Shrine, Bone Ridge, Desert Well Basin, Oasis Palms, Royal Palace of Xerxes, Temple of the Domes, Old Market of Xerxes, Xerxes Plantations, Xerxes Stepwell, Elder Shan's Camp, Bonath, Carved Stone of Xerxes, Hall of Scholars, Stucco Lanes, Dry Canal of Xerxes, Xerxes Signal Tower, Diggers' Camp, Crossing Camel Market, Eastern Customs House, Caravan Clinic, Xingese Trading House, Desert Telegraph Relay, Tamarisk Caravanserai, Eastbound Cistern, Far Bank Inn, Xingese Well Tower, Envoy's Rest, Camel Post Station, Storm Wall Shelter, Unfinished Rail Embankment, Desert Water Depot, Desert Patrol Cemetery, Military Survey Camp, Red Sandstone Quarry, Salt Pan Works, Copper Diggings, Black Tent Camp, Silica Kilns, Leto Mission House, Raiders' Hollow, Red Wadi, Flat-Top Mesa, Sulphur Springs, Dry Lake Bed, Scorpion Flats, Exiles' Track. By land it joins Ishval and Reole, Xing, Youswell and the coal frontier.
