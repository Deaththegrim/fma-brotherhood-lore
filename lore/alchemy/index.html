Alchemy

Alchemy is the science of changing matter by transmutation: taking what is in front of you, understanding it, breaking it down and building it back up as something else. A trained alchemist can mend a broken radio, raise a wall out of a road or turn a fence into a spear, given the right materials and the right circle. It is a science, not magic. It demands chemistry, physics and centuries of theory, and on top of all that an inborn knack for feeling how matter and energy move, which is why not everyone who studies it can do it. Amestris prides itself on its alchemists, and most towns of any size have at least one.
