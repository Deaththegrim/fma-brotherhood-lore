District: Youswell and the coal frontier

Youswell and the coal frontier is a district of Amestris and its neighbours. A coal-mining town on the desert's edge and the land around it: the mines, the coal flats and the spur that carries the coal west. For years a lieutenant owned the land and taxed it hard, and the people of the town work the shafts for what they can get.
