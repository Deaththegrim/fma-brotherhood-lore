Princes and princesses of Xing

Among the Emperor of Xing's forty-three heirs, a few names reach Amestris through merchants. Ling Yao, the twelfth prince and heir of the great Yao clan, is said to be clever, cheerful and dangerous, guarded by a grandfather and granddaughter of the clan's retainers, Fu and Lan Fan. May Chang, the seventeenth princess, of the small and powerless Chang clan, is a child prodigy in alkahestry. Both clans are said to be desperate for the Emperor's favour.
