Father Cornello

Father Cornello is the prophet of Leto in Reole, a stout, bald preacher in a priest's robe with a red stone set in a ring on his finger. Before the faithful he works wonders, and he promises those who serve Leto more miracles, even the return of their dead. Reole loves him. Alchemists who hear the stories notice that every miracle is something an alchemist could do with a circle, and wonder where his circle is.
